**core**

- [History-Conditioned Spatio-Temporal Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06480) — Wang et al. · arXiv
  Training-free spatio-temporal token pruning for VLA navigation: spatial selection on the current view plus temporal compression for historical memories. Directly occupies SURF's slot with temporal history as the prior signal rather than structural line features — raises whether SURF's prior needs explicit temporal conditioning to survive across task phases. New in digest; potential comparison baseline.

- [Beyond Attention or Similarity: Maximizing Conditional Diversity for Token Pruning in MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10967) — Zhang et al. · NeurIPS
  CDPruner: proposes that attention-based pruning retains duplicate tokens and similarity-based pruning ignores instruction relevance — both fail; joint optimization of instruction-conditional diversity outperforms both. SURF's structural prior provides a spatial relevance signal, but CDPruner's diversity criterion is orthogonal: a structural prior could improve the diversity pool before CDPruner selects from it, or compete if instruction-relevance scores already localize the structural-prior regions. Directly constrains [[experiments/contrastive alignment]] design.

- [DeCAL: Towards Physically-Grounded Dexterous Vision-Language-Action Models via Contact-Aware Latent Co-Imagination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09119) — Fu et al. · arXiv
  Unifies understanding, imagination, and control in a dexterous VLA via contact-aware latent co-imagination — imagines the contact-region future state and fuses it with the current observation to handle occlusions and complex contact dynamics. Seventh signal in SURF's comparison set with structural contact priors (alongside One Demonstration Many Objects, RoboPCA); specifically, the contact-aware latent is imagined forward, not extracted from structure — a fundamentally different mechanism. Sharpens the question: does SURF's structural line-drawing prior need to represent contact regions explicitly, or is contact-awareness recovered downstream if the patch prior selects structurally relevant patches?

**adjacent**

- [Studying Image Tokenizers as Visual Languages in Unified Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09143) — Li et al. · arXiv
  Controlled study of how visual tokenizer choice affects joint multimodal learning, tracking T2I and I2T validation losses during continual pretraining to define "multimodal learnability." For SURF: if structural patch selection alters the visual token distribution the VLM sees (more structure-rich, fewer texture/background patches), the resulting distribution may have different multimodal learnability properties — a training-time concern for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]] if the structural prior is applied before VLM input.

- [CLAMP: Constrained Decoding for Vision-Language Embodied Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08602) — Ma & Kordjamshidi · arXiv
  Multimodal constraint-grounding that uses scene observation to restrict VLM plan decoding: objects must be visually present, actions must have available affordances. Alternative injection point for affordance/structural constraints vs. SURF's patch pre-selection — if affordances can be enforced at the LM decoding step with a frozen VLM, one argument for pre-selection priors weakens (compute efficiency argument remains, but task-relevance argument needs recalibration).

**horizon**

- [Interpreting V1 Population Activity via Image-Neural Latent Representation Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04309) — Wang et al. · arXiv
  Contrastive dual-tower framework (DINA) aligning image feature representations with V1 population responses — enables interpretable probing of which image features drive V1 activity. Methodologically adjacent to SURF's contrastive patch-language alignment; the biological grounding question for V4 (does SURF's line-drawing prior capture V4-relevant structure?) could adopt a similar dual-tower probing approach against V4 neural recordings, rather than relying only on the dispersity measure in @jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying.

- [IBNorm: Information-Bottleneck Inspired Normalization for Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25262) — Zou & Zhou · arXiv
  IB-inspired normalization that adds bounded compression into LayerNorm/RMSNorm operations — encourages task-relevant information and suppresses nuisance variability at the representation level. If SURF's visual budget is operationalized as an IB problem, IBNorm suggests the compression can be distributed across normalization layers in the encoder rather than concentrated in a discrete token selection step; the efficiency-without-collapse concern becomes a normalization design question rather than a pruning ratio question.
