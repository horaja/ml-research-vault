**core**

- [Gaze on the Prize: Shaping Visual Attention with Return-Guided Contrastive Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08442) — Lee, Chuang, Gao · arXiv 2025
  Self-supervised contrastive objective derived from the agent's return signal trains a foveal attention mechanism to focus on task-relevant regions — no structural priors, no external labels. 2.52× sample efficiency gain on ManiSkill3; solves tasks the baseline fails. Direct ablation baseline for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]]: this is "contrastive supervision without structural geometry" — if return-guided contrastive attention alone recovers task-relevant patches, SURF's line-drawing prior must show additive benefit on OOD generalization or annotation-free deployment.

- [Attention from Action, for Action: Emergent Visual Bottlenecks for Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13422) — Zhuang, Wang, Heppert et al. · arXiv 2026
  "Seeker": task- and state-conditioned ROI readout trained from action supervision alone — no gaze, no segmentation labels, no structural geometry. Produces progression-aware spatial masks used for RGB cropping, background augmentation, and point-cloud filtering. Entered priorities on 09-11 but not yet digested. Defines the action-supervision baseline for [[claims/structure helps]]: if action-supervised ROI covers manipulation-relevant regions in-distribution, SURF's structural prior must differentiate on data-efficiency or pre-deployment generalization where action labels are unavailable.

- [Efficient Semantic Understanding from Digital Foveation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04088) — Caccavella, Fra, Ziegler et al. · arXiv 2026
  Active-vision pipeline: saliency-driven fixation selection → high-resolution foveal + low-resolution peripheral patches → semantic accumulation under adaptive budget. Tests biological foveation as a compute-reduction strategy for semantic segmentation. Tests SURF's operative assumption: non-uniform spatial sampling under task-adaptive fixation maintains semantic quality at lower compute. Domain is segmentation, not manipulation — the key open question is whether saliency-driven fixation generalizes to instruction-conditioned priors in closed-loop VLA inference.

**adjacent**

- [MINERVA: How Small Can a Manipulation Policy Be and Still Solve LIBERO?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03715) — Sendai, Matsushima, Iwasawa · arXiv 2026
  0.54M-parameter visuomotor policy achieves 95.1% average success on LIBERO — 2.4 pts below billion-parameter SOTA VLAs. Extends the PredVLA falsifier question from 09-02: if sub-million-parameter models nearly match billion-parameter VLAs on LIBERO, SURF's compute-efficiency argument for token pruning must target (a) harder tasks requiring richer visual semantics, or (b) real-deployment latency constraints — not benchmark performance on LIBERO-scale tasks.

- [World-to-Wrist: Task-Conditioned Future Wrist Modeling for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05369) — Pan, Peng, Zhang et al. · arXiv 2026
  Decouples main-view and wrist-view processing: global task context grounds the main view; wrist-view attention is guided by task-conditioned prediction of where the wrist will be. Directly addresses the multi-view budget design question SURF needs to resolve: the wrist-view token budget should be allocated based on predicted task state, not compressed uniformly with the main view. W2-VLA provides one instantiation of this design decision.

- [Select, Compress, Reinvest: A Controlled Study of Visual-Token Allocation in Long-Video MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03820) — Khatri · arXiv 2026
  Controlled ablation holding all allocation decisions fixed and varying one at a time (frame selection, spatial compression, compute reinvestment). Methodological reference for SURF's efficiency evaluation design: the controlled-study framing is how SURF should isolate the contribution of structural priors from other allocation policy choices when reporting efficiency results.

**horizon**

- [Explicitly Modeling Subcortical Vision with a Neuro-Inspired Front-End Improves CNN Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03089) — Piper, Oliveira, Marques · NeurIPS 2025 · 4 cites
  Introduces EVNets: VOneBlock (V1) + SubcorticalBlock (retina/LGN) as a CNN front-end parameterized to match subcortical physiology; improves OOD robustness without task-specific training. Horizon connection to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: EVNets operationalize the question "what does cortical front-end modeling buy for robustness?" at the subcortical level. The V4 analogue would be: does modeling V4 mid-level shape/texture selectivity as a patch-selection prior improve out-of-distribution robustness analogously? EVNets' with/without-front-end OOD robustness benchmark design is a template for the V4 prior ablation.
