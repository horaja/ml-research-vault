**core**

- [Vision-Language-Action Models: A Life-Cycle Survey of Design, Training and Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18372) — Kadam & Parsa · arXiv Nov 2026
  Reviews 88 VLA works (Dec 2022–Oct 2026) across manipulation, navigation, and humanoid control along seven life-cycle axes: model design, pretraining, fine-tuning, deployment, efficiency, safety, evaluation. For SURF: the efficiency axis likely surveys token-selection methods; the design axis may position structural priors relative to the dominant architectural choices. Reading this before drafting the positioning note may compress the unread queue bottleneck — if the survey's efficiency coverage is current through Oct 2026, it reaches most of SURF's competitor papers.

- [SWAM: Saliency-Weighted Asymmetric Merging for Efficient Vision Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18057) — Singh et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Extends Bipartite Soft Matching (ToMe) with two saliency-aware mechanisms: cosine similarity gates candidate selection, gradient-based weighting refines merge ratios. Training-based, not training-free; beats ToMe on ImageNet at the same token budget. For SURF: saliency-weighted merging is a competing approach in the token-reduction space that uses a purely data-driven signal (gradients + cosine) instead of a structural prior. The key question: does V4 shape structure generalize better than gradient-based saliency on OOD manipulation scenes, or is SWAM's saliency signal sufficient without geometric inductive bias?

- [An In-Silico Audit of Topographic Organization in a Multimodal Brain-Response Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18105) — Strawa · arXiv Nov 2026
  Audits TRIBE v2 (a large fMRI cortical response model) for the classic markers of topographic organization — retinotopy, tonotopy, MT+ motion selectivity — and finds that TRIBE v2's spatial averaging before the response head destroys retinotopic structure. For SURF: this is the first empirical confirmation that spatial averaging is architecturally destructive to topographic organization, directly supporting the pre-encoder placement rationale. SURF's structural prior operates before spatial pooling precisely to preserve the spatially organized information that TRIBE v2 discards — connects to [[claims/v4 relevance]].

**adjacent**

- [ESI: An Embodied Self-Improvement Flywheel for LLM-VLA Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18176) — Cai et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Introduces an automated flywheel that uses environment interaction to align reasoning and action in a modular LLM+VLA system; repeatedly refines the interface so the LLM's intended subtask and the VLA's actions stay coordinated. For SURF: shows the field is actively solving the LLM-VLA interface problem that SURF's action pipeline will eventually face. The flywheel mechanism — environment interaction as alignment signal — is a candidate approach for SURF's adaptive budget policy training (outcome-conditioned, no step-level supervision).

- [Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18562) — Tang et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Trains an open-weight 27B reasoning model via RL in simulation with no demonstrations; the model observes the environment, reasons in chain-of-thought, and generates end-effector poses natively. For SURF: extends the Reins frontier-baseline question to open-weight models. If a 27B reasoning model achieves manipulation via RL in simulation, SURF's efficiency claims need a comparison against this paradigm (not only fine-tuned VLAs) — particularly on tasks where structured visual selection would be expected to help (shortcut scenes, OOD textures).

**horizon**

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — Zhao, Verdel, Tan, Burdet · Jul 2026
  Proposes a dual stochastic model predictive control formulation where gaze patterns emerge from task-relevance uncertainty reduction; saccade-pursuit sequences are explained as an information-extraction strategy that minimizes action uncertainty before movement onset. For SURF: behavioral grounding for the action-relevance claim — if gaze selection in humans is fundamentally an uncertainty-reduction strategy for action planning, SURF's visual token selection (structural prior → task-conditioned selection) approximates the same computation in a learned form. The dual-MPC framing is a candidate formal model for SURF's selector objective.

- [Why Finite Intelligence Works in an Infinite World: A Mathematical Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18538) — Soltanalian · arXiv Nov 2026
  Formalizes why finite representations succeed in infinite environments when task queries share collective structure; defines "future-task information" as the minimum representation needed to preserve decision value relative to available evidence. For SURF: a theoretical framing of why a fixed visual budget can work at all — if manipulation tasks share structural patterns, a compact structural prior (SURF's V4-inspired selector) is sufficient. The "future-task information" measure may be an operational metric for SURF's visual budget: the minimum token set preserving task-relevant decision value.
