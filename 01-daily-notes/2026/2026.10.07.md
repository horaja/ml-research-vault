# 2026-10-07

## opener

Positioning note is day 43. The core spatial-structure argument is now showing up in the published literature as a diagnosed failure mode: "Beyond Token Importance: Preserving Spatial Scaffolds" demonstrates that VLA pruning methods which select by semantic relevance alone fail because spatial relationships between tokens matter for manipulation. They build a uniform Stride baseline (spatial uniform sampling) that beats semantic-only pruning. SURF's structural geometric prior must beat this spatial-uniform Stride baseline — not just generic semantic selection — to justify line-drawing computation. Add Stride as a required baseline in the contrastive alignment spec.

SAVLA provides the architecture-level version of the same finding: VLAs trained only on demonstrations fail on OOD viewpoints because they acquire spatial competence from demo coverage, not from geometric inductive biases. This sharpens SURF's positioning — structural priors are geometric inductive biases that generalize beyond demo viewpoints.

"Have I Seen Enough?" is directly relevant to [[claims/iterative selection]]: frozen VideoLLMs carry a linearly decodable evidence-readiness signal. If frozen VLA backbones carry the same signal under closed-loop manipulation, SURF's stopping criterion may not require a trained RL policy — just a lightweight linear readout from backbone activations. Read before finalizing the iterative selection design.

All three papers inform the positioning note. Write it today. No remaining structural blockers.

  Stage-aware VLA token pruning asks both which tokens to drop and at which pipeline stage — early vision, cross-modal interaction, or action head. - [Have I Seen Enough? Frozen Video-Language Models Encode Evidence Readiness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08560v1) — Ben-Ami et al. · arXiv
  Unmodified frozen VideoLLMs carry a linearly decodable signal indicating when sufficient evidence for answering the current question has accumulated. Candidate mechanism for [[claims/iterative selection]]: if frozen VLA backbones encode the same readiness signal under closed-loop manipulation, the stopping criterion for iterative visual selection is already implicit in the backbone — no separate RL policy or threshold rule needed. Check: does the readiness signal generalize from streaming video QA to VLA closed-loop inference with a fixed instruction and evolving scene?

**adjacent**

- [TLC-DiT: Task-Aligned Local Visual Conditioning for Robust Multitask Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/519e831fb8ede93c653bc2b157a275badb65a10e) — Cai et al. · preprint
  Makes task-relevant local visual evidence explicit as a conditioning signal rather than letting it remain buried inside backbone attention; improves robustness to visual distribution shift. Design reference for SURF's contrastive alignment architecture: how to inject spatially explicit geometric features into a policy without the stop-gradient problem documented in Wiring Matters. The explicit-local-evidence framing mirrors SURF's structural prior output, and the injection mechanism is the open architectural question.

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — Zhao et al. · bioRxiv
  Characteristic human saccade-pursuit patterns in manipulation emerge from information-dependent gaze — shared neural circuits govern both eye and hand movements, with gaze arriving at contact regions ~200ms before hand arrival. Biological grounding for SURF's active vision framing: the ground-truth gaze signal is information-driven and precedes contact, which is consistent with SURF's structural prior as a predictive pre-movement selection mechanism. Key check: are the informative gaze targets in manipulation consistent with structural/edge-defined regions, or do they require semantic object knowledge?

- [SAVLA: Symmetry-Aware Vision-Language-Action Models for Robotic Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d1f93e6c5590ad9a52d8908f356fd1f264dcb0f5) — Li et al. · preprint
  VLAs that learn spatial competence only from demonstrations fail dramatically on OOD viewpoints; injecting geometric symmetry priors as inductive bias closes the gap. Architecture-level motivation for SURF's structural prior: demo-fitted spatial competence doesn't generalize; geometry-grounded priors do. SAVLA's viewpoint-shift evaluations could serve as a benchmark for SURF's generalization claims if the same manipulation suite is accessible.

**horizon**

- [From the Drosophila Visual Connectome to General-Purpose Computer Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08418v1) — Li et al. · arXiv
  Connectome-derived architecture (FlyVision: ON/OFF parallel processing, recurrence, population-level graph) improves OOD robustness in general-purpose computer vision. Extends [[claims/v4 relevance]]: biologically structured inductive biases from connectomes transfer useful computational properties even without direct task correspondence. SURF's structural prior is a simpler form of the same principle — organized mid-level visual structure as a prior over where to allocate compute.

- [Knowing When to Trust a Prior: Reliability-Gated Cue Fusion for Video Gaze Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08663v1) — Zhu et al. · arXiv
  FocusGate gates an ensemble of gaze-free spatial priors (including defocus-map-derived shape statistics) based on per-frame reliability estimates; priors abstain when their signal is unreliable. Meta-principle for SURF's prior module: the line-drawing structural prior should be conditionally applied based on image content reliability (e.g., low edge density, textureless uniform backgrounds), not always applied uniformly. Formalizes the open question of when SURF's geometric prior is informative vs. uninformative.

## questions

New from today:
- Beyond Token Importance: Does SURF's line-drawing structural prior outperform uniform spatial Stride sampling at the same compression ratio in manipulation? Stride beats semantic-only; structure must beat Stride to justify geometric computation. This is now a required baseline.
- Have I Seen Enough: Does the linearly decodable evidence-readiness signal in frozen VideoLLMs generalize to frozen VLA backbones under closed-loop manipulation? If yes, iterative selection stopping criterion is a linear readout, not a trained RL policy.
- When and What to Prune: What is the stage taxonomy — before cross-modal attention, after, or at the action head boundary? Does SURF's input-stage operation correspond to the earliest or an intermediate stage in this taxonomy?
- FocusGate / Reliability-Gated Prior: Can image statistics (edge density map, texture homogeneity) serve as reliability signals for SURF's structural prior — deciding when to trust line-drawing selection vs. fall back to full-patch processing?
- SAVLA: Do the viewpoint-OOD failure modes in demo-trained VLAs correspond to scenes where SURF's geometric prior would maintain correct spatial coverage, while the demo-trained policy fails? This could be a testable prediction for SURF's generalization claim.
- Eye-hand coordination (Zhao et al.): Are the contact-preceding fixation targets in manipulation consistent with edge/structural regions, or do they require object-semantic knowledge (e.g., handle vs. rim identification that pure structure can't provide)?
