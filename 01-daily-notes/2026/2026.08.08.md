**World-to-Wrist** (Pan et al., s2 today) is today's most immediately useful paper for the spec. The VLA community treats main-view and wrist-view tokens as parallel inputs, but W2-VLA decomposes them explicitly: main view provides global task context, wrist view contains action-local interaction detail. The paper trains a task-conditioned wrist-view predictor that anticipates how the wrist region will evolve given the task instruction and current main-view state. The prediction target is task-relevant by construction: the wrist region encodes contact, affordance boundary, and object interaction — exactly the patches surf's structural prior should be selecting for. This is the most concrete existing ground truth for "which patches carry action-relevant information" in manipulation. For [[experiments/contrastive alignment]], the wrist-view prediction task is a candidate auxiliary training signal for the contrastive scorer — if surf's structural prior co-locates with W2-VLA's predicted wrist attention, that's a stronger alignment argument than gaze alone.

**Adaptive-WAM** (Ang et al., arXiv 2608.06008) and **Robust-WAM** (Yan et al., arXiv 2608.05903) together provide the clearest world-model-side motivation for surf yet. Robust-WAM identifies the exact failure mode: VAE latent spaces in WAMs are trained with pixel reconstruction loss, which rewards texture and illumination fidelity and leaves action prediction fragile under appearance variation. This is the same structural argument DreamWAM (8/6) made from the opposite direction — two papers in three days. Adaptive-WAM shows that iterative denoising in world-action models can be cut early: quality assessment of intermediate diffusion features determines the stopping point. This is the autonomous driving analog of [[claims/iterative selection]] — a direct operationalized stopping criterion using representational quality as the trigger, not action uncertainty.

**adjacent**

- [Robust-WAM: Bridging Generative Pretraining and Semantic Foresight in World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05903) — Yan et al. · arXiv 2026-08-07
  VAE latent spaces in world-action models are optimized for pixel reconstruction, rewarding texture and illumination fidelity at the cost of action-relevant semantics. Motivational complement to DreamWAM (8/6): DreamWAM argues for non-RGB prediction targets; Robust-WAM provides the mechanistic explanation for why RGB latents fail. Together, two consecutive papers make the case that structural token selection (discarding texture/background while retaining contour geometry) avoids the failure mode that plagues RGB-latent WAMs. External validation of [[claims/structure helps]] from the world-model direction.

- [DyPES-VLA: Learning Shared Dynamics Priors and Embodiment-Specific Control for Cross-Embodiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06374) — Li et al. · arXiv 2026-08-08
  Disentangles shared visual dynamics priors (object physics, contact geometry — common across embodiments) from embodiment-specific action execution. Empirical identification of what a transferable structural prior should capture in VLA: contact geometry and object physics, not appearance. Adjacent to surf's prior module design: surf's line-density prior is a training-free proxy for contact geometry; DyPES-VLA's learned shared prior is the upper bound on what that proxy is approximating. If DyPES-VLA's shared prior co-locates with line density on LIBERO frames, the approximation is valid.

- [Beyond Flat Policies: Hierarchical Post-Training for Embodied Agents in Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05999) — Kong et al. · arXiv 2026-08-07
  Identifies that flat policy optimization fails to model task progression in long-horizon manipulation; introduces hierarchical post-training that explicitly decomposes tasks into stages. Adjacent to [[claims/iterative selection]]: hierarchical task stages imply different visual information needs at each stage — pre-grasp approach needs global spatial structure, contact phase needs wrist-local detail, post-grasp needs object-state changes. Stage-conditioned token allocation is a natural implementation of surf's iterative selection within a hierarchical policy; the stage boundary is a candidate trigger for reallocation.

**horizon**

- [Explicitly Modeling Subcortical Vision with a Neuro-Inspired Front-End Improves CNN Robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d2c2818f16c45693869453594661a79b40a38dd) — Piper et al. · NeurIPS
  Adds an explicit subcortical (LGN, V1) processing front-end to CNNs — center-surround filtering, orientation-selective V1 responses, spatial frequency bandpass — improving OOD robustness on corrupted images and distribution shifts. Biological grounding for surf's structural preprocessing: subcortical processing is what produces edge, line, and orientation structure from raw visual input. If explicitly modeling V1-level structure improves downstream robustness in CNNs, then surf's line-density prior (a simplified V1 output) has analogous justification. Also constrains the biological analogy: surf's prior corresponds to subcortical, not V4-level, processing — V4 relevance argument is about feature selection criteria, not the feature extractor itself.

- Robust-WAM + DreamWAM × [[claims/structure helps]] validation: Two papers in three days identify RGB latent/prediction as the failure mode in world-action models, both motivating non-RGB structural representations. Should the spec include a direct measurement of RGB-vs-structural-token action relevance on LIBERO — e.g., mutual information between structural geometry tokens and action outcomes vs. appearance tokens and action outcomes?
- DyPES-VLA × [[claims/structure helps]] prior approximation quality: DyPES-VLA empirically identifies shared cross-embodiment dynamics priors (contact geometry, object physics). Is surf's line-density prior a valid training-free approximation of DyPES-VLA's learned shared prior? - Beyond Flat Policies × [[claims/iterative selection]] stage trigger: Hierarchical task stages have qualitatively different visual information needs (pre-grasp vs. contact vs. post-grasp). Should surf's iterative selection trigger be stage-conditioned (hierarchical boundary as reallocation event) rather than uncertainty-gated (continuous action entropy threshold)? - Subcortical front-end × [[claims/v4 relevance]] biological hierarchy: Piper et al. show subcortical (LGN, V1) front-ends improve OOD robustness. Surf's structural prior produces subcortical-level features (line density ≈ V1 edge response); [[claims/v4 relevance]] argues for a V4-level prior (cue-invariant 3D structure, as per Aoki et al. from 8/5). Is the correct prior V1-level (subcortical filtering, training-free) or V4-level (cue-aggregated intermediate, requires learned features)? Can both levels be used — V1 as the training-free structural prior, V4 as the learned refinement?
