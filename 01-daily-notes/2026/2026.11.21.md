## questions

Carried from 2026-11-20:
- VLA Survey: Does the efficiency axis cover structural-prior-based token selection as a subfield, and does it position it relative to saliency-based, spectral, and language-conditioned approaches?
- SWAM: Does SWAM's saliency signal (gradient × cosine) capture the same structural patterns as SURF's V4 line-drawing prior, or are the two signals empirically orthogonal on manipulation scenes?
- Topographic audit: Does the spatial-averaging-destroys-retinotopy finding generalize beyond TRIBE v2 to the ViT backbones SURF targets?
- ActPrune: Does SURF's V4 shape structure add OOD robustness over ActPrune's action-grounded signal?
- ThinkProprio: Does proprioceptive state capture enough action-relevance that SURF's structural prior adds nothing beyond state-grounded gating?
- AIM: Does AIM's trajectory-derived spatial value map select the same regions as SURF's V4 shape prior on tabletop manipulation — or are the two signals orthogonal on out-of-distribution scenes?
- APS-VLA: Does APS-VLA's action-guided posterior selection outperform SURF's pre-encoder shape prior on OOD manipulation scenes, and what is the token-budget vs. task-success tradeoff for each?
- Action topography: If action structure organizes V4 topography in DNNs trained on natural video, do ViT backbones fine-tuned on manipulation show analogous action-tuned topographic organization?
- Phoenix vs SURF: Does Phoenix's learned scene structure generalize better on novel objects and backgrounds, or does grounding in primate V4 mid-level vision confer robustness over purely learned structure representations?
- Radial attention: If VLA radial attention is NOT a sink pattern but a signal of action-relevant token importance, which tokens drive it and do they overlap with what a V4 shape prior selects?
- A²-VLA affordance: Does the learned affordance representation in A²-VLA overlap with what a V4 shape prior selects on tabletop manipulation scenes?
