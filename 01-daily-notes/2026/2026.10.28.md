**core**

- [Artificial Foveated Perception for Mitigating Shortcut Learning in Robotic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10655) — Sun et al. · arXiv Jul 2026
  Replaces full-frame fine-tuning with a foveated attention mechanism that directs learning away from spurious background correlations; shows shortcut learning degrades under modest scene changes and foveation recovers it. Direct mechanistic answer to the shortcut risk PerturBot (Oct 27) quantified for SURF's line-drawing prior: are the structural edges selected by SURF co-varying with task identity in the training set? This paper provides a procedural test and a structural fix to compare against.

- [Learning Robot Metareasoning: Adaptive Computation Budgets for Real-Time Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23662) — Magnino et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Frames the deliberation-reactivity tradeoff as a metareasoning problem: how long to plan given that the robot must commit to open-loop action chunks during planning. Directly formalizes the budget allocation problem behind [[claims/iterative selection]] — SURF's coarse-to-fine selection must also decide how many refinement steps to spend given action-chunking constraints. Read alongside FastVLA (Oct 25) and Shi et al. (Oct 27).

- [IronMan: Information-Constrained Video-Action Learning for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07961) — Zhang et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Applies an information-bottleneck-style objective to visual representations in video-action models: restricting visual token exposure to what is action-relevant improves generalization. Closest empirical instantiation of [[claims/efficiency without collapse]]: fewer visual tokens, maintained or improved task performance. Check whether the IB constraint selects structurally coherent tokens (consistent with SURF's prior) or semantically diffuse ones (challenging the structural-prior claim).

**adjacent**

- [ObjDream: Guiding Vision-Language-Action Models with Object-Level Understanding and Future State Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20223) — Ma et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Replaces image-level VLA features with object-level representations and future state predictions as the visual grounding signal. Object-level segmentation regions vs. SURF's line-drawing patches: check geometric overlap on manipulation tasks. If object masks and line-drawing selections are highly overlapping, SURF's prior may be learning to approximate object masks, which would require justification (why line drawings vs. direct segmentation?).

- [GuidedAttention: Interpretable and Correctable Visual Attention for OOD-Robust Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21049) — Murooka et al. · arXiv Jul 2026
  Makes visuomotor attention interpretable and human-correctable; improves OOD robustness by allowing spatial attention to be guided away from spurious cues. Interpretability angle for SURF's prior module: structural priors that produce human-interpretable selection maps are correctable in the same way. If GuidedAttention uses similar spatial selection signals to SURF's line-drawing prior, it provides an interpretability framing SURF can leverage.

**horizon**

- [Can Discrete Tokens Serve as Semantically Lossless Representations of Visual Signals?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37662) — Zou et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Investigates whether discrete visual tokens (VQ-style quantization) lose semantically meaningful information vs. continuous representations; evaluates across VQA, generation, and retrieval tasks. Foundation for SURF's information-sufficiency claim: if hard token selection (k-sparse discrete tokens) is semantically lossless for the relevant downstream task, the efficiency-without-collapse claim holds without continuous approximations. Check whether the paper's notion of "semantic losslessness" maps onto action-relevant information or only language-aligned semantics.

- [Active Vision in Driving: Joint Modeling of Scanpaths and Risk Perception](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b1fc9fdc6865de4e7146709ea4dca6146aaf5cf) — Gou et al. · Journal of Eye Movement Research 2026
  Joint computational model of scanpaths and risk perception in driving under the active vision hypothesis: eye movements driven by task-demand uncertainty, not passive salience. Normative framing for SURF's prior module — if task-directed attention is an uncertainty-minimization policy, the prior module is learning to pre-minimize visual uncertainty for action, and "when to stop selecting" is equivalent to "when is remaining uncertainty action-irrelevant."

## questions

Carried:
- SAPrune: At what LLM layer does token importance stabilize? Is SURF's pre-encoder structural prior a valid coarse filter or misplaced?
- Attention from Action: What is the novel-object failure mode of action-supervision-derived ROIs? At what distribution-shift threshold does label-free ROI emergence degrade, and does SURF's geometric prior cover that regime?
- FastVLA: Does temporal adaptation of visual sparsity require per-step recomputation of the token selector, or can SURF's geometric prior serve as a stable per-step base with iterative refinement on top?
- VLA-ACL: Does action-consistency supervision select structurally coherent regions, or semantically scattered ones? Does adding SURF's geometric prior to action-consistency supervision yield orthogonal coverage?
- CoRP: Does VIB failure on LIBERO-Goal mean SURF's contrastive alignment must produce hard selections?
- Shi et al. (Stage-Aware): Does the per-stage pruning efficacy curve show a distinct inflection after language-fusion, or is degradation gradual? Does the pattern differ for novel-object vs. in-distribution scenes?
- RAIN (Lee et al.): Does region-aware gating select geometrically coherent regions (compatible with line-drawing prior) or semantically diffuse ones (suggesting independent mechanisms)?

New (from today):
- Sun et al. (Artificial Foveated): Does SURF's line-drawing prior select edges that co-vary with task identity in training data? What is the right intervention test — does perturbing background while preserving line-drawing selection region change action prediction?
- Zhang et al. (IronMan): Does the IB-constrained representation select structurally coherent visual regions, or semantically diffuse ones? Is the bottleneck size in IronMan comparable to SURF's token budget?
- Magnino et al. (Metareasoning): What is the correct metareasoning objective for SURF's refinement step — minimize expected action error per compute unit, or minimize value-of-information per unit cost?
