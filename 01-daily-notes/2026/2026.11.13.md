**core**

- [Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334) — Brown et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Isolates visual encoder design axes (patch size, resolution, contrastive vs. reconstructive objective, frozen vs. fine-tuned) and measures their independent downstream effect on spatial/structural task performance. For SURF: answers whether the pre-encoder structural prior is a sufficient intervention or whether encoder choice dominates — if patch size and objective swamp the structural signal, the prior must operate post-encoder; the decomposition method is also a template for SURF's own encoder placement ablation.

- [STVLA: Spatial-aware and Temporal-memory for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16589) — arXiv Nov 2026
  Integrates explicit spatial attention and temporal memory into a VLA; evaluates on manipulation tasks requiring both spatial precision and multi-step temporal dependencies. For SURF: a concurrent system adding spatial awareness to VLA without line-drawing priors — the comparison question is whether explicit geometric structure (SURF) or implicit spatial attention modules (STVLA) gives better sample efficiency and cross-task generalization.

- [Objects as Addresses: Instance Binding in Vision Transformer Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17240) — Sabri et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Shows that ViTs develop intrinsic object-binding mechanisms in attention — specific heads function as "address registers" localizing object instances without explicit supervision. For SURF: if ViTs already bind objects structurally, the line-drawing prior's value shifts from supplying structure the model lacks to amplifying or steering structure already present — different architecture implications (additive vs. multiplicative prior) for [[claims/structure helps]].

**adjacent**

- [Reins: Frontier Multimodal Models Driving Real Robot Arms, Zero-Shot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16632) — arXiv Nov 2026
  Demonstrates zero-shot real-robot arm control using frontier MLLMs (GPT-6.1, Claude Opus 5.5) without fine-tuning; structured prompting and tool-use scaffolding bridge perception to action. For SURF: positions the frontier-model-as-controller approach as a viable no-training baseline — SURF's efficiency claims need comparison against zero-shot frontier-model control, not only fine-tuned VLAs.

- [The Value of Thinking: Learning When to Reason in Hybrid Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16961) — Yu et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Trains a stop criterion for a hybrid fast/slow decision model that determines when chain-of-thought reasoning is worth the compute cost; learns from task outcomes rather than supervision on the reasoning process itself. For SURF: directly relevant to [[claims/iterative selection]] — Yu et al.'s outcome-supervised stop criterion is exactly what SURF needs for adaptive patch selection depth without labeling individual selection steps.

- [From Foundation VLM to Versatile Driving VLM: A Triplet Incremental Training Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16727) — arXiv Nov 2026
  Adapts a foundation VLM to spatial/driving tasks (depth estimation, 3D detection, BEV reasoning) via three sequential fine-tuning stages without structural priors; achieves strong spatial performance. For SURF: a system that develops spatial competence through fine-tuning alone is a direct challenge to [[claims/structure helps]] necessity — the question is whether SURF's structural prior gives equivalent spatial performance with less data or fewer training stages.

**horizon**

- [Natural Gradient Learning in Predictive Coding through Lateral Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16666) — Beumer et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Derives natural gradient updates in predictive coding networks via lateral inhibitory circuits, unifying local energy minimization with efficient Bayesian inference. For SURF: biological grounding for iterative selection via lateral spatial competition — if V4 uses lateral circuits for natural-gradient-like processing, SURF's contrastive alignment objective approximates this computation, and the lateral-circuit substrate suggests where in a ViT to look for analogous dynamics ([[claims/v4 relevance]]).

- [Rethinking Actions: Continuity-Aware Priors for World-Model Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16645) — Yang et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Introduces continuity priors (temporal smoothness, action coherence) into world-model planning; improves sample efficiency and reduces planning artifacts. For SURF: structural priors in the temporal domain improve planning the same way spatial structural priors should improve visual selection — the symmetry supports the structural-prior intuition across modalities and pipeline stages.
