**EcoFrame** (Li et al., arXiv 2608.03918) is today's most structurally informative paper. It decomposes the adaptive visual evidence scheduling problem into a dual "when" + "where" mechanism: "when" scores temporal coverage across the full clip to identify which frames carry the most evidence; "where" performs spatial evidence selection within the prioritized frames. Both are training-free. This is the clearest existing operationalization of the [[claims/iterative selection]] dual-trigger architecture surf needs to formalize. The "when" gate maps directly to surf's replanning trigger (when to reallocate the visual token budget across a manipulation episode); the "where" map maps to surf's structural prior (which patches to retain at the reallocated budget). Three tractable differences — EcoFrame operates over long video (minutes) vs. manipulation episodes (seconds); EcoFrame's spatial selection is appearance-based (visual entropy) vs. surf's structurally grounded (line density + language); EcoFrame targets VQA accuracy vs. surf's action success. These are scope differences, not disqualifying overlaps. The "when+where" decomposition is precisely what the iterative selection section of the spec needs to formalize before it can be written.

**Break-Even Study** (Dou & Tian, arXiv 2608.03649) is a diagnostic threat to [[claims/efficiency without collapse]]. The 30-example pilot demonstrates that reducing token count does not automatically reduce inference latency: selection overhead (computing scores, identifying low-information tokens, compressing the sequence) can exceed the attention savings, especially at low reduction ratios. Break-even varies with decision location in the network, hardware regime (memory-bandwidth-bound vs. compute-bound), and reduction ratio. Surf's efficiency claim is only valid if the specific combination of selection mechanism cost and token reduction ratio clears the break-even on VLA inference hardware. The correct response is not to abandon the efficiency claim but to add a hardware-calibrated break-even measurement as a required component of the spec: include a break-even experiment on the LIBERO inference hardware, reporting the minimum reduction ratio at which surf's selection overhead is recouped.
