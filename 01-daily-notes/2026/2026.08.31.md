**core**

- [KEMO: Event-Driven Keyframe Memory for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation with VLA Policies](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/044e05409875576f8f9d896587d2088137629cfa) — Zeng et al. · arXiv Jun 2026
  Selects and preserves task-relevant keyframes triggered by state events rather than all frames uniformly; lightweight plug-in for VLA memory. Adds a temporal budget axis orthogonal to SURF's spatial patch selection: KEMO selects *which frames* to keep; SURF selects *which patches* within a frame. The positioning note should acknowledge these as distinct but composable — a KEMO+SURF pipeline could reduce both temporal and spatial redundancy.

- [PosA-VLA: Enhancing Action Generation via Pose-Conditioned Anchor Attention](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d77a24298f448ff9f6aeabec20137aebeb4534e9) — Li et al. · arXiv Dec 2025
  Pose-conditioned supervision anchors visual attention in VLA; uses robot pose as a structural signal to guide where the attention concentrates during action generation. A fourth grounding modality for visual attention alongside structure / language / proprioceptive state. If pose-conditioned anchoring already directs attention to task-relevant regions, SURF's structural (geometric line) + language prior needs to show it captures what pose conditioning misses — likely contact regions and object affordances not recoverable from end-effector pose alone.

- [MoE-ACT: Scaling Multi-Task Bimanual Manipulation with Sparse Language-Conditioned Mixture-of-Experts](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6882a0ece1703c2988a0d7e6b7c11ff8d23d26db) — Guo et al. · arXiv Mar 2026
  Sparse language-conditioned MoE routing achieves strong multi-task bimanual manipulation without any geometric prior; sparsity is gating, not token selection, but the mechanism is language conditioning alone. Most direct test of [[claims/language helps]] in bimanual manipulation: if language routing alone reaches strong performance, the "language helps" claim must specify what structural priors add — likely action-relevant spatial localization that language routing learns over many tasks but fails on held-out instruction-object combinations.

**adjacent**

- [ActiveVOO: Value of Observation Guided Active Knowledge Acquisition for Open-World Embodied Lifted Reasoning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/67858f9068e0caffbf978f50602152bb08e0d4b2) — Liu et al. · NeurIPS
  Formalizes active knowledge acquisition as value-of-observation (VOO) maximization; zero-shot framework that selects observations to most reduce uncertainty about task-relevant object properties. Provides decision-theoretic grounding for SURF's visual budget question: the "when to refine" and "what to look at next" subproblems in [[claims/iterative selection]] could be formalized as VOO maximization over structural hypotheses rather than heuristic token scoring.

- [LaVA-Man: Learning Visual Action Representations for Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a202173ded58b596893ecd5919751f36fab82ec8) — Zhu et al. · arXiv Aug 2025
  Self-supervised pretext task: reconstruct a masked goal image conditioned on the instruction; learns action-relevant visual-textual associations without explicit selection or spatial annotation. Alternative path to what SURF's contrastive prior targets — if masked goal reconstruction teaches the model which visual regions matter for task success, the supervision signal is reconstruction quality, not contrastive alignment. Check whether this handles manipulation-instruction specificity (near-field affordances, contact regions) or only scene-level goal semantics.

**horizon**

- [Investigating action topography in visual cortex and deep artificial neural networks](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c87a943995f33c4efc8a3b64d9c2358e3d314649) — Cortinovis et al. · bioRxiv Aug 2025
  Proposes action as a key organizing principle for visual cortex topography alongside shape/texture; tests whether action-tuned ANNs develop V4-like topographic maps. Directly updates [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if action relevance — not just shape sensitivity — organizes V4 topography, SURF's structural (line/shape) prior and V4 grounding are mechanistically connected through action tuning. The implication is that structural priors derived from V4 already encode action relevance, not just shape abstraction.

- [Compact deep neural network models of the visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/18d00056b2cd000d3b365ac5c17e11c885bf0b2f) — Cowley et al. · Nature Feb 2026
  Shows compact DNNs suffice for predicting individual V4 neurons; large models overfit neuron-specific noise; introduces a framework balancing per-neuron accuracy and representational diversity. Relevant to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: compact models that faithfully predict V4 could be used to derive a spatially-grounded selection prior from recorded V4 responses without requiring large-scale model machinery. Makes the V4-to-prior pipeline more tractable than previous work suggested.
