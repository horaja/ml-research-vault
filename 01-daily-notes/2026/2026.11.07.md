**core**

- [Aligning Policy Encoders with a Shape Teacher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08275) — Bernardy et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Proposes shape-teacher alignment: trains an image-based manipulation policy to encode 3D geometric shape information during training via distillation from a shape-aware teacher, without requiring any 3D sensing at deployment. The core mechanism — distilling geometric structure into an image-only encoder — is a direct alternative to SURF's inference-time structural prior. If shape-teacher distillation generalizes well across distribution shift, it becomes a strong baseline for [[claims/structure helps]]: the question shifts from "does structural information help" to "is explicit patch-selection necessary, or does learned shape encoding suffice?" Read to extract: what teacher representation is used, how well distillation generalizes beyond the training distribution, and what performance remains unexplained by shape encoding alone.

**adjacent**

- [OopsieRecover: Learning Transferable VLA Corrections from Semantic Interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08462) — Bahety et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  VLAs frequently fail during deployment; teaching recovery requires substantial corrective data. OopsieRecover learns recovery from semantic-level interventions (relabeling failed observations with corrective instructions) rather than dense action demonstrations, improving generalization of corrections. Adjacent to SURF: understanding VLA failure modes — specifically what visual context causes failures — motivates which patches the structural prior should preserve. The distribution-shift failure mode described here is likely the same failure mode SURF's structural prior is designed to prevent.

- [La-JEPA: Compact Robot Policies with Latent World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08156) — Clémente et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Latent Action JEPA: policy trained from scratch in JEPA feature space rather than on top of a large generation-oriented backbone. Achieves competitive control with significantly fewer parameters by decoupling representation learning from policy training. Relevant to SURF's [[claims/efficiency without collapse]]: if compact JEPA features already select for task-predictive structure at the representation level, what is the marginal value of explicit token selection on top? The comparison — JEPA's implicit learned structure vs. SURF's explicit structural prior — is a useful framing for the positioning note.

**horizon**

- [Beyond Quadratic Attention: Separable Gaussian Operators for ODE Vision Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08236) — Boned Riera et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  SSoG replaces self-attention in ViTs with a separable sum-of-Gaussians spatial operator: axis-factorized Gaussian kernels reduce per-layer cost from O(N²) to subquadratic while maintaining Lipschitz stability. An architectural path to visual efficiency that does not require dropping tokens at all — instead, the receptive field is smooth and bounded by design. Horizon contrast for SURF: discrete token dropping (SURF's approach) vs. continuous receptive field compression (SSoG). The fixed Gaussian structure is a form of structural prior imposed on the attention mechanism itself, which is adjacent to SURF's claim that structural priors improve selection.
