**core**

- [Deep learning-driven characterization of single cell tuning in primate visual area V4 supports topological organization](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f019f4561cd9689cdea12526e7464d3b242302a4) — Willeke, Restivo, Franke, Nix, Cadena, Tolias, Sinz et al. · bioRxiv
  Uses deep neural network models fit to V4 single-cell recordings to characterize functional columns and confirm topological organization. Directly grounds [[claims/v4 relevance]] in empirical single-cell data from the Tolias/Sinz lab. The key question for SURF: does the dispersity/column-geometry measure these models extract translate into a per-patch shape score usable as a selection prior on natural manipulation scenes?

- [SP-VLA: A Joint Model Scheduling and Token Pruning Approach for VLA Model Acceleration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12723) — Li et al. · ICLR
  Jointly optimizes execution frequency and visual token count based on real-time environmental complexity; the scheduling dimension adapts when the VLA runs, the pruning dimension adapts what it processes. ICLR acceptance. SURF must situate against this: scheduling + post-encoder pruning is a competing efficiency paradigm that makes no structural prior assumption. The question is whether SURF's pre-encoder shape-prior selection achieves better compute-performance tradeoffs than adaptive scheduling of a dense pipeline.

- [AIM: Intent-Aware Unified World Action Modeling with Spatial Value Maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11135) — Fan, Xu, Cao et al. · arXiv
  Integrates spatial value maps — density maps derived from robot end-effector trajectories — with video world-model representations to encode "where to interact and the underlying manipulation geometry." A competing structured spatial prior: trajectory-derived vs. SURF's V4-shape-derived. If AIM's dense trajectory prior already captures the spatial structure needed for action planning, SURF must show its shape-based prior generalizes better beyond the training trajectory distribution.

**adjacent**

- [Beyond Attention or Similarity: Maximizing Conditional Diversity for Token Pruning in MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10967) — Zhang, Liu, Li et al. · NeurIPS
  Proposes conditional diversity maximization as a pruning objective, arguing that both attention-based (retains duplicates) and similarity-based (misses inter-token dependencies) methods are suboptimal. NeurIPS. Relevant as a competing paradigm for SURF's importance-scored structural selection — if diversity is the right objective, the V4 prior's selectivity advantage shrinks. Worth reading alongside the efficiency baseline comparison.

- [Sensing Which Modality Matters: Evidence-Gated Regularization for Robust VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03142) — Yang, Romeres, Hori, Bertasius, Szafir, Jain · arXiv
  Modality entanglement under real-world occlusions/distractors: spurious inter-sensor correlations from homogeneous training data cause nuisance sensitivity when uninformative sensors are corrupted. Uses evidence-gated regularization. Adjacent to SURF's shortcut mitigation angle — this addresses spurious multimodal correlations via regularization rather than spatial selection. SURF's structural prior needs to be distinguished from sensor-level gating on the same benchmark scenes.

- [MAST-VLA: Skill-Conditioned Adaptation with Trajectory-Affordance Priors for Multi-Skill Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32312) — Yang, Xu, Zhang et al.
  Models which primitive skill to execute and where/how to contact objects using skill-conditioned experts with trajectory-affordance priors; tested across diverse multi-skill manipulation. Validates trajectory-affordance priors as the structured spatial signal for multi-skill generalization — adjacent because SURF's V4 shape prior is a different spatial signal serving the same role. The multi-skill robustness test is a natural eval dimension SURF should address.

**horizon**

- [Neural signatures of associational cortex emerge in a goal-directed model of visual search](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe2b65fab39706ed38f49423aec299bc32a28972) — Pourrahimi & Bashivan · bioRxiv
  A goal-directed active-vision model develops V4/IT-like associational cortex signatures from task demands alone — without explicit cortical structure. Computational neuroscience grounding for SURF's active-perception framing: if goal-directedness is sufficient to produce the V4 signatures SURF uses as a prior, the biological motivation extends to task-conditioned selection more broadly, not just passive response properties.

- [Resolution as a First-Class Decision: Task-Conditioned Routing for Efficient Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34942) — Xia, Li, Zhang et al. · arXiv
  Treats input resolution as a task-conditioned routing decision rather than a static hyperparameter; routes high-resolution tokens only where needed. An upstream efficiency mechanism that bypasses downstream token pruning. Relevant as a competing paradigm: if task-conditioned resolution routing achieves SURF's efficiency goals without structural priors, the pre-encoder placement argument needs to hold for token-level (not resolution-level) allocation specifically.
