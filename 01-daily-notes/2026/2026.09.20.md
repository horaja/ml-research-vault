**core**

- [Attention from Action, for Action: Emergent Visual Bottlenecks for Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13422) — Zhuang et al. · arXiv
  "Seeker" learns task- and state-conditioned visual ROIs directly from action feedback, separating where-to-look from how-to-act without external ROI supervision. Fifth structural axis in the positioning note: SURF's geometric prior is data-free and pre-deployment-available; Seeker's action-learned bottleneck requires demonstration data but adapts to task dynamics. Critical read before the positioning note: does Seeker generalize to novel objects without retraining, and if not, that is SURF's differentiation window.

- [Beyond Attention or Similarity: Maximizing Conditional Diversity for Token Pruning in LVLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10967) — Zhang et al. · NeurIPS (95 cites)
  Defines conditional similarity between visual tokens given the instruction and reformulates token pruning as maximizing conditional diversity under the instruction. High-impact (NeurIPS, 95 cites). Provides an information-theoretic criterion for instruction-conditioned token selection that SURF should engage: do structurally-selected patches maximize conditional diversity under instruction? If yes, the geometric prior has a formal justification; if not, the selection objective needs to account for conditional diversity explicitly.

**adjacent**

- [AdaVSkip: Adaptive Visual Token Skipping Across Layers For Efficient MLLMs Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15131) — Sun et al. · arXiv
  Targets vertical (layer-wise) redundancy in visual tokens across transformer layers, distinct from SURF's horizontal (token-level) selection. Opens the composability question: layer-wise skipping and token-level selection address orthogonal redundancy axes. If SURF's structural prior selects the right tokens, AdaVSkip can reduce compute on those tokens further across layers.

- [GuidedAttention: Interpretable and Correctable Visual Attention for OOD-Robust Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21049) — Murooka et al. · arXiv
  Uses explicit attention guidance (annotated) to improve OOD robustness in robot visuomotor learning with a "correctable" mechanism at test time. Shares SURF's OOD differentiation axis but uses explicit supervision rather than structural priors. The "correctable" aspect — adjusting attention at inference — is relevant to [[claims/iterative selection]]: if the prior can be corrected using VL evidence, it's a two-stage approach.

- [ResPrune: Text-Conditioned Subspace Reconstruction for Visual Token Pruning in LVLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21105) — Li et al. · arXiv
  Training-free instruction-conditioned token pruning via reconstruction in a text-conditioned subspace. New technical baseline for SURF's structural prior approach: training-free and instruction-conditioned, but derives importance from reconstruction rather than geometry. Positioning note must include this alongside TADP and Compressor-VLA in the training-free baseline comparison.

**horizon**

- [Top-down perceptual inference shaping the activity of early visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6d34bc84826ed09fe3579539e126bfd8ef26ea8d) — Csikor et al. · bioRxiv (9 cites)
  Hierarchical deep generative model predicts top-down perceptual inference in early visual cortex: task-driven predictions propagate backward to modulate V1 activity, selecting which early features are amplified. Biological mechanism underlying [[claims/language helps]]: the task instruction (language) propagates backward through the visual hierarchy to gate which structural features are relevant. Grounds the design choice of language-conditioned structural prior over a purely bottom-up geometric approach.

- [A single computational objective can produce specialization of streams in visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f673f711af4d48c03135e75c4cf08a74fa30e653) — Finzi et al. · bioRxiv (7 cites)
  TDANN shows that ventral/dorsal visual stream specialization emerges from a single topographic self-supervised objective — no explicit stream-level supervision needed. Strengthens [[claims/v4 relevance]]: structural organization in visual cortex is an emergent property of metabolic constraints under task-driven optimization, not an architectural choice. A learned selector operating on unstructured visual tokens may not recover this organization without an explicit prior.
