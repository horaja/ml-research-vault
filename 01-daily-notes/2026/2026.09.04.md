**core**

- [GIFT: Guided Intermediate Feature Training via Action-Oriented Structural Supervision for Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04193v1) — Zheng et al. · arXiv
  Injects action-oriented structural supervision directly into intermediate VLA feature layers. Closest architectural competitor to SURF's structural prior: if action-aligned structural features can be trained end-to-end at intermediate layers, SURF's explicit prior module is a structural hypothesis about *where* to inject, not *whether* structure helps. Read before finalizing positioning note — may sharpen the injection-site framing in [[experiments/contrastive alignment]].

- [World-to-Wrist: Task-Conditioned Future Wrist Modeling for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/70002131c142ad387f1d7ff2291ea9f27672d562) — Pan et al. · arXiv
  Models the wrist-camera view as a task-conditioned future prediction from the main-view observation; improves fine-grained manipulation. View roles matter: SURF's structural prior applies to main-view patch selection; wrist-view may require a distinct (predictive, not structural) strategy. Informs multi-view budget allocation design and positioning note's view-decomposition framing.

- [CoFiE: Coarse-to-Fine Evidence Selection for Efficient Streaming Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03675v1) — Jiang et al. · arXiv
  Coarse-to-fine iterative token selection for VLMs on growing video streams under tight latency. Streaming video ≈ VLA closed-loop inference (growing observation context, fixed instruction, incremental action). Directly instantiates [[claims/iterative selection]] in a live inference loop; the two-pass design maps onto SURF's prior-then-refine architecture.

**adjacent**

- [Efficient Semantic Understanding from Digital Foveation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04088v1) — Caccavella et al. · arXiv
  Biologically-inspired foveation allocates compute non-uniformly across an image for semantic segmentation: high-resolution in task-relevant regions, coarse elsewhere. Foveation operates at the pixel/resolution level (sensor-side); SURF operates at the token level (representation-side). The two compose; provides a task-relevant foveation baseline for any SURF ablation that asks "does token pruning add beyond foveation?"

- [MINERVA: How Small Can a Manipulation Policy Be and Still Solve LIBERO?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03715v1) — Sendai et al. · arXiv
  Systematic capacity ablation on LIBERO; identifies minimum model size that achieves competitive performance, disaggregating vision encoder, language encoder, and action head. If LIBERO is solvable at low capacity, SURF's token pruning on large VLAs needs to run on genuinely capacity-demanding benchmarks to make the efficiency claim non-trivial. Sharpens which evaluations SURF must run.

**horizon**

- [Explicitly Modeling Subcortical Vision with a Neuro-Inspired Front-End Improves CNN Robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d2c2818f16c45693869453594661a79b40a38dd) — Piper et al. · NeurIPS
  LGN/V1-inspired subcortical processing (center-surround antagonism, orientation tuning) before the cortical feature hierarchy improves OOD robustness. Extends SURF's V4 biological grounding to earlier visual stages: SURF's line-density prior captures V1-like orientation structure, and this paper shows pre-V4 structure improves generalization. Biological rationale for why structural priors should improve held-out instruction-object performance, not just efficiency.

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — Zhao et al. · bioRxiv
  Eye-hand coordination in task-directed reaching is driven by task-relevant information extraction: gaze preferentially samples regions that reduce action uncertainty, not just end-effector proximity. Direct biological evidence for SURF's selective-visual-computation thesis: structural priors that capture affordance regions approximate gaze-sampling that minimizes action uncertainty. Updates [[claims/structure helps]].

## questions

Carried forward from 2026-09-03:
- Seeker: what does SURF's structural + language prior add beyond action-supervised ROI learning? Advantage candidate: data efficiency, held-out generalization, contact-region specificity not carried in action trajectory.
- ACTIVE-o3: does RL-induced active perception compose with SURF's structural prior, or supersede the architectural need for one?
- Retrieval Heads Meet Vision: should SURF's gate intervene at visual retrieval heads rather than encoder output? Implication for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]] injection architecture.
- VLA proprioceptive wiring: which wiring scheme (serialized text, projected prefix, direct to action expert) isolates SURF's structural contribution correctly?
- β-free IB: can β be parameterized as a function of task instruction and structural density, making SURF's patch budget adaptive without a manual hyperparameter?
- RayViT + SURF: does camera-ray geometry injection address the NuWa positional-frame erosion concern, composing with SURF's patch prior within the stabilized frame?
- S²Prune: does the spatial-bias finding (importance ≈ Uniform Grid) hold under VLA closed-loop settings with instruction-conditioned task-relevant regions, or only in static VQA?
- SlimVLM: is SURF's advantage over sensitivity-based pruning in per-patch task localization quality or held-out generalization?
