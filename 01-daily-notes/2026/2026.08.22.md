### digest (auto)

**core**

- [Decoding Task Progress from VLA Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13474) — Bhardwaj et al. · arXiv · 2026-08-13
  Probes the residual stream of π₀.5; finds task progress (normalized time remaining in episode) is linearly decodable from intermediate VLA layers. Directly answers "where in the VLA architecture does task-relevant information live?" — the blocking question for surf's contrastive scorer placement in the spec architecture section. If task-relevant signal is already encoded at a specific residual depth, surf's scorer should hook in there rather than at the visual encoder output. Also: if π₀.5 already encodes task progress internally, does surf's external contrastive scorer add genuinely new signal, or does it surface what the backbone already knows?

- [RayViT: Ray-Conditioned Visual Representations for Viewpoint-Robust Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29622) — Wang et al. · arXiv · 2026-07-31
  Injects camera ray geometry (3D ray direction per patch) into a frozen ViT backbone; achieves viewpoint-robust manipulation without policy retraining. Patch-level geometric conditioning rather than patch selection — a lightweight structural prior that operates in the same design space as [[claims/structure helps]] but via injection rather than pruning. Completes the structural-prior-for-VLA design landscape alongside GS-VLA (3D synthesis) and DepthCache (depth-guided merge ratios). Read to establish what geometry injection alone achieves; surf's structural prior must be positioned against this.

- [WCM: A World Critic Model for Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29613) — Fei et al. · arXiv · 2026-07-31
  World-model-based critic for VLA RL post-training: replaces single-frame value estimates with sequential VLA latents, addressing the POMDP mismatch of robot control. Relevant to surf's spec training section — if the value signal for "which visual tokens matter" requires multi-frame context (as WCM argues for action value), surf's iterative token scorer may also need history ([[claims/iterative selection]]). Pair with QWM (day 4) for the spec's training section.

**adjacent**

- [EXIMO: VLM Guided Exploration of VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19891) — Sukhija et al. · arXiv · 2026-08-20
  VLM-guided RL exploration for efficiently finetuning VLA to new tasks without new demonstrations. Uses VLM semantic understanding to generate diverse, informative exploration goals. Adjacent: surf's contrastive scorer conditions on the same language-task embeddings that EXIMO uses for exploration goal generation — shared embedding infrastructure, different purpose (exploration vs. visual selection). Relevant if surf's scorer training needs a curriculum of spatially diverse tasks.

- [AffordSim: A Scalable Data Generator and Benchmark for Affordance-Aware Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11674) — Li et al. · arXiv · 2026-04-13
  Scalable simulation benchmark for affordance-aware grasping: open-vocabulary 3D affordance prediction integrated into trajectory generation, with contact regions required to be task-semantically informed (not just stable grasps). Directly relevant to surf's open question: "are affordance/contact regions recoverable from structural cues alone?" This benchmark operationalizes that question and surf's structural prior could be evaluated against it (IoU between top-k line-density patches and ground-truth affordance regions on held-out tasks).

- [World-to-Wrist: Task-Conditioned Future Wrist Modeling for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05369) — Pan et al. · arXiv · 2026-08-05
  VLA that predicts how wrist-view (local, contact-region) observations will evolve under the global task context, then uses those predictions to guide fine-grained action. The task-conditioned future wrist view is a structural prediction of contact-relevant patches — the model implicitly learns "where manipulation will happen" from the task instruction. Relevant to [[claims/iterative selection]]: if future contact regions are predictable from task context, surf's iterative selection could use such predictions as a learned prior.

**horizon**

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — Zhao, Verdel, Tan, Burdet · bioRxiv · 2026-07-17
  Formalizes active vision during manipulation as dual stochastic MPC: eye movements minimize task-relevant uncertainty while building an internal model of the workspace. Characteristic saccade-pursuit patterns (fast fixations to grasping targets, smooth pursuit on moving objects) emerge from this information-gathering objective. Horizon framing: surf's structural prior is a one-shot hard-coded fast-path to what this model computes iteratively via active gaze. The information-dependent coordination result is also the computation [[eval/gaze]] should be compared against — not just whether surf selects patches humans fixate on, but whether it selects the patches that reduce task-relevant uncertainty.

## questions

- Decoding Task Progress × spec architecture: task progress is linearly decodable from intermediate VLA layers in π₀.5. Which layer? Does this generalize to OpenVLA or other backbones? If so, surf's contrastive scorer should hook in at (or before) that layer rather than at the visual encoder output — this changes the architecture section of the spec.
- RayViT × [[claims/structure helps]]: RayViT injects ray geometry per patch without selecting or dropping tokens; surf selects patches by line density. If RayViT achieves viewpoint robustness without selection, does the selection step add anything over conditioning? Or is surf's claim specifically about *efficiency* (fewer tokens), not viewpoint robustness?
- WCM × [[claims/iterative selection]]: WCM argues single-frame value estimates misalign with robot control's POMDP structure. Does the same argument apply to surf's per-frame token selection? If the most informative visual tokens depend on history (prior actions, prior observations), a history-conditioned scorer outperforms a single-frame one.
- AffordSim × [[claims/structure helps]]: AffordSim provides ground-truth contact regions for task-conditioned grasps. Can surf's line-density prior recover these regions without task conditioning (line prior only), and does language conditioning (surf's full scorer) improve recovery? This is the quantitative test of [[claims/structure helps]] and [[claims/language helps]] on a standardized benchmark.
- Cao et al. (day 4): still no abstract in S2 (Knowledge-Based Systems, 2026-10-01). Find the preprint. Mechanism is unknown — is it attention-based (same design space as SparseVLM+, AtVLA) or contrastive? The title exactly matches surf's core claim. If contrastive + patch-level, surf's geometric prior is the only differentiator.
