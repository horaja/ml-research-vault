**core**

- [TLC-DiT: Task-Aligned Local Visual Conditioning for Robust Multitask Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/519e831fb8ede93c653bc2b157a275badb65a10e) — Cai, Ichiwara, Wang · arXiv · 2026-09-28
  Adds an explicit task-aligned local visual channel alongside the global backbone in a diffusion-policy VLA — task-relevant local evidence is extracted explicitly rather than left buried in attention layers. This is the closest architectural prior to SURF's structural token selection: TLC-DiT's local channel is a hand-specified counterpart to SURF's learned structural selector. Read to extract: what defines "task-aligned" (instruction-matched, contact-region-matched, or learned?), and does the explicit channel improve OOD generalization independently of model size?

- [When Text Matters: Design Principles for Visual Token Pruning in Vision-Language Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e916b0358f6d215227c6296dcf5c84e0ed06e60e) — Kang, Park, Cho · arXiv · 2026-09-28
  Identifies conditions under which image-based selection overlooks task-relevant details and text-guided selection misses text–visual alignment; proposes design principles for when and how to combine both signals. For SURF: a principled empirical decomposition of the [[claims/language helps]] design space — exactly the question SURF's contrastive scoring stage is designed to answer. Read to extract: what conditions favor text-guided vs. image-based, and does the optimal design depend on task type (novel objects, spatial instructions, contact-rich)?

- [NeuronEye: Query-Guided Visual Concept Activation for Vision-Language Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38098v1) — Yan, Chen, Wan · arXiv · 2026-09-29
  Proposes query-guided visual concept disentanglement in VLM hidden states, explicitly separating object identity, spatial layout, and local attributes; inspired by sparse population coding and top-down modulation in biological vision. Biological motivation directly overlaps with [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if V4 acts as a top-down-modulated sparse code for shape/spatial layout, NeuronEye's mechanism is the computational analog SURF's structural prior approximates. Read to extract: does the disentanglement improve task-grounded token selection specifically (not just visual Q&A), and is the V4-like top-down modulation separable from the query-matching component?

**adjacent**

- [MVG-WAM: Multiple View Geometry-Aware World-Action Modeling for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37793v1) — Chen, Li, Xu · arXiv · 2026-09-29
  Makes geometric relationships among multi-view cameras explicit rather than implicit, connecting global scene context with local interaction geometry required for manipulation. MVG-WAM's argument parallels SURF's structural prior claim: implicit geometry (from scale or token concatenation) is insufficient to bridge scene context and local manipulation geometry. Adjacent to the InternW0-Δ capability threat: MVG-WAM adds explicit multi-view geometry; SURF adds explicit within-image structural geometry — both occupy the same "explicit > implicit" design axis.

- [Learning to Act under Visual Interruptions with Vision-Language-Action Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/93e20c0228b871716264a01dc727e575cd68d912) — Jiang, Xu, Wang · arXiv · 2026-09-28
  Studies VLAs that must continue acting when some camera streams are unavailable during task execution; forced visual budget from missing views. Operationalizes SURF's core premise under a hard constraint: not all visual evidence is necessary at every step. Check: does performance delta when losing the task-relevant camera (vs. an irrelevant one) quantify what SURF's selection criterion should maximize?

**horizon**

- [Cross-attention encoding models reveal dynamic spatiotemporal routing across human higher visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/14dc18c749982fdd46b996b213d217f976b8d972) — Inabathini, Henderson · bioRxiv · 2026-09-28
  Cross-attention DNN encoding models of fMRI responses in human higher visual cortex during natural video; finds dynamic spatiotemporal routing (not simple linear mapping) is required to explain cortical responses during action/event perception. Relevant to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if cortical routing during action perception is dynamic and attention-like, SURF's instruction-conditioned structural selector has a non-trivial biological analog rather than being a pure engineering heuristic. Check: does the cross-attention routing pattern align with known V4 shape/texture selectivity, or does it emerge only in higher-level (IT-level) areas?

- [Which Attention Heads are like the Human Head? Not the Ones that Compute](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37991v1) — Pinier, Opiełka, Rosenbusch · arXiv · 2026-09-29
  Brain-AI alignment (representational similarity to EEG) and causal computational role dissociate: most brain-aligned LLM attention heads are not causally involved in task computation. Direct methodological warning for [[claims/v4 relevance]]: high V4 feature alignment in a structural prior module does not imply the module performs V4-like computations — ablation tests are required, not alignment metrics alone.

## questions

- TLC-DiT: What defines "task-aligned" — instruction-matched, contact-region-matched, or learned? Does the explicit local channel improve OOD generalization independently of model size?
- When Text Matters: Under what conditions does text-guided pruning outperform image-based, and does the condition depend on task type (novel objects, spatial instructions, contact-rich)?
- NeuronEye: Does the V4-inspired disentanglement improve task-grounded token selection (vs. just visual Q&A), and is the top-down modulation separable from query-matching?
- MVG-WAM: Does SURF's single-image structural geometry proxy the multi-view geometric information MVG-WAM makes explicit — or is multi-view evidence required for manipulation geometry?
- Learning to Act under Visual Interruptions: Does the performance delta when losing the task-relevant camera quantify what SURF's selection criterion should maximize?
- Cross-attention cortex: Does the dynamic routing pattern align with V4 shape/texture selectivity, or does it emerge only in IT-level areas?
- Which Attention Heads: If brain-aligned heads are not causally effective, what ablation test would establish whether SURF's structural prior module performs V4-like computations?

- Decoupling VLA (Sun et al.): does a richer vision encoder improve policy performance independent of token count, and does the improvement hold under token budget constraints?
- Encoder-free scaling (Chen et al.): at what model/data scale does encoder-free become competitive, and does SURF's structural prior close the performance gap at constrained-compute regimes?
- GSPA: what is the "geo" component — depth, edge, learned geometry? (day 107 unread)

- Does temporal accumulation add independent signal over a static structural prior?
