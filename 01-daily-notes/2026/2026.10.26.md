- [Beyond Attention or Similarity: Maximizing Conditional Diversity for Token Pruning in MLLMs](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3b23dd0634e7de523598df0a907a5bf558eb9c88) — Zhang et al. · NeurIPS

- [Compact Robot Policies Need Fine-Grained Visual Representations](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/60ec1e5d7adce0108658ac2eaa1af20eb2df5657) — Chen et al. · preprint
  Controlled comparison isolating visual representation quality from policy architecture shows fine-grained features drive multi-task manipulation performance, independent of model scale. Grounds SURF's core motivation: selective patching must preserve fine-grained structural detail, not just coarse semantic content.

**adjacent**

- [RayViT: Ray-Conditioned Visual Representations for Viewpoint-Robust Imitation Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8009e0138c252bd117f45b37e49d31eff0314467) — Wang et al. · arXiv
  Conditions visual features on camera rays (3D geometry) rather than RGB appearance; achieves viewpoint robustness for imitation learning without 3D reconstruction. Positions SURF against a 3D geometric alternative: camera-ray conditioning vs. 2D line-drawing prior as the structural signal.

- [When Listening Becomes Easier: Scrubbing Visual Cues for Shortcut-Free VLAs](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/650d08f97f97623f1950b175e4621cca8d6e38b6) — Gerigk et al. · preprint
  Identifies and removes visual shortcut cues from manipulation datasets; shows policies exploit spurious visual correlations even under language conditioning. Live risk for SURF: the line-drawing prior may inadvertently encode or amplify visual shortcuts if structural edges co-vary with task identity in training data.

- [PosA-VLA: Enhancing Action Generation via Pose-Conditioned Anchor Attention](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d77a24298f448ff9f6aeabec20137aebeb4534e9) — Li et al. · arXiv
  Uses estimated human/robot pose as spatial anchor for cross-attention, concentrating VLA computation on pose-relevant visual regions. Alternative structural signal to SURF's line drawings: embodied pose geometry vs. scene object geometry as the selection cue.

**horizon**

- [Explicitly Modeling Subcortical Vision with a Neuro-Inspired Front-End Improves CNN Robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d2c2818f16c45693869453594661a79b40a38dd) — Piper et al. · NeurIPS
  Adding a biologically-grounded retina+LGN front-end to CNNs improves OOD robustness and reduces adversarial vulnerability. Places SURF's V4-derived mid-level prior in the cortical hierarchy: if pre-V4 subcortical processing already filters nuisance variation, the geometric prior's role may be specifically the V4 shape-over-texture selection step rather than general structural preprocessing.

- [Human Scanpath Prediction in Target-Present Visual Search with Semantic-Foveal Bayesian Attention](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/60a3c0c0ba874320ce6cd50ab6de7e634d06004e) — Luzio et al. · ICDL
  Bayesian foveal attention model combining semantic top-down and bottom-up cues predicts human scanpaths in goal-directed search tasks. Normative framing for SURF's prior module: if human task-directed gaze is Bayesian inference over foveal uncertainty, SURF's selector is implementing a learned approximation to this optimal policy.
