# 2026-10-14

## opener

Positioning note is day 50. No new structural blockers.

Three threads converge today. First, **Spa-Bench** introduces the first real-robot benchmark specifically targeting spatial grounding in VLAs — evaluating how reliably VLA policies translate spatial language ("to the left of," "behind") into correct robot behavior. This is the evaluation framework SURF has been missing: a benchmark that tests precisely the spatial precision SURF's structural prior is designed to improve. If the benchmark shows current VLAs fail at spatial grounding under language conditioning, SURF's structural prior is the direct intervention.

Second, **ResPrune** joins TADP, FSR, Conditional Diversity, and When and What to Prune as the sixth training-free text-conditioned token pruning method in the competitor cluster. The cluster is now large enough that the positioning note must make a regime claim: SURF's geometric prior occupies a specific regime (novel object geometry, zero-shot structural coverage) that text-subspace reconstruction and language-attention scoring cannot reach without having seen similar objects. This is now the organizing claim for the positioning note, not just a contingency.

Third, **Gemini 4 Argon for Robotics** landed today from DeepMind — a frontier model applied directly to robotics. This is the capability baseline the field will compare against; SURF needs to articulate what geometric structural selection adds when the backbone is a frontier model that already encodes strong spatial priors.

Cao et al. is day 15. Read today.

- [ResPrune: Text-Conditioned Subspace Reconstruction for Visual Token Pruning in Large Vision-Language Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d8d6855727d59b8f55efe9cade6191b073037d6a) — Li et al. · IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology
  Training-free pruning: selects tokens by greedy subspace expansion guided by residual energy in the attention subspace defined by the text query. Extends the training-free text-conditioned competitor cluster (TADP, FSR, Conditional Diversity, When and What to Prune). Key question for positioning note: does ResPrune's attention-subspace-residual criterion select the same tokens as SURF's geometric line-drawing prior on manipulation scenes with novel objects? Divergence → geometric prior captures structure that text-subspace reconstruction cannot recover without having seen similar appearance.

- [COMPASS: Confined-space Manipulation Planning with Active Sensing Strategy](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/72518e2fed76e4eac240c28825cd4e919374d01f) — Li et al. · ICRA
  Multi-stage exploration and manipulation under partial observability: near-field awareness scan builds a local collision map, then a multi-objective utility function finds viewing positions, then the robot acts. The sensing-before-acting loop under partial observability is architecturally parallel to SURF's [[02-projects/surf/claims/iterative selection]]: both commit visual budget to sensing until sufficient spatial evidence is obtained. Check whether COMPASS formalizes the sensing/acting budget tradeoff in a way that maps onto SURF's stopping criterion design.

**adjacent**

- [Investigating action topography in visual cortex and deep artificial neural networks](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c87a943995f33c4efc8a3b64d9c2358e3d314649) — Cortinovis, Truong, Op de Beeck, Bracci · bioRxiv
  fMRI finds a topographically-organized action gradient in left lateral occipitotemporal cortex, with overlapping activations for bodies, hands, tools, and manipulable objects. Topographic DANNs partially capture this gradient. Directly relevant to [[02-projects/surf/claims/v4 relevance]]: if action topography organizes high-level visual cortex (not just shape/animacy/size), then V4-like structural columns may be upstream of action encoding — strengthening the argument that a V4-grounded structural prior selects action-relevant patches rather than arbitrary shape features.

- [Visual Tokens on Demand: Visual Budgeting for Scalable and Efficient Multi-Image Reasoning in Multimodal LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18858v1) — Rahman et al. · arXiv
  Allocates token budgets dynamically across multiple images based on query relevance, treating multi-image reasoning as a resource allocation problem under a total token budget. The "visual budget" framing and dynamic allocation mechanism are architecturally adjacent to SURF's adaptive compute budget — though SURF focuses on intra-image patch selection for manipulation rather than cross-image budgeting, the per-image budget-query interaction is the same design question. Check whether the allocation policy is differentiable and compatible with a geometric structural prior over patches.

**horizon**

- [TDSNNs: Competitive Topographic Deep Spiking Neural Networks for Visual Cortex Modeling](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5f720e2aa2eb214f3c7bdf8a0bad0c8a01202f54) — Zhou et al. · AAAI
  SNNs with competitive lateral inhibition develop topographic organization matching primate visual cortex better than ANNs, specifically because SNNs capture spike-based temporal dynamics that ANNs neglect. Horizon: topographic organization + temporal dynamics are jointly necessary for visual cortex fidelity; SURF's iterative selection has a temporal dimension (selection changes across inference steps), and TDSNN's temporal competition mechanism is a candidate implementation for a recurrent structural prior that refines its selection over time.

- [What Matters in Quality-Aware VLA Pretraining: A Benchmark and Empirical Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18869v1) — arXiv
  Compares weighting, filtering, and conditioning on demonstration quality (reward definitions, label granularity) across 5,000 suboptimal human demonstrations and 24 target tasks. Horizon: when SURF's contrastive prior requires training data, the quality-conditioning strategy will interact directly with how the prior learns to distinguish task-relevant structural patches. The finding that conditioning outperforms filtering at low quality ratios is a practical signal for contrastive alignment training data curation.
