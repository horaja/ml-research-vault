## opener

Thursday, July 30. Contrastive alignment spec unwritten — 24 weeks. GSPA unread — day 47. "When Attention Collapses" (STS) unread — day 36. Visual Access Boundaries unread — day 16.

**CheckVLA** (Liu et al.) proposes execution-time verification for long-horizon VLA: the model commits to an action chunk, and a world model continuously monitors whether the actual visual observation matches the predicted trajectory; mismatch exceeding a threshold triggers replanning. This is a deployable implementation of the [[claims/iterative selection]] trigger mechanism that the spec has not yet specified. Four trigger proposals now converge: KEMO's event-state detector (7/27), Csikor's prediction-error threshold (7/28), Huang/Wang's latent-state uncertainty (7/29), and CheckVLA's world-model visual mismatch (today). The spec must pick one, argue against the others, or show they are equivalent. CheckVLA's formulation is the most concrete: the trigger is the discrepancy between predicted and observed visual tokens — directly measurable, no additional architecture required if surf already has a world-model head.

**RoboInter1.5** (Wang et al.) provides dense per-frame intermediate representations for manipulation including contact heatmaps, affordances, depth, flow, and scene graphs. Contact heatmaps are exactly the co-location test target for [[claims/structure helps]] that so far required constructing from ViTacWorld / RoboPCA / DeVA separately. If RoboInter1.5's contact heatmaps are available on LIBERO frames, the co-location test collapses to a single dense comparison: surf line-density vs. ground-truth contact heatmap.

Write the spec.

- [CheckVLA: Execution-Time Verification with Action-Conditioned World Model for Long-Horizon Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26789v1) — Liu et al. · arXiv 2026-07-29
  Verifies open-loop VLA execution using an action-conditioned world model: predicted vs. actual visual observations are compared continuously; mismatch triggers replanning. For [[claims/iterative selection]]: this is the prediction-error stopping criterion in concrete VLA form — compare with KEMO's event-detector (7/27), Csikor's prediction-error threshold (7/28), and Huang/Wang's latent-state tracker (7/29) to determine which trigger signal is cheapest and most reliable at LIBERO task phase boundaries.

- [RoboInter1.5: A Holistic Intermediate Representation Suite for Embodied World Modeling and Robotic Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e8ee8d0c9963914602691fdd699a6e29) — Wang et al. · S2 2026-07-21

- [Route by Kinematics, Act by Observation: Kinematics-Supervised Expert Routing in MoE-Augmented VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26807v1) — Yang et al. · arXiv 2026-07-29
  MoE-VLA routing trained on pseudo-kinematic labels extracted from LLM intermediate features; at inference routes by observation only. Key finding: semantic tokens in VLA cluster by kinematic structure, not instruction semantics. For [[claims/language helps]]: if the language-conditioned routing is primarily learning task kinematics, surf's geometric structural prior may approximate the same signal more cheaply — or language adds orthogonal cross-task generalization that kinematics alone cannot provide. Diagnostic: does kinematic routing and surf's contrastive scorer select overlapping LIBERO tokens?

**adjacent**

- [TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27205v1) — Xie et al. · arXiv 2026-07-29
  Achieves 32 Hz real-time VLA control on a single RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM via a V→A pathway shortcut that bypasses the LLM for high-frequency actions; language kept for slow semantic reasoning. For [[claims/efficiency without collapse]]: TurboVLA's efficiency gain is architectural (pathway shortcut), not token-based. Surf's differentiation question: does token selection compound with the V→A shortcut, or do they address orthogonal costs?

- [Enfold: Folding World-Generator Computation into Predictive Representations for Efficient Embodied Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26657v1) — Zeng et al. · arXiv 2026-07-29
  Distills intermediate world-model computations (not world-model outputs) into the encoder's predictive representation at training time, enabling embodied control without world-model rollouts at inference. Adjacent to [[claims/iterative selection]]: Enfold amortizes inference-time compute via training-time distillation; surf's budget policy reallocates it at inference time. Orthogonality test: do they target the same patches, or different computational bottlenecks?

**horizon**

- [Expected Free Energy as Belief-Dependent Utility for rho-POMDPs](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/132f12e0ead650e26ff2efc8431eef1d) — Cooper · S2 2026-07-18
  Proves that minimizing expected free energy (EFE) is equivalent to solving a rho-POMDP whose utility equals expected information gain plus expected reward; gives a closed-form criterion for when gathering additional observations is worthwhile. For surf's budget policy: the rho-POMDP utility function is the principled objective for iterative patch allocation — gather tokens until EFE(next patch) < allocation cost. Pairs with Huang/Wang latent-state tracker (7/29) as the quantity to monitor for this criterion.

## questions

- CheckVLA × [[claims/iterative selection]]: CheckVLA triggers replanning via visual-observation mismatch. KEMO (7/27) uses state-transition events. Csikor (7/28) uses prediction-error threshold in early visual areas. Huang/Wang (7/29) uses latent-state uncertainty. Can these four trigger signals be ordered by compute cost vs. reliability at LIBERO task boundaries? Which is the right abstraction for surf's budget policy — and do they converge to the same decision at contact events?
- RoboInter1.5 × [[claims/structure helps]]: RoboInter1.5 provides dense contact heatmaps. Are these available for LIBERO or DROID? If yes, the co-location test becomes a direct dense comparison — surf line-density heatmap vs. RoboInter1.5 contact heatmap — replacing the piecemeal proxy approach via ViTacWorld, RoboPCA, and DeVA.
- Route by Kinematics × [[claims/language helps]]: KSER shows VLA semantic tokens cluster by kinematic structure. Does surf's language-conditioned scorer activate on the same token clusters as kinematic routing? If yes, language conditioning is approximating task kinematics; if no, it is adding cross-task instruction generalization that kinematic structure does not provide.
- TurboVLA × [[claims/efficiency without collapse]]: TurboVLA's V→A bypass achieves efficiency via pathway shortcut before the LLM processes visual tokens. If surf's token selection reduces visual input to the LLM path, does it compound with TurboVLA's shortcut or does TurboVLA's shortcut make the LLM-path visual tokens irrelevant?
- Enfold × [[claims/iterative selection]]: Enfold pre-computes world-model state into representations, eliminating inference-time rollouts. Does this subsume what surf's iterative reallocation provides — or do they target orthogonal costs (Enfold: world-model rollout cost; surf: visual encoding cost per patch)?
- Cooper rho-POMDP × [[claims/iterative selection]]: The rho-POMDP budget criterion says gather patches until EFE < cost. What is surf's "observation cost" per patch (GFLOPs of processing one more token), and what quantity proxies "expected information gain" — attention entropy, prediction error, or Huang/Wang's latent-state uncertainty?
