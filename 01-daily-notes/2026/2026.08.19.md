  QWM: world-model-guided test-time search over imagined trajectories on top of Q-learning for VLA. **adjacent**

- [OV3D-Bench: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Open-Vocabulary Monocular 3D Detection](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/89c68096e7a98cc89d78fe0d98dd6d1235439e49) — Gladkova, Peri, Ramanan et al. · S2 · 2026-08-17
  From Ramanan group (tracked author). Decouples open-vocabulary 3D detection into localization accuracy, semantic robustness, and cross-domain transfer. Key finding: models localize objects well but mislabel semantically adjacent categories; a frozen closed-vocabulary detector remapped via SigLIPv2 contrastive embeddings is competitive with purpose-built open-vocab methods. Adjacent to surf: geometric localization is the more tractable axis than semantic conditioning — surf's structural prior operates on the localization side, which this finding suggests is already comparatively mature. The contrastive-VLM-as-semantic-head design is a reference for surf's scorer architecture.

- [Reinforcement Learning for Multimodal Foundation Models: A Survey](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/443bbd0160ff39bd659da0e796f0e4a89aa2d7a2) — Wu et al. · S2 · 2026-08-12
  Comprehensive survey (200+ works) covering RL+VLM: RLHF → verifiable reward paradigms, PPO → GRPO, spanning VLMs, visual generation, unified models, and VLAs. Field-orientation reading for surf's training section. Covers curriculum-driven training, preference-aligned diffusion, and unified reward modeling — these are the training paradigms surf's contrastive scorer training operates within. Skim the VLA pillar.

- [Thinking outside the frame: viewpoint-aware spatial reasoning in large multimodal models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2712b482e221543467b6e9a41dc44bed144be3b0) — Ke, Shiri, Zhang, Yu · S2 · 2026-08-12
  LMMs show systematic egocentric bias in spatial reasoning; lightweight viewpoint-aware self-correction (low token overhead) improves allocentric reasoning. Adjacent to surf: the egocentric failure mode is exactly the spatial context loss that surf's structural prior is designed to prevent in manipulation settings. If LMMs already self-correct viewpoint bias with low overhead, the argument for an external structural prior needs to be refined — or surf's prior operates at a finer granularity (patch-level geometric structure) that self-correction doesn't address.

**horizon**

- [Predictive processing as a scalable computational principle for embodied multitask intelligence](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/29e12a0e6c1e29b79e245665bfdd986bb5190547) — Idei, Miyake, Ogata, Yamashita · S2 · 2026-08-14
  Hierarchical recurrent neural network under the free-energy principle, handling 30,000-dimensional visuo-proprioceptive input for humanoid robot multitask without dimensionality reduction. Emergent properties: self-organized task transition dynamics, robustness to vision degradation via multimodal integration, asymmetric multitask interference. Horizon framing for surf: if the brain's visual allocation during manipulation is governed by a predictive processing loop (attend where prediction error is highest), surf's structural prior is a hard-coded substitute for the predictive model's attention signal. The multimodal robustness to vision degradation is a concrete test of whether structural priors can substitute during occluded or low-quality visual observation.

## questions

- Cao et al. VT sparsification vs. surf: is the mechanism attention-score-based (same as SparseVLM+, AtVLA) or something new? Does it use language at the token level, the frame level, or the sub-instruction level? If it's another attention-salience approach, surf's line-density geometric prior is still differentiated; if it's contrastive, the differentiation is gone.
- QWM × [[claims/iterative selection]]: QWM's test-time world-model search selects actions, not visual tokens. Can the same imagined-rollout mechanism identify which visual tokens matter for high-Q actions? This would unify surf's adaptive visual budget with QWM's adaptive action selection into a single value-of-information objective.
- s2_author recovery: were today's 2 papers actually recent (Finn published QWM 2026-08-17; Ramanan published OV3D-Bench 2026-08-17)? If so, the sort-order bug is still present but both happened to be in the top-100 by citation. Verify by checking if other tracked authors who published this week surfaced.
- OV3D-Bench × structural prior: if geometric localization is mature while semantic recognition is the bottleneck, does surf's structural prior address the wrong axis? Counter-argument: in manipulation, geometric localization at the patch level is NOT mature — predicting which patches contain contact surfaces is unsolved. The claim needs to be stated at the right granularity.
- Viewpoint-aware self-correction × structural prior: Ke et al.'s token overhead is "low" — if LMMs can correct egocentric bias without an external structural prior, what does surf's prior add that self-correction cannot? Testable: compare surf's selected patches against the residual error cases that self-correction fails on.
- Carried from 8/18: gaze-vs-outcome divergence (Kerkouri); instruction granularity for scorer (Hybrid-Level Injection); trigger mechanism for iterative refinement; texture bias in VLMs and structural prior motivation.
