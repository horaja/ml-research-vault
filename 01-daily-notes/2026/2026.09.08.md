**core**

- [PosA-VLA: Enhancing Action Generation via Pose-Conditioned Anchor Attention](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d77a24298f448ff9f6aeabec20137aebeb4534e9) — arXiv 2025-12-03
  Attributes redundant VLA motions to "spatially uniform perception field" — exactly the problem SURF targets — and anchors visual attention via pose-conditioned supervision toward task-relevant regions. This is a sixth signal in SURF's comparison set (end-effector pose as the prior); the positioning note must now address: when does structural line-drawing geometry predict task-relevant regions more accurately than pose, and does it add independent signal when pose is available? Sharpens [[claims/structure helps]]: structure must contribute beyond the pose-implied spatial constraint.

- [PHR-VLA: Planning Horizon Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/360ceca3bbc857a03e40128ac8903b4b6d83d837) — arXiv 2026-08-27
  Lightweight auxiliary future-dynamics head conditions VLA action generation on privileged latent representations of predicted future task state, improving contact-rich manipulation. Directly sharpens the open question from "What Matters, When?": if visual localization targets shift at phase transitions and a spatial prior does not see future task state, it may select wrong patches at exactly those moments. PHR-VLA's mechanism is orthogonal (future-dynamics conditioning of action, not spatial selection), but establishes a concrete temporal conditioning baseline against which SURF's phase-transition behavior should be ablated.

- [RoboSPA: Can VLA Models Go Beyond Simple Scenes and Short-Horizon Tasks?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05324v1) — arXiv 2026-09-04
  Benchmark for fine-grained spatial reasoning and procedural complexity in VLA, diagnosing failure modes under increasing scene difficulty beyond LIBERO. Critical for experiment selection: MINERVA (09-07) showed 0.54M parameters solves LIBERO, making LIBERO results insufficient to differentiate SURF from trivially compact baselines; RoboSPA is the spatial-procedural complexity axis where a structural prior should show advantage. Informs target benchmark for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]].

**adjacent**

- [Token-Budget Distillation: Transferring Full-Token Semantics to Compressed Video Vision-Language Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4645400e0724e041cf877eb5e80801d5982ec852) — arXiv 2026-08-28
  PEFT framework (LoRA + FlashVID compression) for adapting video VLMs under fixed token budget; shows direct compression degrades semantics and distillation from a full-token teacher recovers performance. Adjacent to SURF's efficiency argument: if task-relevant patch preservation reduces compression-induced semantic drift, SURF's structural prior may improve the distillation signal rather than replace it — a training-scheme interaction worth tracking for the efficiency without collapse claim.

- [VANE: Reliable Test-Time Training for VLAs via Future Visual Representation Prediction](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e786d8f682e51a335c860a9f00cfd807b9e8c506) — arXiv 2026-08-10
  Test-time training for VLA: adapts per vision-language context and learns from future visual consequences of executed actions, preventing shared adaptation from mixing incompatible task corrections. Adjacent to SURF's training-vs-inference decomposition: if SURF's prior can be expressed as a lightweight context-conditioned adapter (VANE-style), it could be adapted at test time without full retraining — alternative deployment path to baking the prior into weights during training ("Reasoning Without Inference Cost", 09-07).

**horizon**

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — bioRxiv 2026-07-17
  Dual stochastic MPC formulation in which eye movements continuously minimize task-relevant uncertainty to construct an internal model for hand movement planning; reproduces saccade-pursuit patterns from first principles. Biological grounding for SURF's active-perception framing: the computational principle here is that gaze is uncertainty-minimizing for action — not saliency-driven or structure-driven — so SURF's structural prior should be evaluated against whether line-drawing features predict high task-relevant uncertainty, not just high visual saliency.

- [RoboPCA: Pose-centered Affordance Learning from Human Demonstrations for Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9c6b8a6eef623256c41c33000fb6cd4da1ffeadd) — arXiv 2026-03-08
  Jointly predicts contact regions and contact poses from language instructions; outperforms decoupled contact-then-pose pipelines on generalization across tasks and categories. Horizon update for the contact-geometry thread (One Demonstration Many Objects, 09-05): instruction-conditioned contact localization is solvable without an explicit structural prior, but RoboPCA's joint contact-pose prediction implies the relevant prior is orientation-sensitive (region + approach direction), which may favor SURF's line-drawing representation over patch-only spatial priors.

- "Attention from Action": when action-derived ROIs (trajectory-based) and structure-derived ROIs (line-drawing-based) disagree on patch placement, which better predicts downstream policy performance?
