**core**

- [AVA-VLA: Improving Vision-Language-Action models with Active Visual Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18960) — Xiao et al. · arXiv 2025 (16 cites)
  Reformulates VLA as POMDP and adds a recurrent state that aggregates visual history — prior timesteps' spatial attention maps — to condition action generation. SURF's structural prior operates within a single frame (spatial); AVA-VLA's prior operates across frames (temporal). The two are orthogonal, but if temporal visual attention history captures which spatial regions have been attended to over the episode, it may subsume SURF's single-frame structural prior in tasks where manipulation phase resolves spatial ambiguity. Read before finalizing positioning note — AVA-VLA is nine months old with 16 cites and has not appeared in any recent digest.

- [AIM: Intent-Aware Unified World Action Modeling with Spatial Value Maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11135) — Fan et al. · arXiv 2026 (10 cites)
  Bridges video generation priors and action decoding via an explicit spatial value map encoding "where to interact." The value map is a learned, task-conditioned spatial interface — closest conceptual competitor to SURF's structural prior in the "spatial prior for action" design space: task-conditioned value map vs. data-free line-drawing structural prior. If AIM's value maps cover in-domain task-relevant geometry, SURF's structural prior must prove its edge in data-efficiency, out-of-domain generalization, or zero-shot deployment. Enters the comparison set as a learned-spatial-prior baseline.

- [UniMPA: A Unified Memory-Prediction-Action Model via Action-Grounded Transition Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11875) — Li et al. · arXiv 2026 (0 cites)
  Addresses "transition ambiguity" — visually similar observations at different manipulation phases implying different subsequent transitions — via action-grounded latent prediction. Directly targets the same phase-transition gap raised by PHR-VLA (09-08). If phase state can be internally modeled via action-grounded transitions, SURF's structural prior must do more than phase disambiguation alone. Adds another datapoint to the spatial vs. temporal prior decomposition in the positioning note.

**adjacent**

- [Caption-once, Frames-on-Demand: Visual-Need Routing for Budget-Aware Agentic Long Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11899) — Cai et al. · arXiv 2026 (0 cites)
  Routes visual frame requests dynamically: language memories handle temporal structure, visual pixels are fetched only for attribute-level queries that language cannot answer. The visual-need routing decision is a learned temporal adaptive budget in video agents. Maps directly onto SURF's "when to stop refining" open question in closed-loop VLA inference: the visual-textual duality here may formalize the condition under which SURF's structural prior should trigger a patch refinement step vs. defer to instruction memory.

- [FARM: Reading Failure Signals from the Internal Predictive States of a Frozen Robotic World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11445) — Pei et al. · arXiv 2026 (0 cites)
  A 33k-parameter probing head trained on top of a frozen world model's internal predictive states decodes task failure without requiring dedicated monitoring components or modifying the backbone. Evidence that task-relevant signals are recoverable from VLA internals via lightweight probing — directly adjacent to SURF's adaptive compute module: if task progress and failure are decodable, SURF's structural prior could be conditioned on probed internal state (rather than only on instruction) to gate spatial attention adaptively.

**horizon**

- [End-to-end topographic networks as models of cortical map formation and human visual behaviour](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4b8bf7404003befc7d91df4fd193b812a8083c) — Lu et al. · Nature Human Behaviour 2025 (27 cites)
  All-Topographic Neural Networks (All-TNNs) learn smooth orientation- and category-selectivity maps, center-surround receptive fields, and topographic spatial organization end-to-end — without hard-coded convolutional inductive biases. Topographic organization emerges from task-pressure alone. Directly relevant to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if topographic map formation is learnable from task pressure rather than architectural hardwiring, a VLM fine-tuned on structural line-drawing priors might develop patch-level topographic selectivity organically. Strongest published evidence that the V4 topographic structure argument for SURF is grounded in a learnable and emergent property, not a biological artifact. Likely worth reading alongside @jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying.

## questions

Carried forward from 2026-09-10:
- CDPruner: if conditional diversity maximization already accounts for instruction-relevance, does SURF's structural prior improve the diversity pool (complementary), or provide redundant localization (competing)?
- History-conditioned VLN pruning: does temporal history conditioning subsume structural prior conditioning for spatial patch selection, or are the two orthogonal?
- DeCAL: does contact-aware latent co-imagination at inference time make SURF's patch-selection prior redundant for contact-rich tasks, or do the two compose?
- Seeker: what does SURF's structural + language prior add beyond action-supervised ROI learning?
- ACTIVE-o3: does RL-induced active perception compose with SURF's structural prior, or supersede the architectural need for one?
- S²Prune: does the spatial-bias finding hold under VLA closed-loop settings with instruction-conditioned task-relevant regions?
- PosA-VLA: does SURF's line-drawing prior add localization signal beyond what end-effector pose implies?
- PHR-VLA: should SURF's structural prior be conditioned on predicted future state to correctly localize patches at phase transitions?

New from today:
- AVA-VLA: if recurrent temporal visual attention (across-frame) handles partial observability and phase disambiguation, what spatial information does SURF's single-frame structural prior add that the temporal prior does not already capture?
- AIM: if learned spatial value maps (from video generation priors) capture task-relevant where-to-interact geometry in-domain, on what axis (data-efficiency, out-of-domain generalization, inference cost) does SURF's structural line-drawing prior differentiate?
- Caption-once, Frames-on-Demand: does their visual-need routing condition (attribute-level queries require pixels; temporal queries use language) formalize SURF's "when to refine spatially" open question — and if so, what is the VLA analog of the routing condition?
