# 2026-10-04

## opener

Positioning note is day 40. Two new direct VLA token-selection competitors appear today that must enter the baseline table before the note circulates.

Zhuang et al. (Attention from Action) shows that label-free visual bottlenecks focused on task-relevant ROIs can emerge from policy gradient training alone, without structural labels or external spatial supervision. This reframes SURF's positioning: structural priors are a training-free substitute for learned ROI masks, not a fundamentally different type of signal. The claim SURF must make explicit is that structural priors generalize to new tasks and scenes without per-task training, while emergent bottlenecks require task-specific RL rollouts to acquire. Write this into the positioning note's competitor table alongside GeoScaffold and GSPA.

TADP (Jianping Lin et al.) is a training-free dynamic VLA token pruning framework that estimates task-conditioned importance after early vision-language interaction, then decouples manipulation-relevant from manipulation-irrelevant tokens. This is the closest published analog to SURF's full pipeline: training-free, VLA-targeted, task-conditioned. The key architectural difference is TADP uses cross-modal VL attention as its token importance signal; SURF uses a structural geometric prior. If TADP's cross-modal attention already captures the spatial structure SURF argues for, [[claims/structure helps]] requires direct ablation evidence that geometric priors outperform cross-modal attention-based selection at the same compression ratio.

Zhu (Depth Matters) uses depth maps as geometric signal for contrastive language-visual representation learning in manipulation. Different geometric modality from SURF (depth vs. line drawings), but same claim type: language-conditioned geometric representation improves manipulation. Check whether depth-map and line-drawing priors activate on the same patches in contact-rich scenes — if they do, one is a substitute; if not, these are complementary geometric modalities.

**adjacent**

- [Uncertainty-Aware Sequential Decision Rules for Event-Triggered LLM Invocation in Streaming Systems](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/24794495a8136c1c5ca42583c5e5dc8e5abeda7a) — Zhao-Hui Wang · arXiv
  Risk-based sequential stopping problem for when to invoke an expensive LLM; trigger policy fires when a risk functional over observation history exceeds a threshold. Formal treatment of the adaptive-compute triggering problem. SURF's efficiency/RL module needs a triggering criterion for when to expand the token budget; this paper provides a decision-theoretic framework (risk over observation history) as a candidate formalism. Not robotics-specific — the formalism is task-agnostic.

- [Investigating Action Topography in Visual Cortex and Deep Artificial Neural Networks](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c87a943995f33c4efc8a3b64d9c2358e3d314649) — Davide Cortinovis et al. · bioRxiv
  Action categories organize topographic maps in high-level visual cortex (fMRI) and in topographic DANNs; action-category topography is a stable organizing principle alongside object-category topography. Extends the biological grounding for [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if action categories shape V4 topographic organization, SURF's token selection must preserve the spatial layout needed for action-topographic signals, not only object-category signals. Composability question with Zhuang et al.: do learned action-derived ROI masks align with action-topographic organization in V4-like models?

**horizon**
