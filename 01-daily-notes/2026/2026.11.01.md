**core**

- [Don't Ask the LLM: Visual Token Pruning with Spectral Structure and Semantic Relevance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38303) — Shin et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  CLS attention produces background-biased, scattered selections under aggressive compression; query-conditioned language signals become available only later in the network. Fix: spectral structure (frequency-domain image features, no LLM) combined with semantic relevance forms complementary signals. Frames SURF's architecture as correct — structural priors handle early-layer selection, language conditioning adds task specificity. Key check: does spectral structure overlap with line-drawing selection? If yes, line drawings are a computable proxy for frequency structure; if line drawings additionally capture affordance-relevant geometry beyond frequency content, the prior adds information spectral analysis misses.

- [PhysVLMM: Interaction-Conditioned Physical Belief Maps for Articulated Objects in Robot Scene Maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40539) — Ghosh · arXiv Oct 2026
  Maintains a posterior over physical affordance per articulated part — tracking how each part responds to force rather than just where it is. Watching alone cannot identify dynamics; only measured interaction resolves the posterior. Competing prior type: physics-conditioned spatial belief vs. SURF's structure-conditioned prior. The union question: do structural edges consistently identify regions that are also physically affordant, or does physics-conditioned selection fill a gap structure misses?

- [BURGER: Benchmarking and Understanding Robotic Generalization via Embodied Drawing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40532) — Xu et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  VLA benchmark for language-conditioned generalization via scalable simulation of robotic drawing tasks. Drawing requires spatial language understanding + fine-grained motor control — both capabilities SURF's structural prior targets. Scalable prompt-driven pipeline could supply diverse instruction-conditioned trajectories for eval. Check: does structural salience in the scene guide the arm toward the correct drawing region, and does SURF's prior improve that grounding?

**adjacent**

- [SpaceAct: Benchmarking 3D Spatial and Temporal Reasoning via Closed-Loop Action and Active Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38405) — Shi et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Tests 3D geometry reconstruction from occluded 2D views, physical dynamics anticipation, and closed-loop action execution. SURF's structural prior operates on 2D patch selection; SpaceAct tests 3D spatial reasoning SURF may not address. Important to track: if evaluation in the field moves toward 3D reasoning tasks, the scope of SURF's 2D prior must be explicitly bounded.

- [ASPEN: Adaptive Semantic Reasoning via Command Persistence Prediction for Efficient Navigation VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36285) — Akustia, Hu · arXiv Oct 2026
  Skips full semantic reasoning when a navigation instruction is stable. Command persistence prediction as an event trigger for recomputation. Directly adjacent to [[claims/iterative selection]] — SURF needs a visual-change analog: when has the scene changed enough to warrant re-running the structural prior? ASPEN addresses the instruction-stable case; SURF needs the scene-stable / scene-changed case.

**horizon**

- [world2vec: Mechanistic World Models through Latent Dynamics Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40637) — Schmidt et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Faithful latent dynamics without reward, 1 OOM fewer parameters than DreamerV3, strong performance on continuous control. If world models become 10× cheaper without sacrificing task performance, the pure efficiency argument for token pruning weakens unless SURF shows structural selection improves generalization and novel-object robustness beyond just compute reduction. The sample-efficiency finding is the more relevant angle: mechanistic models may generalize with less data, connecting to SURF's claim that structural priors improve generalization via task-relevant visual selection.
