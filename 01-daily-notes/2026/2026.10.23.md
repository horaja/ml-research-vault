**Core**

- [FocusVLA: Future-Intent Guided Dynamic View Routing for VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31423v1) — Xu et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  View-level anticipated perception: predicts which camera views and spatial regions will matter for upcoming actions before the policy runs, then routes only those views to the VLA backbone. Multi-view analog of SURF's patch-level prior module; "anticipated perception" from Watch Your Step generalizes to view selection. Required for positioning note's multi-view axis.

- [Drift Says When, Budget Says How Much: Rate-Controlled KV Re-selection for Long-Form Multimodal Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31433v1) — Hu et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  One-shot token selection fails when semantic context drifts during generation; proposes drift-triggered re-selection with a learned rate controller. Directly instantiates [[claims/iterative selection]]: drift as the refinement trigger is a new operationalization of when to request more patches, distinct from step-count or confidence-threshold stopping criteria.

- [Attention from Action, for Action: Emergent Visual Bottlenecks for Policy Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/97c2db623247367a7622c4c8b0c79bb41f098dcc) — Zhuang et al.
  Label-free ROI emergence from action-prediction supervision alone, without gaze, affordance, or class labels. Direct threat to SURF's structural prior framing: if action supervision produces good ROIs without any external prior, SURF's geometric prior must justify itself specifically on novel-object distribution shift where action-derived ROIs fail. Read before writing positioning note's "why structural prior" axis.

**Adjacent**

- [Memory before Goal: Role of Pre-History Memory for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31166v1) — Chen et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Non-Markovian humanoid VLA requiring rolling pre-history memory because spatial cues vanish as the robot moves. Adjacent to GeoMem-VLA and Watch Your Step's temporal dimension question: if patch-relevance is non-Markovian, SURF's per-frame structural prior may miss tokens whose importance was established at prior timesteps.

- [On the dynamic evolution of CLIP texture-shape bias and its relationship to human alignment and model robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34d010689667d234226cdec857c77b30a27d62c4) — Hernández-Cámara et al.
  CLIP's shape bias emerges late in training after texture bias dominates; late-stage shape-biased checkpoints are substantially more robust to distribution shift. Adjacent to [[claims/structure helps]] and TLC-DiT's failure regime: frozen pretrained CLIP-conditioned maps (TLC-DiT) may be texture-biased, explaining their failure on novel objects — SURF's line-drawing prior (shape-biased by construction) addresses exactly that gap.

**Horizon**

- [Neural signatures of associational cortex emerge in a goal-directed model of visual search](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe2b65fab39706ed38f49423aec299bc32a28972) — Pourrahimi et al. · bioRxiv
  Computational model of goal-directed active vision reproduces neural signatures of associational cortex through a learned sequential-fixation policy. Horizon grounding for SURF's prior module as an anticipation mechanism: the biological closed loop between perception and action is implemented here as the computational analog of patch-level pre-selection.

- [A Fix for Softmax and Cross-Entropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31642v1) — Barron · arXiv Oct 2026
  Two numerical bugs in standard softmax/cross-entropy: Softmax Collapse (dominant logit rounds its gradient to zero) and Softmax Cancellation. Horizon correctness for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]]: if SURF's contrastive objective has a near-deterministically dominant patch ranking, Softmax Collapse silences its gradient entirely.
