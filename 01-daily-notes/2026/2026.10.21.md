**Core**

- [TLC-DiT: Task-Aligned Local Visual Conditioning for Robust Multitask Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34297) — Cai et al. · arXiv Sep 2026
  Adds explicit task-guided local visual feature maps to Multitask DiT via FiLM conditioning on CLIP embeddings; 93.5% LIBERO. Direct prior competitor for positioning note. The key distinction is where CLIP-conditioned maps fail: when query-target alignment is absent on novel objects, SURF's geometric prior covers that regime. Unread; required before positioning note closes.

- [When and What to Prune? Stage-Aware Visual Token Pruning for Efficient VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05273) — Shi et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  This is SAPrune. Token importance for manipulation stabilizes only after cross-modal language fusion completes in mid-LLM layers; pruning earlier loses action-critical context. 87.5% token reduction, 1.718× speedup. Resolves the stage-placement design decision: read before writing the contrastive alignment spec.

- [Beyond Token Importance: Preserving Spatial Scaffolds for Efficient VLA Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36967) — Chen et al. · arXiv Sep 2026
  Stride (uniform geometric sampling) outperforms semantic-relevance pruning at moderate ratios but collapses at extreme ratios. Directly supports SURF's structural prior claim while revealing its limits as a standalone filter. The "spatial scaffold" framing in today's positioning note frame originates here.

**Adjacent**

- [VLA-ACL: Action-Consistent Visual Token Pruning for Efficient VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08133) — Du et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Pruning criterion: action-prediction consistency across timesteps rather than attention scores or motion. Training-required. Competitor on positioning note's "action-consistency" axis.

- [FastVLA: Task-Conditioned and Temporally Adaptive Visual Token Compression for Efficient VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22146) — Xu et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Training-free, task-aware, temporally adaptive compression; matches or beats plug-and-play baselines on efficiency-performance trade-off. Competitor on positioning note's "temporal" axis.

- [AVA-VLA: Improving VLA Models with Active Visual Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18960) — Xiao et al. · arXiv Nov 2025 · 32 cites
  History-conditioned dynamic reweighting of visual tokens (POMDP formulation); SOTA on LIBERO and CALVIN. The 32-citation count puts this above the current cluster. Adjacent to SURF's active perception dimension; also threatens the claim that single-timestep geometric priors are sufficient without history.

**Horizon**

- [GSPA: Geo-Semantic Prior-Guided Attention for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/593fe3f7bbdcbd6169f9903fcab28e8e65316c4e) — Che et al. · ICSI 2026
