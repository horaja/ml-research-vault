**core**

- [SlimVLM: Sensitivity-aware Dynamic Structured Pruning with Adaptive Visual Token Selection for Efficient Vision-Language Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5cdcb7dd54ec3553a3ba59a0bb685d80788ac87e) — Wen et al. · arXiv 2026-08-04
  Pairs sensitivity-based structured channel pruning with adaptive visual token selection for efficient VLMs. Direct competitive baseline for SURF in the token-selection space: if sensitivity-aware pruning achieves SURF-level efficiency without any structural or language prior, SURF's contribution must be task-localization quality and held-out generalization, not raw efficiency.

- [GuidedAttention: Interpretable and Correctable Visual Attention for OOD-Robust Robot Manipulation via Imitation Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e21e550b5e561747284e9e919d53319b78cb9bf7) — Murooka et al. · arXiv 2026-07-23
  Explicit visual attention as an intermediate representation in imitation learning; humans can inspect and correct the attention map during deployment, giving positional and appearance OOD robustness. Comparison question for SURF: does a structural + language prior provide OOD robustness comparable to human-corrected attention without requiring the correction overhead? Positions SURF's prior as interpretable-by-construction vs. interpretable-by-supervision.

- [RoboInter1.5: A Holistic Intermediate Representation Suite for Embodied World Modeling and Robotic Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e8ee8d0c9963914602691fdd699a6e296ca6d8ba) — Wang et al. · arXiv 2026-07-21
  Extends RoboInter1.0 with contact regions, affordances, geometry, and trajectory annotations as structured conditioning signals for world modeling and manipulation. Field-level validation for [[claims/structure helps]]: convergence on fine-grained structural representations as conditioning signals for robot policies. RoboInter datasets are a candidate source for evaluating whether SURF's line-prior selects patches that coincide with affordance/contact annotations.

**adjacent**

- [RayViT: Ray-Conditioned Visual Representations for Viewpoint-Robust Imitation Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8009e0138c252bd117f45b37e49d31eff0314467) — Wang et al. · arXiv 2026-07-31
  Lightweight ViT encoder that injects camera ray geometry into pretrained ViT tokens via a conditioning mechanism, achieving viewpoint-robust IL without retraining the backbone. Geometric structure injection from a different level: SURF injects object-level structural priors (line density); RayViT injects camera-level geometric priors (ray direction). The two compose — RayViT stabilizes viewpoint; SURF selects task-relevant patches within the stabilized frame.

- [On the dynamic evolution of CLIP texture-shape bias and its relationship to human alignment and model robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34d010689667d234226cdec857c77b30a27d62c4) — Hernández-Cámara et al. · 2025
  Epoch-by-epoch analysis shows CLIP shifts from texture-biased (early training, better low-level perceptual alignment) to shape-biased (later training, better robustness). CLIP-based VLA encoders inherit this trajectory. Directly informs whether SURF's line-prior injects genuinely new structural signal or merely reinforces late-training CLIP shape bias — key question for [[claims/structure helps]] when CLIP is the backbone.

**horizon**

- [β-free Information Bottleneck in Representation Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e47df8102dc4a4474fc5d417fd95507107c66bbe) — Wang et al. · IEEE Trans. Image Processing 2026-08-26
  Reformulates the IB β hyperparameter as a learnable variable with static (intrinsic) and dynamic (task-dependent) components, removing the need to pre-specify the compression-preservation trade-off. Relevant to SURF's visual budget: if β can be parameterized as a function of task instruction and scene structural density, SURF's patch budget becomes adaptive rather than fixed — a theoretical grounding for [[claims/iterative selection]] without manual budget tuning.

- [Top-down perceptual inference shaping the activity of early visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6d34bc84826ed09fe3579539e126bfd8ef26ea8d) — Csikor et al. · bioRxiv 2025-02-04 (10 cites)
  Hierarchical deep generative model shows that top-down signals are inherent to hierarchical visual inference and predict V1/V2 activity. Updates SURF's biological motivation: if top-down task modulation shapes early visual cortex (not just V4), SURF's language-conditioned selection is grounded in a general hierarchical inference principle, not a V4-specific mechanism. Strengthens [[claims/language helps]] biological framing.
