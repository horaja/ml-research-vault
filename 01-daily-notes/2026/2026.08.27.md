## opener

Thursday, August 27. S2 rate-limiting resolved; second consecutive clean run (s2_keyword: 346, s2_recommend: 64, s2_author: 2, arXiv: 200, RSS: 20 → 619 after dedup).

Today's most important paper: **LM-X** (arXiv:2608.25757) trains return-to-go (RTG), event-to-go (ETG), and heteroscedastic uncertainty as explicit auxiliary signals alongside VLA actions — 74.1% RoboTwin2.0 vs. 55.4% GR00T N1.7. This is the "Decoding Task Progress" discovery from a different angle: not reading what's already encoded in the residual stream, but supervising explicit progress decoders during training. The RTG/ETG signals are natural candidates for SURF's selection gate: gate coverage widens when RTG drops (task regression) or ETG shifts (event boundary), making iterative selection principled rather than heuristic.

Secondary: **GaussianDream++** (arXiv:2608.25659) reaches 98.6% LIBERO and 87.8% LIBERO-Plus using 3D Gaussian world tokens trained into the VLA backbone but fully removed at inference (20 compact world tokens survive). This is the strongest structural-prior-trained VLA result I've seen. SURF's positioning note must now address GaussianDream++ alongside E2S-Pruner and Pointing-VLA: line-density prior vs. 3D Gaussian geometry; 2D vs. 3D structural signal; cheap prior vs. reconstruction-supervised training.

Execution priority remains unchanged: write the one-page positioning note. Every day it isn't written, the comparison set grows.

**core**

- [LM-X: Explainable Action Modeling with Progress, Event, and Uncertainty Prediction for Generalist Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25757v1) — Lou et al. · arXiv 2026
  RTG (visible task progress), ETG (next semantic event), and heteroscedastic action flow (local reliability) trained as explicit auxiliary heads alongside VLA actions; 74.1% on 50-task RoboTwin2.0 hard vs. 55.4% GR00T N1.7. RTG/ETG are candidate input signals for SURF's selection gate — if progress is decodable online, the gate can shift coverage at event boundaries rather than running fixed allocation throughout the episode.

- [PosA-VLA: Enhancing Action Generation via Pose-Conditioned Anchor Attention](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d77a24298f448ff9f6aeabec20137aebeb4534e9) — Li et al. · arXiv 2026
  Anchors VLA visual attention to task-relevant regions via pose-conditioned supervision; no auxiliary perception modules (no segmentor or grounder). Same motivating problem as SURF — distraction by task-irrelevant objects — but the structural signal is robot end-effector pose rather than line geometry or language conditioning. Baseline question: does pose anchoring achieve comparable task-relevant focus at lower annotation cost than SURF's contrastive prior?

- [GaussianDream++: Efficient 3D Gaussian World Modeling for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25659v1) — Jiang et al. · arXiv 2026
  World State Tokens + World Prediction Tokens trained with 3D Gaussian reconstruction and prediction objectives, removed at inference (20 compact world tokens remain); 98.6% LIBERO, 87.8% LIBERO-Plus, strong camera/layout shift robustness. Strongest structural-geometry-trained VLA baseline to date — directly challenges [[claims/structure helps]] positioning: if depth/3D Gaussian is the winning structural signal, SURF's line-density prior needs a cost/data/generalization argument.

**adjacent**

- [KEMO: Event-Driven Keyframe Memory for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation with VLA Policies](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/044e05409875576f8f9d896587d2088137629cfa) — Zeng et al. · arXiv 2026
  Kinematics + visual filtering detects task-relevant state-change events; selected keyframes encoded as compact temporally ordered memory tokens and fused via cross-attention; 23.6% TSR improvement on real-world long-horizon dual-arm tasks vs. memory-free baseline. Connects to [[claims/iterative selection]]: events are the principled trigger for re-allocating visual attention, and KEMO's event detector is a candidate design component for SURF's "when to refine" decision.

- [Reinforcement Learning for Multimodal Foundation Models: A Survey](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/443bbd0160ff39bd659da0e796f0e4a89aa2d7a2) — Wu et al. · ACM Computing Surveys
  Systematic coverage of 200+ papers at the RL × VLM intersection: PPO/GRPO policy optimization, verifiable reward paradigms, reward engineering, and VLA RL post-training. Reference document for SURF's efficiency/RL module design phase; maps the training strategy landscape the budget policy will sit in.

**horizon**

- [Explicitly Modeling Subcortical Vision with a Neuro-Inspired Front-End Improves CNN Robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d2c2818f16c45693869453594661a79b40a38dd) — Piper et al. · NeurIPS
  SubcorticalBlock + VOneBlock (V1) front-end produces stronger emergent shape bias and +9.3% robustness across adversarial perturbations, common corruptions, and domain shifts; complements training-based augmentation rather than replacing it. Grounding for why structural inductive biases from the biological vision hierarchy add robustness beyond what data augmentation provides — biological motivation for [[claims/structure helps]] at the architecture level, not just at the training-data level.
