## opener

Positioning note is day 65. Unread queue unchanged: Attention from Action, SAPrune, TLC-DiT, SPAR, Cao et al., Shi et al. Stage-Aware. Every downstream spec decision remains blocked on that first read.

Two themes emerge from today's digest. First, deployment constraints tighten the efficiency framing: TRIAGE (Kwon et al.) operationalizes why SURF needs both token reuse across timesteps and per-step pruning — on embedded boards, prefill latency scales with token count × re-observation frequency, making per-timestep structural consistency a deployment-level requirement, not just an efficiency metric. Second, the selection signal question sharpens further: ThinkProprio (Wang et al.) grounds token gating in robot proprioceptive state ("where is the gripper") rather than visual structure ("where are the structural boundaries"), directly competing with SURF's line-drawing prior. Cheng et al.'s interaction-aligned pruning independently names the gap SURF is positioned to fill: attention-score and motion-based selection both discard task-relevant structural regions when instruction-relevant appearance differs from attended regions.

**core**

- [TRIAGE: Efficient VLA Inference Needs Both Reuse and Pruning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34519) — Kwon et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Frames VLA inference on embedded hardware as a joint reuse+pruning problem: prefill latency grows with token count × re-observation frequency. Validates SURF's compute-budget framing at deployment level. SURF's pre-encoder structural selection maps naturally onto the "reuse" component — geometric prior tokens should be consistent across timesteps, enabling reuse. Check whether TRIAGE's reuse policy selects geometrically consistent tokens; if so, line-drawing priors provide a structural basis for that consistency rather than a per-step attended-region recomputation.

- [Think Proprioceptively: State-Grounded Visual Token Selection for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06575) — Wang et al. · arXiv Feb 2026
  Discretizes proprioceptive state into VLM tokens and uses robot state jointly with the instruction to gate visual patches before VLM computation (ThinkProprio). Direct competing design to SURF's line-drawing prior: proprioception gates by current end-effector position while SURF gates by visual structural boundaries. These may be complementary — structural edges select interaction-relevant regions before the gripper arrives; proprioception selects based on where it is now. Key ablation: structure-only vs. proprioception-only vs. both. Add as explicit baseline for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]].

- [Training-Free Interaction-Aligned Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22991) — Cheng et al. · arXiv Mar 2026
  Explicitly critiques attention-score and motion-based token selection for discarding task-relevant regions when instruction-relevant appearance differs from attended regions. Proposes contact/interaction-region priors as the corrective signal. Names the exact failure mode [[claims/structure helps]] is designed to address: structural priors capture task-relevant contact regions that attention and motion signals miss. Check whether the interaction-region signal overlaps with SURF's line-drawing selection on manipulation datasets — if high overlap, this paper validates the structural approach; if low, the signals are orthogonal and SURF needs to explain why structure generalizes beyond contact.

**adjacent**

- [GigaWorld-Policy: An Efficient Action-Centered World-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17240) — Ye et al. · arXiv Mar 2026 · 157 citations
  Decouples visual dynamics prediction from action generation in world-action models; eliminates joint-generation bottleneck and achieves SOTA on manipulation benchmarks. 157 citations marks this as a landmark efficiency paper in the world-action model space. SURF's structural prior could serve as the "action-centered" visual pre-filter this paper identifies as missing — selecting the tokens that matter for action without running full video generation at inference.

- [TAPESTRY: A Single Backbone for Video Generation and Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36823) — Collins et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Shares one backbone for video generation and robot control; shows joint training provides mutual gains. If a single backbone must support both visual prediction and action generation, token selection should preserve tokens that are jointly useful for both objectives. SURF's structural tokens (geometric shape, boundary information) are good candidates — they carry the visual prediction signal and the action-relevant structural information simultaneously.

**horizon**

- [Compact deep neural network models of the visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/18d00056b2cd000d3b365ac5c17e11c885bf0b2f) — Cowley et al. · preprint 2026 · 8 citations
  Systematic study of compact DNN architectures that best predict neural responses across V1–IT. Shape-selective intermediate layers are necessary for V4-level neural prediction accuracy; texture-biased compact models fail to match V4. Directly grounds [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if DNNs that match V4 responses require shape-selective intermediate representations, VLA backbones lacking shape bias (i.e., most texture-biased pretrained ViTs) are architecturally mismatched with V4-like computation — exactly the gap SURF's line-drawing prior is designed to inject. Check whether VLA backbones SURF targets pass or fail the V4-compactness test.
