**core**

- [T3R: Training-Free Two-Stage Token Refinement Towards Efficient and Robust VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08413) — Hassan et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Identifies two orthogonal redundancies in VLA visual tokens: spatial irrelevance (background tokens) and attention misallocation (retained tokens poorly weighted). T3R's two-stage pipeline — stage-1 spatial filter, stage-2 attention redistribution — maps directly onto SURF's structural prior + language conditioning architecture. This is the most direct training-free baseline SURF must outperform. Read to extract the stage-1 spatial filter: does it select the same tokens as a line-drawing prior, and what is the residual gap that learned contrastive alignment would close?

- [SVD-Prune: Spectral Vision Token Pruning under Extreme Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02284) — Apedo et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Reformulates token pruning as preserving the dominant low-rank spectral structure of the visual representation via SVD leverage scores — architecture-agnostic, training-free. Surfaced by the "spectral token pruning" query added Nov 1. The spectral formulation is mechanistically adjacent to line-drawing priors (edges live in high-energy singular components). The key diagnostic: do SVD leverage scores and line-drawing prior select the same tokens? If yes, SVD-Prune is a training-free approximation to [[claims/structure helps]]; if not, the gap identifies where geometric edge structure adds value over spectral structure.

**adjacent**

- [SLA: Scaling Spatial-Language-Action Model via Continual Pre-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08373) — Li et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Scales implicit 3D spatial alignment to VLA pre-training, maintaining it across task-specific adaptation. If spatial/structural understanding is captured during pre-training, SURF's pre-encoder structural prior may be redundant on spatially-pretrained foundations. Read to understand what spatial features SLA's pre-training captures vs. what a line-drawing prior adds on top — this directly bears on whether SURF's value is in the prior itself or in the selection mechanism.

- [DATP: Training-Free Depth-Aware Token Pruning for Vision-Language Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/45e8c0dd256067e58581a8cb537b16b260c68986) — Liu et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Training-free depth-guided pre-LLM token pruning using depth signal to differentiate workspace (near-field, preserve) from background (far-field, prune). A direct structural-prior baseline using continuous depth vs. SURF's discrete edge/line prior — independent of @liDepthCacheDepthGuidedTrainingFree2026 (DepthCache). The comparison question: does continuous depth (near/far distance) or discrete edge structure (object boundaries) better predict task-relevant patches in manipulation scenes where the workspace is geometrically complex?

**horizon**

- [Invariance before structure: two modes of emergent compression in representation learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07952) — Rajpal & Goodman · arXiv Nov 2026
  Distinguishes state-space compression (representations gain predictive power over future states) from parameter-space compression (networks converge to the same representation despite different micro-level parameters), formalizing both as Fisher sensitivities. The parameter-space compression result — invariance emerges before structural convergence — provides theoretical grounding for why a fixed structural prior (line drawings) could generalize across different VLA architectures without task-specific retraining. The Fisher sensitivity framework is also relevant to determining which visual tokens are most important to preserve under a visual budget constraint.
