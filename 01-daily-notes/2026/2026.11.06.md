## opener

Positioning note is day 73. Unread queue unchanged: Attention from Action, SAPrune, TLC-DiT, SPAR, Cao et al., Shi et al. Stage-Aware. Every downstream spec decision remains blocked.

The architectural question that sharpened over Nov 3–5: pre-encoder structural prior placement is under simultaneous challenge from three directions. NEO-VLA removes the ViT encoder entirely, requiring structural priors to be repositioned at patch tokenization inside the LLM. Herr et al. show intrinsic (query-independent) ViT token importance grows with depth, which favors post-language-fusion selection over SURF's pre-encoder placement. Shi et al. Stage-Aware is unread but may confirm that pruning efficacy improves after language-fusion. These three are independent lines converging on the same design question. The positioning note cannot close without an explicit answer.

The A³-VLA comparison (from Nov 5) also needs resolution before the positioning note: supervised segmentation-guided attention alignment is a training-time fix that bypasses the need for a hand-engineered structural prior. SURF must argue why the unsupervised structural prior is preferable — distribution shift, inference-time flexibility, or token selection (not just attention supervision).

- Sun et al. (Artificial Foveated): Does SURF's line-drawing prior select edges that co-vary with task identity in the training distribution?

- TRIAGE (Kwon et al.): Does TRIAGE's reuse policy select geometrically consistent tokens across timesteps?
- ThinkProprio (Wang et al.): Does proprioception-grounded selection overlap or diverge from structure-grounded selection on manipulation tasks?

- GazeAct (Pani et al.): Does SURF's line-drawing selection overlap with GazeAct's human gaze annotations on shared manipulation scenes?
- Athi et al. (Query-Vision Decomposition): Does query-response token scoring outperform attention-based selection under novel-object distribution shift?

- PhysVLMM (Ghosh): Does SURF's structural prior select regions that are also physically affordant in manipulation datasets?

- FoAViS (Hassler et al.): What fixation strategy emerges from training, and does structural scene content predict fixation placement?
- When Does Test-Time Search Help? (Liu et al.): Is the value-selector failure mode specific to the reward model, or a general result?
- Attention Maps Know When to Think (Cho et al.): Is attention entropy a patch-level or token-level signal? Reliable under distribution shift?
- VLALagBench (Wen et al.): Which manipulation task categories are most latency-sensitive? Are they in SURF's planned evaluation scope?

- Attention Biases (Herr et al.): Does the intrinsic token importance bias term align with structural/edge content in natural scenes and manipulation scenes? Is the depth-dependence finding consistent across ViT-S/B/L?

## risks

- **Positioning note (day 73)**: Unread queue is the direct blocker. No progress for 73 days.
- **Pre-encoder placement validity**: Three concurrent challenges — NEO-VLA removes the encoder (structural priors must be repositioned); Herr et al. show intrinsic importance grows with depth (favors post-fusion selection); Shi et al. Stage-Aware (unread) may confirm post-fusion is better. Stage placement decision cannot be deferred past the positioning note.
- **Supervised attention alignment**: A³-VLA achieves alignment via segmentation-guided supervision at fine-tuning. SURF must demonstrate why unsupervised structural prior is preferable — the most defensible argument is distribution generalization and inference-time flexibility.
- **Encoder-free designs**: NEO-VLA removes ViT; if encoder-free becomes standard, the pre-encoder stage disappears and structural priors must be applied at patch tokenization inside the LLM. Flag for positioning note.
- **Symbolic abstraction baseline**: "Do Agentic Policies Need to Look at Every Step?" introduces symbolic geometric caching as a competing approach; positioning note must address it.
- **Compute-matched baseline**: RTPolicy (32.9 Hz) may achieve SURF's efficiency target via a different mechanism.
- **Latency floor**: VLALagBench shows 100ms ≈ 50% success drop on moving targets.
- **Query-guided diversity collapse**: R²-Omni — SURF's contrastive selection may face this failure mode.
- **Test-time compute null result**: Liu et al. (Nov 2) show learned selectors underperform random on RoboCasa.
- **ThinkProprio baseline**: Proprioception-grounded selection not yet ablated.
- **Cao et al. (day 38)**: Required for [[02-projects/surf/claims/language helps]].
