**core**

- [Training-Free Interaction-Aligned Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Embodied Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a63ff0e77bf727e8235c4fc6768f8f59e5cfcdf4) — Cheng et al. · arXiv Mar 2026
  IAprune treats per-frame budget and within-budget selection as linked decisions; argues that semantic/attention-score ranking fails when instruction-related appearance and action-space motion are not yet spatially aligned. This is exactly the alignment gap [[claims/structure helps]] and [[claims/language helps]] are meant to fill — but IAprune claims to close it training-free. Strongest training-free manipulation baseline seen so far; must appear in positioning note before the contrastive alignment spec is drafted.

- [GRAFT: Grounded and Efficient Online Reinforcement Adaptation for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27079v1) — Qiu et al. · arXiv 2026
  Online RL adaptation of VLA for fine-grained biomedical manipulation; explicitly frames "task-level rewards provide little guidance about which regions matter" as the core problem and uses visual grounding to resolve it. This is the closest framing to SURF's contrastive prior rationale seen in a live paper: SURF's structural + language prior is one candidate answer to exactly this problem. Skim to understand their grounding mechanism and whether it's composable with or competitive to SURF's approach.

- [SparseVLM+: Visual Token Sparsification With Improved Text-Visual Attention Pattern](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a54bd69aa751389cf0f0bb7566234e6633589c25) — Zhang et al. · IEEE TPAMI Jul 2026
  TPAMI journal upgrade of SparseVLM; text-guided training-free token pruning based on improved text-visual attention pattern analysis. Highest-authority publication in the training-free text-guided selection line. If this works without fine-tuning, [[claims/language helps]] must argue why training is worth it: better alignment, generalization, or action-relevance that pure attention-pattern scoring cannot capture.

**adjacent**

- [PACE: A Unified Condense-and-Extract Paradigm for Fast VLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27206v1) — Liu et al. · arXiv 2026
  Addresses two blind spots in prior token reduction: (1) pre-encoder latency unoptimized by post-encoder pruning, (2) hard budget → loss of holistic context. Introduces Holistic Context Tokens to jointly preserve global + local. Relevant to TOWN-VLA's attention collapse concern: if holistic context is lost under hard budgets, SURF's hard selection gate may suffer the same failure mode as the IT-collapsed attention. Skim the Holistic Context Token design.

- [WCM: A World Critic Model for Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6baeeb619be6041013d59387d1099f5653ec7ed9) — Fei et al. · arXiv Jul 2026
  World critic for VLA RL post-training: argues single-frame critics mismatch the POMDP nature of manipulation, proposes a multi-frame critic with efficient history encoding. Design input for SURF's efficiency/RL module: the budget policy critic needs temporal context to evaluate whether the current visual selection is sufficient, not just a single-frame value estimate. File under efficiency/RL module design phase.

**horizon**

- [End-to-end topographic networks as models of cortical map formation and human visual behaviour](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4b8bf7404003befc7d91df4fd193b812a8083cbc) — Lu et al. · Nature Human Behaviour
  All-TNNs develop topographic organization from scratch without hard-wired spatial constraints, reproduce V4/MT properties, match human behavioral signatures, and operate on low energy budget — suggesting metabolic benefit of smooth topographic map formation. Modern computational justification for topographic structural priors as principled rather than just biologically inspired. Relevant to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if All-TNNs develop V4-like topography under efficiency pressure, the topographic prior has an energy-cost motivation independent of biological analogy.

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — Zhao et al. · bioRxiv Jul 2026
  Dual stochastic MPC where eye movements are controlled to minimize task-relevant uncertainty and build an internal model used for hand movement planning; explains saccade-pursuit patterns in continuous tasks. Formal computational model of active vision mapping onto SURF's "where to look" framing: the selection objective is uncertainty minimization over task-relevant features, not generic saliency. Could ground [[claims/iterative selection]] in a principled uncertainty-reduction objective.
