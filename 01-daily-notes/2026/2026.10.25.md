# 2026-10-25

## opener

Positioning note is day 61. The unread queue (Attention from Action, SAPrune, TLC-DiT, SPAR, Cao et al.) has not moved in three days.

Two new papers directly reinforce SURF's position without adding to the unread queue. **Beyond Token Importance: Preserving Spatial Scaffolds** (2609.36967) shows that a purely geometric stride baseline outperforms semantic token selectors in some VLA settings, providing direct evidence for [[claims/structure helps]] that does not rely on SURF's own experiments. **FastVLA** (2610.22146) demonstrates that temporal adaptation of visual sparsity is achievable training-free: different timesteps need different amounts of visual compute, which empirically validates the premise behind [[claims/iterative selection]] without requiring a learned policy.

**VLA-ACL** (2610.08133) introduces action-consistency supervision as a pruning signal — a directly trained baseline SURF's contrastive alignment design must account for. If action-consistency training achieves the structural relevance that SURF aims to learn through contrastive alignment + geometric prior, the marginal contribution of the structural prior needs to be isolated.

The unread queue remains the blocking bottleneck. Do not write the positioning note today without reading Attention from Action first.

**core**

- [FastVLA: Task-Conditioned and Temporally Adaptive Visual Token Compression for Efficient VLA Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6a1ecd601dafa2e204cd2c7b9d1b0aae8c229410) — Xu et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Training-free framework that adapts visual token count per timestep and per task; achieves comparable or better efficiency-performance trade-off than plug-in compressors. Validates temporal adaptation as an inference-time paradigm for [[claims/iterative selection]]; task-conditioning matches SURF's language-guided selection without geometric prior — sets the floor SURF must beat.

- [Beyond Token Importance: Preserving Spatial Scaffolds for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Inference](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ef62d971f2796de5224660cade47c195f294f5f3) — Chen et al. · arXiv Sep 2026
  Builds a Stride baseline (uniformly samples tokens along the spatial sequence, purely geometric) and finds it outperforms semantic/attention-based selectors on some manipulation tasks; constructs SSTP framework that preserves spatial scaffolding. Direct empirical support for [[claims/structure helps]]: spatial geometry, not learned semantic relevance, is sometimes sufficient and often necessary for manipulation.

- [VLA-ACL: Action-Consistent Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7578503b06613a30bcb06248ad9fdb933ee21c9f) — Du et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Trains token pruning directly on action-prediction consistency rather than attention scores or semantic similarity. New supervised baseline for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]]: if action-consistency supervision alone achieves structural alignment, SURF's contrastive objective + geometric prior must show additive gain in the novel-object distribution-shift regime.

**adjacent**

- [Think Proprioceptively: State-Grounded Visual Token Selection for VLA Policies](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ce0a7201ebc0b4804a7f28d3536338e57e5c345) — Wang et al. · arXiv Feb 2026
  Discretizes proprioceptive state into VLM vocabulary and uses it jointly with the instruction to gate visual patches before VLM computation. Adjacent design for [[experiments/contrastive alignment]]: embodied state (robot posture) vs. geometric structure as the selection signal. Check whether proprioceptive gating and geometric gating select overlapping or complementary regions.

- [Token-World: World Modeling in Vision-Language Model Token Space for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00575) — Fu et al. · arXiv Sep 2026
  Compresses VLM visual tokens into a compact latent state, learns future dynamics in that reduced space, and maps predicted states back to policy inputs — eliminating RGB prediction as an intermediate. Adjacent to adaptive compute: if world dynamics are learnable in sparse token space, SURF's per-step token budget can be adapted via predicted future state rather than current observation alone.

**horizon**

- [End-to-end topographic networks as models of cortical map formation and human visual behaviour](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4b8bf7404003befc7d91df4fd193b812a8083cbc) — Lu et al. · Nature Human Behaviour 2025
  All-TNNs develop V4-like topographic feature maps end-to-end from task objectives alone; smooth topographic organization yields metabolic benefits. Directly grounds [[claims/v4 relevance]]: topographic smoothness has a functional computational benefit (metabolic efficiency + generalization), not merely an anatomical accident. Relevant to whether a V4-dispersity-derived prior has intrinsic computational justification beyond biological analogy.

- [Information-dependent eye-hand coordination emerges from active vision](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/439f4faa6ea6f868d768d06c98a2dcdbcc8bfc44) — Zhao et al. · bioRxiv Jul 2026
  Dual stochastic MPC where eye movements are continuously controlled to minimize task-relevant uncertainty; saccade-pursuit patterns emerge from uncertainty minimization and build an internal model for hand planning. Formalizes the gaze-as-uncertainty-reduction principle underlying SURF's active perception frame: prior module as a mechanism to pre-reduce visual uncertainty before the action model runs.

## questions

- CoRP: Does VIB failure on LIBERO-Goal mean SURF's contrastive alignment must produce hard selections?
- PrismWAM: Do depth-space activations co-locate with line-drawing-selected patches?
- BrainLoRA: Can LoRA on DINOv2 predict V4 dispersity?
- GeoMem-VLA: Does SURF's 2D line-drawing prior capture the contour/shape geometry that GeoMem-VLA's 3D temporal memory provides?
- Watch Your Step: Does SURF's prior module need a temporal dimension?
- When Text Matters: Which text-visual alignment failure modes apply to SURF's contrastive objective on novel manipulation objects?

New from today:
- FastVLA: Does temporal adaptation of visual sparsity require per-step recomputation of the token selector, or can SURF's geometric prior serve as a stable per-step base with iterative refinement on top?
- Spatial Scaffolds / Stride baseline: Does SURF's line-drawing prior select a geometrically coherent spatial scaffold, or does it select scattered structural tokens that fail the contiguity assumption the Stride baseline relies on?
- VLA-ACL: Does action-consistency supervision select structurally coherent regions, or semantically scattered ones? Does adding SURF's geometric prior to action-consistency supervision yield orthogonal coverage?
- ThinkProprio: Do proprioceptive-gated patches overlap with line-drawing-selected patches? Is embodied state a competing or complementary selection signal?
