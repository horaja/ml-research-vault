# 2026-09-27

## opener

Two papers this cycle test language conditioning from opposite directions and together tighten the design requirement on SURF's scoring stage. Robot Independent Intelligence studies VLA decision-making after explicit instruction is removed; it uses vision-language entropy to quantify task uncertainty and finds models can recover reliable manipulation behavior from visual features alone on contact-rich tasks. This is the causal ablation that [[claims/language helps]] has lacked: not that language is unhelpful, but that the margin over instruction-free baselines defines where instruction conditioning uniquely matters. VPRune independently arrives at the same pressure point from an efficiency angle: it identifies text-guided selection bias as a first-class failure mode of instruction-conditioned token pruning — alongside information loss and positional distortion — and proposes visual-only diversity selection as the fix. The structural geometry argument for SURF is now framed from two sides: if language instruction introduces bias during selection, and if instruction-free VLAs still perform on contact-rich tasks, then [[claims/language helps]] needs precise task-category evidence, not general validation.

WorldContact adds a third angle: if contact organizes world models for manipulation, contact prediction is a candidate auxiliary selection signal that neither language nor structure currently captures in SURF's scoring stage.

**core**

- [Robot Independent Intelligence: When Language Vanishes in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2b733fcd0d3741c9139ab8e7bccebc5f0fb49933) — Gui, Gui, Luximon · IEEE RA-L · 2026
  Uses vision-language entropy to quantify task uncertainty; studies VLA decision-making when explicit instructions are removed. Tests the inverse of "language helps": if contact-rich tasks succeed without instruction, the claim requires evidence for specific task categories (novel objects, language-specified goals, long-horizon reasoning) where instruction dominates. This is the causal ablation [[claims/language helps]] has lacked. Check: does entropy at inference time predict which steps require instruction conditioning, and is there a per-patch entropy formulation compatible with SURF's scoring stage?

- [VPRune: Efficient Training-free Pre-LLM Visual Token Pruning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/abcacc5f990610ea511c80381d696fae0c0830b2) — Lv et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-21
  Identifies three failure modes of existing visual token pruning: text-guided selection bias, information loss, and positional distortion. Proposes visual-only diversity selection + similarity-guided recycling + position-preserving restoration. The text-guided bias finding is independently motivated: instruction-conditioned selection produces spatially biased token sets. Directly relevant to SURF's claim that structural geometry corrects for language-introduced bias. Check: is the bias spatial (center-weighted) or semantic (text-similar patches), and does the Laplacian/line-drawing prior correct for the same bias or a different one?

**adjacent**

- [Mind What Matters for Reasoning: Aligning Cross-Modal Attention via Selective Probability Mass Concentration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29940v1) — Deng et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-24
  Improves visual grounding by concentrating cross-modal attention probability mass on task-relevant regions without direct supervision. Model-side intervention (attention redistribution) vs. SURF's input-side intervention (token selection). Complementary if SURF selects tokens and model-side concentration further sharpens within-token attention. Check: does probability mass concentration degrade when tokens are pre-pruned upstream, or does it compensate for selection gaps?

- [WorldContact: A Contact-Centric World Model for Scalable Robot Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4151bce449850bb4843d393cabe1fa08d1c8eabb) — Wang et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-17
  Contact as the organizing abstraction for manipulation world modeling; uses contact prediction to structure world model rollouts. Raises the question: if contact organizes world models, should contact prediction serve as an auxiliary scoring signal in SURF's selection stage? Distinct from instruction and structure — contact is action-predictive but not language-mediated. Check: does contact-centric world model predict which patches are manipulation-critical, and does this correlate with line-drawing-based structural scores?

- [Think Like a World Model, Act Like a VLA: Distilling World-Model Representations into Compact Robot Policies](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/93d93e01a0425926feb978956797e3fdb54c320a) — Dao et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-21
  Distills world model representations into VLA backbone at training time, without forward-pass overhead at inference. Pattern: external representation import into VLA without inference cost. Applicable to SURF's structural prior module — V4-derived shape maps or contact-centric representations could be imported by distillation rather than requiring separate inference. Check: what is the distillation objective, and does it preserve spatial structure of the source representation?

**horizon**

- [Compact deep neural network models of the visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/18d00056b2cd000d3b365ac5c17e11c885bf0b2f) — Cowley et al. · Nature · 2026-02-25
  Adaptive data collection + targeted training produces compact (few-unit) predictive models of macaque V4; compact models match full-scale models on held-out neural responses. Directly relevant to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if V4 is well-approximated by compact models, the inference cost of a V4-derived per-patch shape prior is feasible. Horizon question: what is the minimum model size that preserves shape selectivity, and does a compact V4 model generalize across stimulus domains (natural images vs. robot workspace)?

- [A single computational objective can produce specialization of streams in visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f673f711af4d48c03135e75c4cf08a74fa30e653) — Finzi, Margalit, Kay, Yamins · bioRxiv · 2026-06-20
  TDANN (topographic DNN with local similarity loss) better predicts dorsal/lateral/ventral stream organization than task-specialized models trained separately. Stream specialization is emergent from a single topographic training objective, not hand-coded. Supports [[claims/v4 relevance]]: V4 topographic shape priors may be learnable rather than requiring hand-crafted V4 architectures. Read alongside @jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying.

## questions

Carried forward:
- Does S²Prune's spatial-bias confirmation in static MLLM settings transfer to instruction-conditioned closed-loop VLA? Is the center/texture bias mechanism the same under dynamic inference?
- Is SURF's line-drawing prior mainly measuring local Laplacian variance, or does it recover task-specific contact/workspace layout that Laplacian misses?
- GSPA: what is the "geo" component — depth, edge, learned geometry? (day 104 unread)
- ThinkProprio: does proprioception gating operate at the same patch granularity as SURF's selection? What task categories require instruction conditioning over state?
- Conditional Removability: which layer is SURF's selection intended to operate at, and does iterative selection address the depth-conditioned removability constraint by construction?
- Does SURF's contrastive loss induce conditional diversity (per Beyond Attention or Similarity), or does it under-select along non-geometric instruction-relevant dimensions?
- History-Conditioned: does temporal accumulation add independent signal over a static structural prior, or does it substitute for one when no prior is available at step 0?
- Focus-Scan-Refine: is SURF's structural prior functionally equivalent to FSR's focal saliency stage, and if so, what does SURF's refinement stage need to add?
- Gaze on the Prize: for which task categories does language supervision select better patches than return-grounded contrastive learning?

New from today:
- Robot Independent Intelligence: if vision-language entropy quantifies task uncertainty without instructions, can it serve as an auxiliary per-patch signal in SURF's scoring stage? What is the operational distinction between entropy-based uncertainty and instruction-conditioned importance?
- VPRune: text-guided selection bias is identified as a failure mode of instruction-conditioned pruning — does SURF's structural geometry prior mitigate this by grounding selection in spatial layout rather than text similarity?
- WorldContact: should contact prediction serve as an auxiliary selection signal in SURF's scoring stage, and does it correlate with line-drawing-based structural scores on manipulation benchmarks?
