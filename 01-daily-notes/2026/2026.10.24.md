This must be read before the positioning note can close. Also worth watching: yesterday surfaced two new claims. (1) CLIP's shape bias emerges only late in training (Hernández-Cámara et al.) — if TLC-DiT uses an early-stage CLIP checkpoint, its spatial maps may be texture-biased, which is exactly the gap SURF's line-drawing prior is designed to fill. (2) Barron's Softmax Collapse finding raises a concrete implementation risk: if SURF's contrastive alignment objective produces near-deterministic patch rankings early in training, the gradient vanishes under standard PyTorch cross-entropy. Check before implementing.
