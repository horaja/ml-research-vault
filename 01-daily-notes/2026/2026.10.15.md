# 2026-10-15

## opener

Positioning note is day 51. No new structural blockers but one direct challenge.

Three threads converge today. First, **PerturBot** shows that VLA policies can complete tasks while systematically ignoring the evidence they should attend to — shortcut priors built on appearance statistics substitute for genuine structural grounding. This is the sharpest version of the challenge to [[02-projects/surf/claims/structure helps]]: SURF's geometric prior must demonstrate causal necessity, not just correlation with task-relevant patches. Removing geometric structural evidence should degrade VLA performance more than removing texture; PerturBot's perturbation methodology is the evaluation framework to test this. The positioning note's regime claim must absorb this: the argument is no longer just "geometric prior covers novel objects where text-subspace statistics are absent" but "geometric prior breaks the appearance shortcuts that text-conditioned methods inherit."

Second, **VAttnPO** uses answer-conditioned attention maps as direct policy optimization signals, closing the loop between SURF's selection prior and training reward. This is the missing design element for [[02-projects/surf/experiments/contrastive alignment]]: instead of offline contrastive supervision over patch-relevance labels, VAttnPO suggests training the selection module end-to-end against a language-attention policy gradient.

Third, **EarlyCommit** shows human listeners commit to object identity several words before instructions complete; robots currently lag. [[02-projects/surf/claims/language helps]] must extend to this incremental regime: SURF's instruction-conditioned selector should be robust to partial instructions, not only complete ones.

Cao et al. is day 16. Read today.
