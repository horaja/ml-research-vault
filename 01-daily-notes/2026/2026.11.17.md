Stage-Aware) plus the nine added Nov 15–16 (ActPrune, AFP, ThinkProprio, STD, Gerigk scrubbing, plus VLA Survey, SWAM, topographic audit, Finite Intelligence). The VLA Survey (2611.18372, 88 works, Dec 2022–Oct 2026) added yesterday is the highest-leverage read this week: if the efficiency axis covers structural-prior-based token selection as a subfield, it may compress the unread queue bottleneck and unblock the positioning note faster than reading individual competitor papers.

The competitive picture sharpened over Nov 15–16. Three papers now define the pre-encoder competitor cluster: ActPrune (action-grounded, training-free, no structural prior), AFP (task-conditioned foveation for shortcut mitigation), ThinkProprio (proprioception-grounded patch gating). Together they define the precise question SURF must answer: does V4 shape structure add OOD robustness beyond action grounding alone, task-conditioned foveation alone, or state-grounded gating alone? None of these have been read yet.

One positive signal from yesterday: the topographic audit (2611.18105) confirmed empirically that spatial averaging destroys retinotopic organization — supporting SURF's pre-encoder placement argument on a novel failure-mode ground. This is evidence, not proof (specific to TRIBE v2, not ViT backbones), but it's the first empirical confirmation of the spatial-averaging argument.

## papers

### digest (auto)

**core**
- [Beyond Token Stacking: Multi-scale Visual Modulation and Adaptive Fused-feature Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21168v1) — Xiao et al. · arXiv
  Multi-scale visual modulation injected into FFNs rather than concatenating tokens, targeting the same redundant-token overhead SURF targets. The adaptive calibration step is a structural analogue of SURF's selection gate. Read before the positioning note; defines a new competitor approach in the core efficiency space.

- [How VLMs Plan What's Next](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21600v1) — Gandikota et al. · arXiv
  Mechanistic dissection of VLM attention: binding heads resolve text-to-image pointers, routing heads propagate, iteration heads cycle through objects, inhibition heads suppress already-named regions. This is the internal mechanism SURF's pre-encoder structural prior needs to amplify or bypass. Directly relevant to the pre-encoder placement question and the STD stage-placement question.

- [ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607v1) — Yu et al. · arXiv
  Scales VLA backbone to 552B via GPT-6 Astra. Sets the frontier scale SURF must target; determines whether structural token pruning remains tractable at this scale and what efficiency gains survive frontier-scale pretraining.

**adjacent**
- [ThinkWAM: Rethinking Latent Futures for Action Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21258v1) — Tang et al. · arXiv
  World-action model with bidirectional latent coupling: future predictions conditioned on the proposed action chunk enable refinement before execution. Relevant to SURF's iterative selection claim — if world-model latents can refine actions, SURF's iterative visual selection may be compressible into fewer rounds.

- [ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21099v1) — Sun et al. · arXiv
  Force sensing adapter for frozen frontier VLMs via a fast subloop beneath the model. New data point on proprioceptive grounding without retraining — directly adjacent to ThinkProprio's state-grounded gating argument. SURF must distinguish its shape-structure contribution from force/state grounding at the VLM interface.

- [ReuseMem: One Memory, Multiple Goals in Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21115v1) — Kang et al. · arXiv
  Goal-independent visual memory for manipulation: records features without knowing future goals, retrieves and adapts at query time. Adjacent to the "Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock?" question — if goal-agnostic spatial memory achieves multi-goal manipulation, SURF's structural prior must add structure that goal-agnostic recording misses.

**horizon**
- [Classical structural priors improve neural molecular representations in molecule language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21276v1) — Lee et al. · arXiv
  Classical molecular similarity used as a CKA regularizer (SAGA) improves neural molecular representations in molecule-language models. Cross-domain evidence for SURF's core hypothesis: a hand-designed structural prior from domain knowledge improves neural representations even when the model was not trained with it.

## questions

Carried (see Nov 16):
- VLA Survey: Does the efficiency axis cover structural-prior-based token selection as a subfield, and does it position it relative to saliency-based, spectral, and language-conditioned approaches?
- SWAM: Does SWAM's saliency signal (gradient × cosine) capture the same structural patterns as SURF's V4 line-drawing prior, or are the two signals empirically orthogonal on manipulation scenes?
- Topographic audit: Does the spatial-averaging-destroys-retinotopy finding generalize beyond TRIBE v2 to the ViT backbones SURF targets — does this strengthen the pre-encoder placement argument or is it specific to brain response models?
- Finite intelligence: Can the "future-task information" measure be computed on SURF's candidate token sets to give a formal lower bound on the required visual budget for a given manipulation task?
- ActPrune: Does SURF's V4 shape structure add OOD robustness or task-selectivity over ActPrune's action-grounded signal on distribution-shifted manipulation scenes?
- ThinkProprio: Does proprioceptive state capture enough action-relevance signal that SURF's structural prior adds nothing beyond state-grounded gating on tabletop manipulation?
- STD: Are SURF's pre-encoder selection targets concentrated in the shallow (edge-detection) functional layers, or do they require deep semantic representations?
- Task scrubbing (Gerigk et al.): Which VLM backbone families show the highest visual shortcut susceptibility, and does SURF's structural prior reduce susceptibility more for high-susceptibility backbones?

## risks

- **Positioning note (day 84)**: Unread queue at ~15 papers; VLA Survey is the highest-leverage entry point.
- **ActPrune (day 3, unread)**: Pre-encoder action-grounded pruning is training-free and competitive — if ActPrune matches SURF's accuracy at equal compression on manipulation benchmarks, the structural prior's value over pure action grounding must be demonstrated specifically on OOD/shortcut scenarios.
- **ThinkProprio (day 3, unread)**: State-grounded patch gating — SURF must show structural prior adds beyond state+instruction. Possible that tabletop manipulation is sufficiently covered by proprioceptive state without shape structure.
- **SWAM (day 2, unread)**: Saliency-weighted merging (gradient × cosine) keeps the token-reduction baseline moving. If gradient signal captures the same structural patterns as V4 prior on in-distribution manipulation, SURF's novelty requires OOD or low-data justification.
- **Encoder choice dominance**: "Unbundling" (unread) may show patch size and pretraining objective dominate spatial performance.
- **ViT intrinsic binding**: "Objects as Addresses" suggests ViTs already have structural binding — SURF's prior must demonstrate it adds over this.
- **Frontier zero-shot baseline**: Reins (GPT-6.1, Claude Opus 5.5 zero-shot) + open-weight RL training (2611.18562, day 2) define the no-prior efficiency baseline.
- **Be My Eye baseline (unread)**: Task-prediction compression without structural prior achieves dual-robot VLA results.
- **Pre-encoder placement validity**: Three concurrent challenges (NEO-VLA, Herr et al., Shi et al. Stage-Aware) unresolved; topographic audit (yesterday) is positive evidence but specific to TRIBE v2.
