**core**

- [Task-Aware Dynamic Visual Token Pruning for Efficient VLA Inference](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/57777db0ec2f567f3742d2d0cab4f1e00613e765) — Lin, Sun, Pang · IEEE ERTS
  Training-free dynamic VT pruning that estimates task-conditioned token importance after early vision-language interaction; decouples main/wrist-view budgets; reuses keyframe scores under temporal consistency. Achieves 96.25% on LIBERO at 39% of OpenVLA-OFT FLOPs. Now the sharpest direct baseline for SURF: both approaches are training-free and task-conditioned, but TADP derives importance from VL interaction (learned signal after forward pass), while SURF's structural prior is geometry-derived (data-free, available before any inference). The differentiation axis: pre-deployment generalization and novel object settings where learned importance has no support, vs. in-distribution inference where VL interaction is available.

- [Depth Matters: Language-Guided Spatial Representation Learning for Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d6087ec299513c92af00e2061701d128aa135681) — Zhu, Zhou, Guan, Wang · CAAC 2026
  Decoupled visual encoder with pseudo-depth injection (convolutional spatial positional encoding in late transformer layers) and language-guided attention pooling. Uses JEPA self-supervised pretraining with cross-task contrastive regularization. Matches or outperforms SOTA visual encoders on RLBench and ManiSkill. Competing structural signal: depth vs. SURF's line-drawing prior. Critical question: does pseudo-depth capture manipulation-relevant 3D structure that 2D line drawings miss, or do both capture the same surface geometry at different representations?

- [Decoupling Vision, Language, and Action for Efficient Multi-Task Robot Policies](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/24eeaa684ff420a7b568fc7696de4c1b78cebd47) — Sun, Liang, Zeng, Wang
  DINOv3 + NeoBERT + MeanFlow head (DEM) matches GR00T N1.7 (56.9% → 55.6%) and π₀.₅ (54.6% → 55.6%) on 18 RoboCasa tasks at 8-17× throughput and 6-15× less GPU energy. Validates SURF's core architectural assumption: decoupled encoders are competitive with billion-parameter joint encoding for multi-task manipulation. The efficiency gap is now measured empirically — SURF's structural prior module must achieve comparable or better task-relevant selection within this decoupled design budget.

**adjacent**

- [MoE-ACT: Scaling Multi-Task Bimanual Manipulation with Sparse Language-Conditioned Mixture-of-Experts](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6882a0ece1703c2988a0d7e6b7c11ff8d23d26db) — Guo, Liu, Sun, Zhao · arXiv
  Sparse MoE in the transformer encoder with FiLM language conditioning routes multi-task distributions to independent expert components; multi-scale cross-attention captures low- and high-level semantics jointly. +33% success over vanilla ACT. Language-conditioned sparse activation at the *expert* level vs. SURF's language-conditioned sparse activation at the *patch* level — the granularity question: which level of language-conditioned sparsity is most effective for task-relevant visual selection?

- [Explicitly Modeling Subcortical Vision with a Neuro-Inspired Front-End Improves CNN Robustness](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d2c2818f16c45693869453594661a79b40a38dd) — Piper, Oliveira, Marques · NeurIPS
  SubcorticalBlock + VOneBlock (V1 model) front-end improves OOD robustness by 9.3% over base CNN; develops emergent shape bias; complementary to data augmentation. Adjacent to [[claims/structure helps]]: biologically-grounded V1/subcortical front-ends add shape bias and OOD robustness. Threat to SURF's line-drawing framing: if V1-parameterized filters already capture the structural signal, does a line-drawing approximation add further, or does it approximate a less faithful version of V1 selectivity? The OOD robustness axis is shared — worth comparing.

**horizon**

- [Compact deep neural network models of the visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/18d00056b2cd000d3b365ac5c17e11c885bf0b2f) — Cowley, Stan, Pillow, Smith · Nature
  Builds a highly predictive 60M-parameter V4 model via adaptive closed-loop experiments, then compresses it 5,000× to ~12,000 parameters with comparable accuracy. Reveals "consolidation" as a shared computational motif: early filters generalize, then specialize. Directly grounds [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if V4 responses are captured by 12,000 parameters, a lightweight structural prior derived from V4 organization is computationally justified. The consolidation motif (shared → specialized) mirrors SURF's prior module design: generic structural features (line drawings) + task-specific specialization (language conditioning).

- [End-to-end topographic networks as models of cortical map formation and human visual behaviour](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4b8bf7404003befc7d91df4fd193b812a8083cbc) — Lu, Doerig, Bosch, Krahmer · Nature Human Behaviour
  All-TNNs develop smooth orientation and category selectivity maps, operate on low energy budget, and outperform control models on human spatial recognition biases. Topographic organization emerges from task-driven optimization under metabolic constraints. Grounds [[claims/v4 relevance]] from first principles: topographic maps form because they are metabolically efficient for processing task-relevant spatial structure — directly motivating why a spatial prior derived from V4 topography should be structurally compact and task-aligned. The metabolic budget framing is the biological analogue of SURF's visual budget.
