**core**

- [ActPrune: Action-Grounded Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08827) — Moon et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Moves the pruning decision ahead of the vision encoder, selecting patches via an action-grounded signal before ViT computation; training-free, no structural prior, demonstrated on manipulation benchmarks. For SURF: architecturally the closest published competitor — pre-encoder, instruction-conditioned patch gating with no shape prior. The key question: does SURF's V4 shape structure add OOD robustness or task-selectivity over action-grounded scoring alone? ActPrune is the must-beat baseline for SURF's selector.

- [Artificial Foveated Perception for Mitigating Shortcut Learning in Robotic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10655) — Sun et al. · arXiv Jul 2026
  Lightweight policy-agnostic module predicting task-conditioned foveation masks over relevant objects, the robot, and affordance regions; trained supervision-free; improves generalization under layout and distractor perturbations. For SURF: foveation IS SURF's selection mechanism — AFP independently validates that task-conditioned foveation addresses the shortcut problem in VLAs. SURF's distinctive claim over AFP: V4 shape structure makes foveation mask generation more generalizable than learned attention-based masks, particularly on out-of-distribution scene structure.

- [Think Proprioceptively: State-Grounded Visual Token Selection for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06575) — Wang et al. · arXiv Feb 2026
  Discretizes proprioception into VLM-vocabulary tokens and uses them jointly with the instruction to gate visual patches before VLM computation (ThinkProprio); proprioceptive state replaces structural prior as the gating signal. For SURF: third pre-encoder competitor (ActPrune = action-grounded, ThinkProprio = state-grounded, SURF = shape-prior-grounded). Critical gap: does proprioceptive state encode enough of the action-relevance signal that SURF's structural prior adds nothing beyond it? The overlap between state-grounding and shape-prior-grounding on tabletop manipulation is the specific empirical question.

**adjacent**

- [When Listening Becomes Easier: Scrubbing Visual Cues for Shortcut-Free VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10912) — Gerigk et al. · arXiv Oct 2026
  Domain-adversarial "task scrubbing" removes task-irrelevant visual features from VLA representations and benchmarks backbone susceptibility to visual shortcuts across VLM families. For SURF: fourth competing shortcut solution (PerturBot: data augmentation; AFP: foveation; scrubbing: adversarial feature removal; SURF: structural prior). The backbone susceptibility benchmark data directly informs [[claims/structure helps]] — which base models are most vulnerable and therefore most likely to benefit from structural scaffolding.

- [Shallow to Deep: Aligning Token Pruning with Stage-wise Roles in LVLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25635) — Zhang et al. · arXiv Sep 2026
  Finds shallow ViT layers primarily perform edge detection while deep layers handle semantic reasoning; proposes STD, a hierarchical pruning framework adapted to each stage's functional role. For SURF: the edge-detection characterization of shallow layers directly supports SURF's pre-encoder placement argument — the structural information SURF's V4 prior amplifies is concentrated where edge/contour computation already occurs. Strengthens the stage-placement case but raises the question of whether SURF's prior adds over the existing edge-detection computation or duplicates it.

**horizon**

- [Energy-Driven Adaptive Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05950) — He and Chen · arXiv Mar 2026
  Determines per-image token budget from the singular value spectrum of visual features (SVD energy) rather than a fixed budget; routes saved budget to information-rich images. For SURF: SVD-energy adaptive budgeting is a complementary mechanism to structural selection — SURF selects which tokens to keep (by shape structure), E-AdaPrune decides how many to keep (by spectral energy). The question is whether the two signals are orthogonal (combine well) or correlated (each sufficient).

## risks

- **Positioning note (day 82)**: Unread queue unchanged.
- **ActPrune (new)**: Pre-encoder action-grounded pruning is training-free and competitive — if ActPrune matches SURF's accuracy at equal compression on manipulation benchmarks, the structural prior's value over pure action grounding must be demonstrated specifically on OOD/shortcut scenarios.
- **ThinkProprio (new)**: Proprioception-grounded patch gating is a competing pre-encoder design — SURF must show structural prior adds beyond state grounding (possible that state + language instruction is sufficient without V4 shape structure on tabletop manipulation).
- **Encoder choice dominance**: "Unbundling" may show patch size and pretraining objective dominate spatial performance, making the structural prior a second-order effect. Requires reading before spec.
- **ViT intrinsic binding**: "Objects as Addresses" suggests ViTs already have structural binding — SURF's prior must demonstrate it adds over this, not replaces it.
- **Frontier zero-shot baseline**: Reins (GPT-6.1, Claude Opus 5.5 zero-shot robot control) is a no-training competitor not in the original positioning. SURF's efficiency claims need this as a baseline.
- **Be My Eye baseline**: Task-prediction compression without structural prior achieves dual-robot VLA results — challenges the strict necessity of line-drawing prior for task-conditioned selection.
- **ACTSB benchmark**: Eval design underspecified if SURF can't evaluate on ACTSB-style queries.
- **Pre-encoder placement validity**: Three concurrent challenges (NEO-VLA, Herr et al., Shi et al. Stage-Aware) unresolved.
