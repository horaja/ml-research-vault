Today's digest arrives with two papers that directly pressure [[claims/language helps]] from opposite directions. **SparseVLM+** (Zhang et al., 2026-07-29) is a training-free text-guided token optimizer that achieves competitive reduction in VLMs without structural priors — if it transfers to VLA manipulation frames, it is a go/no-go baseline for whether language conditioning alone is sufficient and structural geometry is the separating factor. **SlimVLM** (Wen et al., 2026-08-04) combines sensitivity-aware structured model pruning with adaptive visual token selection; it is a different architectural attack on the same efficiency problem, worth characterizing as a baseline for surf's token selection stage.

A new neuroscience paper from CMU's own lab (Henderson, Tarr, Wehbe; 2026-05-15) directly operationalizes the higher visual cortex map using diffusion-based stimulus optimization. Their BrainDiVE framework synthesizes images predicted to strongly activate specific cortical regions, revealing fine-grained functional organization across V1–IT. This is the most methodologically concrete handle on [[claims/v4 relevance]] to date: the same framework applied to manipulation-style stimuli could produce a per-patch V4 relevance score usable as a selection prior.

Direct competing method for surf's token selection stage — their adaptive selector uses attention sensitivity, not structural geometry. Add to spec baseline suite alongside RoRA and CogVLA.

- [SparseVLM+: Visual Token Sparsification With Improved Text-Visual Attention Pattern](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a54bd69aa751389cf0f0bb7566234e6633589c25) — Zhang et al. · 2026-07-29
  Training-free text-guided token optimizer that identifies which visual tokens the text cross-attends to most strongly and drops the rest. 2 citations. Core pressure on [[claims/language helps]]: if SparseVLM+ with pure text-attention guidance achieves competitive VLA manipulation results, it isolates whether structural geometry adds anything beyond text attention — exactly the "language-only baseline" the spec's ablation must beat. Closer read needed before writing that ablation section.

- [GuidedAttention: Interpretable and Correctable Visual Attention for OOD-Robust Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e21e550b5e561747284e9e919d53319b78cb9bf7) — Murooka et al. · 2026-07-23
  Predicts task-relevant spatial keypoints from camera images as an explicit intermediate representation, then conditions a diffusion-based policy on those keypoints. OOD robustness gains are attributed to the structural bottleneck (keypoints force policy to rely on spatially stable features). Convergent evidence for surf's interpretable spatial intermediate representation design: their keypoints from RGB images are the appearance-based analog to surf's line-density-based structural prior. Key difference: their keypoints are learned, task-specific, and require training data; surf's structural prior is training-free. The OOD robustness result supports [[claims/structure helps]] from a different mechanism.

**adjacent**

- [History-Conditioned Spatio-Temporal Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language Navigation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4755e2c70d9686aeb663bcb341c339a8edc6df33) — Wang et al. · 2026-03-06
  Training-free spatio-temporal token pruning for VLN (navigation, not manipulation): instruction-conditioned visual tokens are dropped by combining spatial attention saliency with temporal redundancy across frames. 1 citation. - [Thinking outside the frame: viewpoint-aware spatial reasoning in large multimodal models](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2712b482e221543467b6e9a41dc44bed144be3b0) — Ke et al. · 2026-08-12

Relevant to the viewpoint robustness axis: surf's line-geometry structural prior is more viewpoint-invariant than RGB attention, and this paper provides a reasoning-level mechanism that may compound with token-level fragility.

## questions

- SparseVLM+ × [[claims/language helps]] isolability: SparseVLM+ achieves language-guided token reduction by identifying visual tokens with high cross-attention to text queries, without any structural prior. On VLMs, this is competitive with trained methods. On LIBERO manipulation frames where the instruction ("pick the cup to the right") specifies a spatial region, does text cross-attention converge on the same tokens as line-density + instruction-conditioned scoring? If they largely agree, surf's structural prior is redundant; if they diverge on affordance boundaries and contact surfaces, surf's differential claim is grounded.
- SlimVLM × token selection architecture: SlimVLM scores attention heads by their sensitivity to visual tokens, preserving heads with high visual sensitivity. Does this head-level selection interact with surf's patch-level selection? Are there heads that are uniformly sensitive to all visual tokens (surf's selection can drop some) vs. heads sensitive only to specific patch positions (surf's selection must preserve those)? This determines whether SlimVLM-style pruning is composable with surf's patch selection or competing.
- GuidedAttention × [[claims/structure helps]] mechanism: GuidedAttention learns task-relevant keypoints from training demonstrations; surf uses line-density as a training-free structural prior. Both produce spatial intermediate representations, but through different mechanisms. Do GuidedAttention keypoints and line-density-selected tokens co-locate on LIBERO manipulation frames? If yes, this would be convergent validation. If they diverge, it reveals whether structural geometry captures something different from task-demonstrated saliency.
- Henderson et al. × [[claims/v4 relevance]] operationalization: BrainDiVE synthesizes images that maximally activate V4 ROIs. Running BrainDiVE on manipulation-style frames (or using V4-activating image features as a filter) could produce per-patch V4 relevance scores without neural recording data. Is this feasible given @jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying's digital twin, or does BrainDiVE require the full neural recording setup (fMRI/calcium imaging data as training signal)? What resolution would a BrainDiVE-derived V4 prior achieve on LIBERO patch grids?
- Zhao et al. × active vision formalism: The information-dependent eye-hand coordination model formalizes saccade timing as uncertainty reduction. Is this objective function the correct analytic framing for surf's visual budget allocation — i.e., should surf's selection criterion be "reduce visual uncertainty about task state" rather than "maximize structural prior coverage"? How does this change the contrastive training objective?
