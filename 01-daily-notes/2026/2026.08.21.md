## questions

- GS-VLA × spec architecture (day 2 of reading): GS-VLA uses 4M-param 3DGS to canonicalize the 3D observation before passing to a frozen VLA. Surf selects which 2D patches to process from the raw observation. Are these composable (canonicalize, then select) or substitutes (canonical view renders structural prior redundant)? This is the core differentiation question blocking the architecture section.
- Contrastive scorer granularity: at what instruction level should the scorer condition — full instruction, sub-task, patch-level token? Hybrid-Level Injection (day 4) addresses this directly. The answer determines scorer architecture.
- Cao et al. (day 3): is the mechanism attention-salience-based (same design space as SparseVLM+, AtVLA) or contrastive? If contrastive + language-conditioned at patch level, surf's differentiation reduces to the geometric structural prior alone.
- QWM (day 3) × [[claims/iterative selection]]: can QWM's world-model value signal serve as the trigger for adaptive visual compute rather than uncertainty? If the same world model that selects actions could identify which visual tokens mattered for high-Q actions, surf's iterative selection and QWM's adaptive search could share the same value objective.
- Neural signatures × [[eval/gaze]] (day 2): Pourrahimi & Bashivan's saccadic model produces human-like scanpaths on novel objects. If its fixation sequence matches surf's line-density selected patches on manipulation frames, is that converging evidence for structural salience, or just evidence that any reasonable attention model tracks geometric regions?
