**core**

- [Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock? Temporal Shortcuts in Memory-Augmented VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488) — Zhang et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Uniform temporal frame sampling + timestamps let VLA policies learn to count steps instead of track task progress; a null event (brief pause, wrist fidget) that changes nothing task-relevant still triggers a changed prediction. The paper proposes task-conditioned frame selection as the fix. For SURF: this is experimental evidence that naive temporal selection is insufficient and that task conditioning is not just an efficiency trick — it's necessary for semantic correctness of the visual context. Strengthens the argument behind [[02-projects/surf/claims/language helps]] and task-conditioned selection more broadly.

- [Elastic-WAM: Adaptive Spatiotemporal Imagination for Efficient Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14257) — Li et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  World-Action Models with adaptive refinement budgets: preferred refinement depths vary across tasks and states, and different future regions vary in relevance to action generation. Proposes a task/state-adaptive budget rather than fixed refinement depth. For SURF: direct empirical support for [[02-projects/surf/claims/iterative selection]] from robot manipulation — the finding that compute depth should be task/state-conditioned, not fixed, is exactly the adaptive budget argument SURF is building toward.

- [C²-Routing: Complementary Cropping and Compression for Efficient Vision-Language Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14469) — Lu et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Model-agnostic VLM input reduction using complementary cropping (spatial zoom) + compression (token reduction) strategies; enforcing a visual-token cap while preserving question-answering accuracy. For SURF: this is a direct model-agnostic competitor for [[02-projects/surf/claims/efficiency without collapse]] — it doesn't use a structural prior, just complementary geometry-free strategies. SURF needs to show structural priors provide something cropping + compression cannot.

**adjacent**

- [LaST0.5: A Generalist World Action Model with Interaction-Centric Latent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14048) — Zhang et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  World Action Model finding that global scene representations underemphasize local robot-object interactions; proposes interaction-centric latent reasoning as an architectural fix. For SURF: the core argument here — that global scene reps are mismatched to manipulation requirements — is precisely the motivation for SURF's selective visual attention. Useful framing for the positioning note.

- [Where Do Vision-Language Models Get Left and Right? Position-Code Counterfactuals in Qwen2.5-VL, Qwen3-VL and InternVL3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14060) — Cui et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Ablations show ViT position codes are causally necessary for VLMs to correctly resolve spatial directions; LLM position codes are insufficient. Mirroring ViT width positions reproduces the flipped answer on 84–93% of spatial queries. For SURF: directly answers the open question in [[02-projects/surf/questions]] (architecture section): yes, patch-level position codes in the ViT are load-bearing for spatial reasoning. Any patch selection scheme that breaks positional structure — e.g., global-semantic selection that ignores spatial layout — risks eroding this. Supports pre-encoder, structure-preserving selection over post-encoder re-ordering.

- [EVAThink: Evidence Sufficiency-Guided Training for Adaptive Thinking in Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13907) — Wu et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Under a fixed frame budget, whether sampled frames provide sufficient evidence determines whether direct answering vs. extended reasoning is better. Trains a VLM to adaptively think more when evidence is insufficient. For SURF: "evidence sufficiency" operationalizes [[02-projects/surf/claims/iterative selection]]'s stopping criterion — select until evidence sufficiency exceeds a threshold, not until a fixed budget is exhausted. The paper's training strategy (evidence gap mining) is a potential approach to learning when to refine in SURF.

**horizon**

- [When Does the Real Wiring Win? Testing connectome structure-function claims against unmeasured physiology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14508) — Liu · arXiv Nov 2026
  Separates three experimental protocols for testing whether real wiring outperforms shuffled/rewired nulls, finding that the verdict depends strongly on whether physiology is tuned independently on the null condition or transferred from the real-wiring model. In one Drosophila circuit, the real-wiring model loses to a cross-subtype shuffle under the fair protocol (P3). For SURF: the same confound threatens [[02-projects/surf/claims/structure helps]] — if experiments comparing structural prior vs. random baseline tune hyperparameters on the structural prior's performance, the advantage may reflect tuning, not structure. Before writing the eval spec, read this paper for concrete baseline protocol design.
