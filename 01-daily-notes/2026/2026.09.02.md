## papers

### digest (auto)

**Core**

- @wang2026histCondVLNPrune — *History-Conditioned Spatio-Temporal Visual Token Pruning for Efficient VLN* (Wang et al., arXiv 2603.06480). Training-free spatio-temporal token pruning for VLA-based navigation; applies spatial selection per step and temporal pruning conditioned on navigation history. Directly relevant to [[claims/iterative selection]]: history conditioning for temporal consistency is the same mechanism SURF needs, tested in a VLA navigation loop.

- @cheng2026interactionAlignedPrune — *Training-Free Interaction-Aligned Visual Token Pruning for Embodied Manipulation* (Cheng et al., arXiv 2603.22991). Proposes "interaction alignment" as selection criterion — tokens where instruction-related appearance and action-space motion co-occur spatially — arguing semantic relevance and motion signals alone discard task-critical tokens when they are spatially misaligned with each other. Direct competitor to SURF's structural prior as a training-free signal; worth checking whether interaction alignment subsumes edge structure in practice.

- @jia2026S2Prune — *S²Prune: Spatially Structured Visual Token Pruning* (Jia et al., arXiv 2609.01224, Sep 2026). Key empirical finding: importance-based and redundancy-based pruning criteria produce stable spatial biases across inputs and do not consistently outperform Uniform Grid sampling; proposes spatial coverage objective instead. Strongest current challenge to [[claims/structure helps]] — if structural signals reduce to center-weighted biases, SURF needs an ablation against center-bias-corrected grid before the claim can stand.

**Adjacent**

- @lin2026TADP — *TADP: Task-Aware Dynamic Visual Token Pruning for VLA Inference* (Lin et al., RTCSA 2026 poster). Training-free dynamic pruning for VLA: estimates task-conditioned importance after early vision-language interaction, decouples main- and wrist-view budgets, reuses keyframe scores under temporal consistency. Architecture note: multi-view budget decoupling is a design detail SURF will need.

- @he2026DiffPrune — *DiffPrune: Differentiable Information Throttling for Token Pruning* (He et al., arXiv 2608.01985). Replaces Gumbel-Softmax with a differentiable selector that scores tokens by direct information-loss consequence rather than a relaxed proxy. Relevant if SURF trains a selection module: "information throttle" framing maps to SURF's rate-distortion angle and provides a cleaner gradient signal than KL-divergence approximations.

- @zhang2026conditionalDiversity — *Beyond Attention or Similarity: Conditional Diversity for Token Pruning* (Zhang et al., NeurIPS 38, arXiv 2506.10967). Argues attention-based pruning retains duplicates and similarity-based pruning ignores instruction relevance; proposes instruction-conditioned diversity maximization. NeurIPS result — lends theoretical weight to [[claims/language helps]] by showing instruction conditioning strictly improves over both attention and similarity baselines.

**Horizon**

- @sawada2026PredVLA — *PredVLA: Sub-Million-Parameter Predictive-Coding Policy* (Sawada & Kasahara, arXiv 2608.26673). 0.68M-parameter predictive-coding VLA with hierarchical recurrent generative dynamics; claims competitive performance with large pretrained VLAs on manipulation benchmarks. Raises a falsifier question for SURF: if tiny predictive-coding architectures match VLA performance, does token pruning in billion-parameter VLAs solve the right problem? Predictive coding vs. token pruning as efficiency strategies are not obviously complementary.

- @guo2026TokenBudgetDistill — *Token-Budget Distillation* (Guo et al., arXiv 2608.28138). PEFT framework for adapting video VLMs under a fixed token budget; uses full-token-model distillation to prevent semantic drift under compression. Relevant to [[claims/efficiency without collapse]]: TBD demonstrates that distillation — not pruning alone — is required to maintain semantics at high compression ratios.

## questions

- Does S²Prune's spatial-bias finding hold in VLA closed-loop settings where the task-relevant region is instruction-dependent, or only in static VQA where center bias dominates?
- Is SURF's Canny-edge prior just a non-uniform spatial weighting that collapses to center bias when edges concentrate at center? How would an ablation that corrects for center bias be structured?
- DiffPrune's information throttle: what is their distortion metric — is it cross-entropy on the downstream task or KL on token distributions? This matters for whether their rate-distortion framing aligns with SURF's.
