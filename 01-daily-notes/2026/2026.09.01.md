**core**

- [Attention from Action, for Action: Emergent Visual Bottlenecks for Policy Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/97c2db623247367a7622c4c8b0c79bb41f098dcc) — Zhuang et al. · arXiv
  "Seeker": task- and state-conditioned readout that learns action-relevant ROIs from action supervision alone, without structural labels, language priors, or spatial annotations. Claims label-free visual bottleneck for visuomotor policies. Most direct competitor to SURF's structural + language prior: if action labels alone produce task-relevant ROIs, SURF needs to show what structural geometry and language add — likely contact affordances and instruction-specific localization not recoverable from the action trajectory itself. Read before writing positioning note.

- [How Should Vision-Language-Action Models Use Proprioceptive State?](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/288011bcf545a3e17b79430e9fd9e6982ae520b5) — Zhao et al. · arXiv
  Systematic ablation of how proprioceptive state is wired into VLA models: serialized text, projected prefix, or direct to action expert; single frame vs. history. Extends Think Proprioceptively (Aug 30) with concrete wiring comparisons. Determines the correct proprioceptive baseline for isolating SURF's structural contribution; the winning wiring scheme becomes the baseline SURF must beat on held-out instruction-object combinations.

- [ACTIVE-o3: Empowering MLLMs with Active Perception via Pure Reinforcement Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/aae1df4156ae2088e9abb5905902b1da7eb7b6bf) — Zhu et al. · arXiv
  GRPO-based RL framework that trains active perception in MLLMs without architectural priors: the model learns where and how to look from task rewards alone. If pure RL induces active perception, SURF's structural prior and contrastive alignment become a training-time shortcut (better data efficiency, faster convergence) rather than an architectural necessity. Sharpens the novelty framing: does SURF's prior improve generalization to held-out instructions, or is it primarily a sample-efficiency advantage?

**adjacent**

- [Look, Zoom, Understand: The Robotic Eyeball for Embodied Perception](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2c0375bf1aaa5637453373a5bd9b267bfdaab26d) — Yang et al. · arXiv
  EyeVLA: physical camera control (pan, zoom) for instruction-driven active visual acquisition in embodied AI; treats camera actuation as a budget mechanism orthogonal to token pruning. SURF selects which patches to process within a fixed FOV; EyeVLA selects what the sensor captures. The two compose — EyeVLA reduces sensor-level redundancy, SURF reduces processing-level redundancy within the acquired frame. Useful for positioning SURF at the compute-budget layer, not the sensing layer.

- [Gaze on the Prize: Shaping Visual Attention with Return-Guided Contrastive Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ac43acda376c1d2a4c79f236046064b9bb062df8) — Lee et al. · arXiv
  Learnable foveal attention in visual RL shaped by a return-guided contrastive signal: high-return vs. low-return visual experience grounds attention in task outcomes. RL analogue of SURF's contrastive alignment — SURF aligns patches to language instructions; this aligns gaze to task returns. If return-guided contrastive learning captures task-relevant attention in manipulation RL, SURF's language + structure grounding adds prior knowledge to bootstrap without environmental interaction — a data-efficiency framing for [[claims/language helps]].

**horizon**

- [A single computational objective can produce specialization of streams in visual cortex](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f673f711af4d48c03135e75c4cf08a74fa30e653) — Finzi, Margalit, Kay et al. · bioRxiv
  Self-supervised TDANN (topographic DNN) produces dorsal/lateral/ventral stream specialization from a single objective; topographic pressure alone drives functional stream segregation without specialized training. Updates [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if stream specialization arises from topographic pressure, the visual regularities SURF's line/shape prior captures may reflect topographically stable structure — suggesting SURF's V4 grounding predicts which features are computationally robust, not just which ones V4 happens to be sensitive to.
