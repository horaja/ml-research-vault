# 2026-10-20

## opener

Positioning note is day 56. Two days of candidates land together.

Three new threads. **SPAR** is the most structurally important: it compresses π₀.5's LLM backbone from 2.34B to 0.09B parameters (96% reduction) and uses RL to recover from 84.55% to 93.45% LIBERO success at 2x speedup. This sets the new baseline target — SURF's structural prior must demonstrate additive gains on a 1.12B total-parameter compressed model, not the 3.37B+ models the field was evaluating against last month. The RL recovery result also raises an open question about training: if aggressive structural compression requires RL recovery, does SURF's contrastive alignment objective need a similar post-compression RL phase?

**SAPrune** provides direct evidence for [[02-projects/surf/claims/iterative selection]]: attention patterns for token importance don't stabilize until language instruction integrates across several LLM layers. Pruning before that point discards tokens that become important after cross-modal fusion. If SURF's structural prior is applied pre-encoder (before the LLM sees language), it may operate at the wrong stage. SAPrune's 87.5% token reduction at 1.718x speedup on LIBERO constrains SURF's efficiency claims — the efficiency bar is already set there.

**TLC-DiT** enters as the most direct architectural competitor to SURF's language-conditioned prior module: it adds an explicit FiLM+CoordConv spatial feature map conditioned on CLIP task embeddings as a plug-in to diffusion policy, reaching 93.5% LIBERO. TLC-DiT's task-aligned spatial maps are exactly what [[02-projects/surf/claims/language helps]] claims SURF's prior should produce. The question: does SURF's geometric structural prior (line drawings) add to TLC-DiT's learned maps on novel objects where CLIP statistics fail, or does TLC-DiT already cover the affordance-relevant regions?

Cao et al. is day 21. Read today.

**core**

- [SPAR: Structural Pruning and Action Recovery for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26487v1) — arXiv 2026-10-19
  Structural pruning of π₀.5's LLM backbone from 2.34B to 0.09B parameters (96% reduction), followed by RL recovery (84.55% → 93.45% LIBERO average, +8.9pp) at 2x inference speedup. New compressed-model performance floor for [[02-projects/surf/experiments/contrastive alignment]]: SURF's structural prior must show additive gains on a 1.12B total-parameter model, not the 3.37B baseline. The RL recovery result also raises a training-objective question — whether SURF's contrastive alignment alone is sufficient post-compression, or whether an RL recovery phase is needed.

- [TLC-DiT: Task-Aligned Local Visual Conditioning for Robust Multitask Robot Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/519e831fb8ede93c653bc2b157a275badb65a10e) — Liu et al. · preprint 2026-09-28
  Plug-in extension of diffusion policy: frozen DINOv2 patch features modulated by CLIP task embedding (FiLM) + lightweight CoordConv CNN → explicit task-aligned spatial feature maps concatenated as conditioning. 93.5% LIBERO (+7pp over Multitask DiT), larger gains under camera/background perturbations. Closest existing instantiation of [[02-projects/surf/claims/language helps]]: TLC-DiT constructs task-conditioned spatial maps without any geometric prior. Check whether SURF's line-drawing prior adds on top of TLC-DiT's learned maps on novel objects where CLIP query-target statistics fail — if TLC-DiT already covers affordance-relevant regions, SURF's structural prior is justified only for novel-object generalization.

- [When and What to Prune? Stage-Aware Visual Token Pruning for Efficient VLA (SAPrune)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/11187459e83137556bc7d856ec19e256d7872a06) — Chen et al. · preprint 2026-10-04
  Training-free visual token pruning for VLA that delays pruning until attention-pattern stabilization (cross-modal fusion of language + vision complete); dual-path rule protects attended tokens and surrounding context; 87.5% token reduction, 1.718x speedup on LIBERO/SIMPLER/real robot. Directly supports [[02-projects/surf/claims/iterative selection]]: token importance is language-context-dependent and unstable in early LLM layers. If SURF's structural prior operates pre-encoder (before language integrates), it may prune tokens that become critical after cross-modal fusion — the correct intervention point may be mid-LLM, not at the visual encoder.

**adjacent**

- [Compact Robot Policies Need Fine-Grained Visual Representations (CoRP)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/60ec1e5d7adce0108658ac2eaa1af20eb2df5657) — Lee et al. · preprint 2026-10-06
  Ablation-controlled compact policy (48.9M params, no VLM, no generative prior) achieves 97% LIBERO; hard token budget (resampling to 48 tokens) beats variational information bottleneck: VIB over tokens cuts LIBERO-Goal from 95.8% to 33.0%. Language conditioning only helps where observation leaves goal ambiguous; on unambiguous tasks (RoboTwin 2.0), removing language slightly improves success. Adjacent to [[02-projects/surf/claims/language helps]] and [[02-projects/surf/experiments/contrastive alignment]]: VIB as selection objective is worse than hard budget — SURF's contrastive selection should produce deterministic/hard selections, not soft probabilistic ones. Language helps only under visual ambiguity, which is exactly the novel-object regime SURF targets.

- [PrismWAM: Decoupling State Representations in World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26564v1) — Zou et al. · arXiv 2026-10-19
  Factorizes world state into semantic (appearance) and physical (occupancy/pose) components; physical rollout in depth space, semantic planning in RGB/goal-image space; achieves manipulation SOTA without embodied pretraining. Adjacent to [[02-projects/surf/claims/structure helps]]: depth-space physical rollout achieves strong manipulation performance, reinforcing the case that geometric/structural representations capture action-relevant dynamics better than RGB appearance. Check whether PrismWAM's depth rollout activates the same spatial regions as SURF's line-drawing prior on manipulation scenes — if they co-locate on contact/affordance regions, depth and line drawings are redundant structural priors; if they diverge, they're complementary.

**horizon**

- [Scaling Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning for Brain Encoding (BrainLoRA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26802v1) — Benchetrit et al. · arXiv 2026-10-19
  LoRA adapts pretrained multimodal backbones (up to 104B parameters) for neural response prediction with minimal trainable parameters; modality attribution recovers canonical sensory topography without construction from separate unimodal encoders; achieves SOTA on Algonauts 2025 Challenge. Direct operationalization hook for [[02-projects/surf/claims/v4 relevance]]: if LoRA on a pretrained visual backbone (DINOv2, CLIP) can predict V4 column-level responses with few parameters, SURF's structural prior could be derived from a V4-adapted readout rather than hand-crafted line drawings. The modality attribution showing sensory topography as emergent from adaptation confirms that pretrained backbones already encode the right features — low-rank adaptation isolates them.

## risks

- **Positioning note (day 56)**: TLC-DiT is now the most direct language-conditioned prior competitor. The note must distinguish SURF on the novel-object axis: TLC-DiT's CLIP-conditioned maps fail when query-target CLIP statistics are absent for novel objects — SURF's geometric prior covers that regime. Write today.
- **SAPrune stage finding (new)**: If token importance stabilizes mid-LLM (not pre-encoder), SURF's structural prior applied at the visual encoder stage may operate before the useful information is available. Structural priors should either (a) pre-select coarsely at the encoder and refine post-language-integration, or (b) be applied mid-LLM as a geometric attention bias. This is a design decision the spec must address.
- **CoRP VIB failure (new)**: VIB over tokens degrades LIBERO-Goal by 62.8 points. If SURF's contrastive alignment produces a probabilistic selection, this is a known failure mode. Hard k-sparse selection is the empirically validated alternative.
- **SPAR compressed model floor (new)**: Performance floor is now 93.45% on LIBERO from a 1.12B model. SURF must show additive gains on this, not on the 7B OpenVLA baseline.
- **TLC-DiT as prior competitor (new)**: TLC-DiT at 93.5% is almost identical to SPAR's recovered performance (93.45%). If SURF's language-conditioned prior fails to beat TLC-DiT on standard benchmarks, the differentiation must be novel-object generalization and explicit geometric interpretability.
