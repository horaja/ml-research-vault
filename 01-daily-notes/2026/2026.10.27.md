- [RAIN: Robotic Task Generalization with Region-Aware Interaction Networks](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/423a259760d043b1292fd130658babbe4027c5ed) — Lee et al. · arXiv 2610.14039

Direct architectural reference for SURF's prior module: compare region-gating selection patterns against SURF's patch-level line-based selection and check geometric coherence of selected regions.

**adjacent**

- [PerturBot: Breaking Shortcut Priors in VLAs with Perturbative Training](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/26de490d3dc0ebb6c0ed53c3d53ea44a3ce72fdd) — Liu et al. · arXiv 2610.04616

Non-VLA validation of the structural-prior approach: object boundaries as the selection signal are the closest non-line-drawing analog to SURF's line prior. Check patch-selection overlap and degradation pattern on novel objects.

- [VAttnPO: Visual Attention-Guided Policy Optimization](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4061edbc30b59f168c3429c17ae0517154d4b60b) — Zheng et al. · arXiv 2610.16735

Adjacent to SURF's iterative selection design under [[claims/iterative selection]]: soft attention-based credit vs. hard structural selection — does soft RL credit align with the geometric tokens SURF's prior selects?

## questions

Carried:
- SAPrune: At what LLM layer does token importance stabilize? Is SURF's pre-encoder structural prior a valid coarse filter or misplaced?
- Attention from Action: What is the novel-object failure mode of action-supervision-derived ROIs? At what distribution-shift threshold does label-free ROI emergence degrade, and does SURF's geometric prior cover that regime?
- FastVLA: Does temporal adaptation of visual sparsity require per-step recomputation of the token selector, or can SURF's geometric prior serve as a stable per-step base with iterative refinement on top?
- VLA-ACL: Does action-consistency supervision select structurally coherent regions, or semantically scattered ones? Does adding SURF's geometric prior to action-consistency supervision yield orthogonal coverage?
- CoRP: Does VIB failure on LIBERO-Goal mean SURF's contrastive alignment must produce hard selections?

New (from today):
- Shi et al. (Stage-Aware): Does the per-stage pruning efficacy curve show a distinct inflection after language-fusion, or is degradation gradual? Does the pattern differ for novel-object vs. in-distribution scenes?
- RAIN (Lee et al.): Does region-aware gating select geometrically coherent regions (compatible with line-drawing prior) or semantically diffuse ones (suggesting independent mechanisms)?
- Issa et al.: Is V4 population code diversity measurable from the digital-twin calcium dataset (@wangLargescaleCalciumImaging2024), and does it correlate with the dispersity metric in @jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying?
