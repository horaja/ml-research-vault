**core**

- [APS-VLA: Action-Guided and Future-Aware Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13030) — Xing, Feng, Cohn, Gong, Nandi · arXiv
  Uses ground-truth action signals during training to identify which visual tokens are action-relevant, plus a future-prediction auxiliary loss biasing selection toward tokens that predict the next scene state. Post-encoder, action-guided: requires action signal availability during training but not inference. SURF's difference is structural: pre-encoder V4 shape prior operates with no action signal at inference and without training-distribution dependence. The critical comparison is OOD generalization — does a V4 structural prior outperform action-guided training priors when manipulation configurations shift?

- [Phoenix: Learning Scene Structure Representations for Generalizable World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24938) — Zheng, Li, Wang, Li et al. · arXiv
  Augments a DINOv3-based world-action model with representations of scene spatial relationships and object-state changes relevant to manipulation. Learned scene structure as the mid-level representational layer between raw pixels and action — competes with SURF's V4 shape prior on the same layer. Key question: does Phoenix's learned structure generalize better on novel objects/backgrounds, or does grounding in primate V4 mid-level vision confer a robustness advantage? Must be in the positioning note's related work alongside AIM.

**adjacent**

- [Z-sight: Unifying Vision-Language-Action and Latent World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24940) — Ye, Zhao, Xu, Wang et al. · arXiv
  Couples VLA with generative latent world modeling, avoiding pixel generation while preserving future-prediction structure as an implicit spatial signal. The latent dynamics model provides structural inductive bias without an explicit prior. For SURF: if latent world models already capture the spatial structure needed for action planning, the case for SURF's explicit V4 prior must specify what it adds beyond what future-latent dynamics provide — particularly in low-data regimes.

- [RECLAIM: Robot-Vision-Informed Adversarial Patch Defense via Embodied Attention Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24494) — Zhu, Guo, Zhang et al. · arXiv
  Adversarial patches corrupt VLA action predictions by corrupting the observation loop; uses the policy's own attention maps (embodied attention feedback) to localize and suppress patches without auxiliary models. Adjacent to SURF's shortcut robustness claim: adversarial patch robustness and visual shortcut robustness are both failures from spurious high-activation non-structure. SURF's structural prior reduces both by biasing selection toward shape-defined regions, but the embodied-attention defense is a competing post-hoc approach.

**horizon**

- [Investigating action topography in visual cortex and deep artificial neural networks](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c87a943995f33c4efc8a3b64d9c2358e3d314649) — 5 citations · bioRxiv (Aug 2025)
  Examines whether action tuning — not just visual feature selectivity — organizes topographic maps in V4 and in trained deep networks. If action structure drives V4 topography, then V4-derived shape priors carry manipulation-relevant geometry by design, not just by analogy. Direct complementary evidence for [[claims/v4 relevance]] alongside Willeke et al. — read together before writing that section.
