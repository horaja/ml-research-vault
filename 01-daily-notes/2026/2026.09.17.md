**core**

- [Think Proprioceptively: State-Grounded Visual Token Selection for VLA Policies](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ce0a7201ebc0b4804a7f28d3536338e57e5c345) — Wang, Zhou, Qi · S2
  Uses proprioceptive robot state to ground visual token selection — selecting which patches to attend to based on where the robot is in its task, not structural geometry or language. Positions against SURF as the third grounding axis: structural (SURF), language, and proprioceptive state. If proprioceptive grounding alone achieves task-relevant selection, the burden on SURF's line-drawing prior is to show what it adds before any action history exists (pre-deployment, novel environments, first-shot generalization).

- [Investigating action topography in visual cortex and deep artificial neural networks](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c87a943995f33c4efc8a3b64d9c2358e3d314649) — Cortinovis, Truong, Op de Beeck · S2
  Proposes action as a key topographic organizing factor in high-level visual cortex, alongside animacy and real-world size. If action-tuned regions are spatially clustered in cortex, this directly grounds [[claims/v4 relevance]]: SURF's structural prior captures geometry that correlates with those action-tuned regions. Connects the V4 topo paper (shape/texture columns) to an action-relevance axis — the missing link between V4 organization and patch selection for manipulation.

**adjacent**

- [rMuscle: Robotic Muscle Memory for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Model Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19104v1) — Zhou, Li, Chen, Gu · arXiv 2026
  Caches observation-action patterns to skip redundant visual processing for repetitive factory manipulation. Competing efficiency paradigm: cache-based redundancy reduction vs. SURF's structure-based spatial selection. Differentiation axis: caching is powerful for high-repetition ID tasks; structural priors are data-free and available at first-deployment in novel environments before any action history is accumulated.

- [SAVLA: Symmetry-Aware Vision-Language-Action Models for Robotic Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d1f93e6c5590ad9a52d8908f356fd1f264dcb0f5) — Li, Li, Wu · S2
  Incorporates geometric symmetry (equivariance) as an inductive bias in VLA manipulation policies. Different geometric prior from SURF's structural contour approach — symmetry constraints vs. local edge/line geometry. Comparison question: symmetry priors capture global geometric invariances; line-drawing priors capture local structural salience. Under what task distribution does each dominate, and can they compose?

- [Learning task-adaptive visual-language relevancy for language-conditioned manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/78fa37093cd4c0f6cd64b78006220edbf8f05919) — Luo, Wu, Liu · S2
  Learns dynamic visual-language attention alignment for language-conditioned manipulation, addressing VLM attention misalignment on task-relevant objects. Directly tests [[claims/language helps]] without structural priors: if learned language-visual relevancy alone corrects manipulation attention, SURF's structural contribution needs a harder axis — OOD generalization, pre-deployment, or novel object categories where learned alignment fails.

**horizon**

- [Deep learning-driven characterization of single cell tuning in primate visual area V4 supports topological organization](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f019f4561cd9689cdea12526e7464d3b242302a4) — Willeke, Restivo, Franke et al. · S2
  DL models trained on single V4 neuron responses reveal topological organization of tuning properties within V4. Extends [[claims/v4 relevance]] from the population level (calcium imaging, Kohonen SOMs in @jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying) to the single-cell level: if V4 neurons are topologically organized by shape/edge tuning, SURF's structural prior is a proxy for the per-patch V4 response pattern. The digital twin question (@jiangComputationalConstraintsUnderlying): is the dispersity measure in the Kohonen SOM consistent with what single-cell tuning maps show here?
