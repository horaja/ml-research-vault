**core**

- [VLALagBench: Understanding When and Why VLA Inference Latency Matters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03103) — Wen et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Systematic benchmark for VLA latency sensitivity: 100ms can halve task success on moving targets; sensitivity varies by task type and policy. Empirical predictor maps latency → success from limited calibration, enabling algorithm-system co-design. This is the empirical floor SURF's efficiency claim must demonstrate against, and the per-task sensitivity data determines which evaluation tasks SURF should prioritize for latency measurement. Read to extract which manipulation task categories are most sensitive.

- [R²-Omni: Reducing Structural and Precision Redundancy in Omni-LLMs for Long-Form Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03247) — Feng et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Query-guided token selection (removing structural redundancy) preserves enough evidence for recognition but drops diversity needed for long-form generation; introduces spatiotemporal-similarity consolidation (STC) + semantic-sensitivity precision allocation (SSPA) achieving 5× memory reduction at 97.98% quality. Directly relevant to the contrastive alignment spec: if SURF's instruction-conditioned pruning over-specializes to task-predictive patches, it may degrade action generation quality the same way — the structural prior's task-agnostic first pass is a partial mitigation, but the failure mode needs an explicit test.

**adjacent**

- [FineCraft: Enhancing VLA Models for Fine-Grained Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03163) — Zhang et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Task-routed mixture: DINOv2-DiT local features for instructed object-part identification + π₀.5 backbone, scoring 0.878 on MetaFine Challenge (rank 4). Local visual features for instruction grounding is a competing approach to SURF's structural priors — achieved through task-level routing rather than patch-level pre-encoder selection. Read to understand when local-feature routing outperforms structural patch selection and whether the two are complementary.

- [Seeing Through the Illusion: Test-Time Scaling for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03585) — Ahmed et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Pre-execution rollout selector combining action validity + temporal regularity + instruction-conditioned task progress; raises RoboLab success from 6% to 24% with Cosmos 3 Edge. Selection at rollout-level is coarser than SURF's patch-level work, but the instruction-conditioned task-progress score is language-conditioned selection at action-trajectory granularity. The per-score ablation — whether instruction-conditioned progress drives most of the gain — is the critical read for [[claims/language helps]].

**horizon**

- [Structured Visual Landscape: Generating Preferred Representations in Biological and Artificial Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03597) — Zhao et al. · arXiv Nov 2026
  Generates fMRI-grounded preferred visual images for biological cortex and artificial network units via activation-regularized VAE + diffusion (Vis-Lens). Relevant to [[claims/v4 relevance]]: if the framework is applied to V4-responsive units, the generated preferred images characterize what V4 prefers to see — structural/edge content vs. texture/color. Read to check whether V4-preferred images look like mid-level structure, which would provide activation-level evidence for line-drawing priors.
