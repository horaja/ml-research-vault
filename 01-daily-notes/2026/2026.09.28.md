# 2026-09-28

## opener

SAVLA and DualManip together define two positions on the geometric-prior design space that SURF inhabits, and together they narrow the positioning note's geometry axis to something writable. SAVLA keeps the pretrained VL backbone entirely frozen and adds an equivariant flow-matching action head: geometric symmetry improves pose generalization and data efficiency, but geometry is applied _after_ visual encoding, at the action head. DualManip decouples infrequent semantic VLM calls from a continuous geometric adaptation path that tracks template-to-observation correspondence — geometry handles most frames without re-running the VLM; the semantic path triggers only when task intent changes. Both say the same thing from different angles: structural geometry can substitute for repeated full-model computation.

SURF occupies a third position: structural geometry at the _input_ stage, before the LLM trunk, to reduce what the LLM processes per frame. SAVLA adds geometry after encoding; DualManip reduces VLM call frequency per scene change; SURF reduces LLM input cost per call. These are complementary rather than competing — but the positioning note needs to articulate this explicitly.

InternW0-Δ is the capability threat: a large world action model that integrates 4D geometric priors, scene semantics, visual dynamics, and action generation in a single pretrained model. If WAMs at scale already absorb the structural priors SURF proposes as a separate module, the modular argument needs an explicit answer: interpretability, budget controllability, OOD generalization, or deployment on hardware where WAM-scale inference is infeasible.

**core**

- [SAVLA: Symmetry-Aware Vision-Language-Action Models for Robotic Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d1f93e6c5590ad9a52d8908f356fd1f264dcb0f5) — Li, Li et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-15
  Frozen VL backbone + equivariant flow-matching action head; uses scene symmetries for pose generalization and data efficiency beyond what demonstrations provide. Geometric prior applied after visual encoding — SURF applies it before, at token selection. The two-stage pattern (token selection → equivariant action head) is composable. Check: does SAVLA's equivariant geometry capture manipulation-relevant spatial structure that SURF's line-drawing prior also selects, and does the data-efficiency gain transfer to token-budget-constrained settings?

- [DualManip: Agentic Dynamic Manipulation via Dual-Path Semantic Reasoning and Geometric Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31112v1) — Li, Di et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-25
  Decouples infrequent semantic VLM calls (task decomposition + object grounding) from a continuous geometric adaptation path (template correspondence, no VLM re-run). Many scene changes alter geometry without invalidating semantic intent — geometric path handles these without calling the VLM. Directly maps to SURF's language/structure split: structural geometry handles most per-frame updates; instruction conditioning is needed selectively. SURF's version: structurally prune tokens at most frames; apply language-conditioned scoring when instruction-relevant content changes. Check: under what conditions does the semantic trigger fire, and is this predictable from visual or task features that SURF could detect?

**adjacent**

- [InternW0-Δ: A World Action Model Bridging Predictive Dynamics and Actions with 20K+ Hours of Open Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31394v1) — Miao, Li et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-25
  Unified WAM pretrained on a heterogeneous corpus integrating visual dynamics, scene semantics, 4D geometric and motion priors, and action generation. Scales structural geometry integration into a single pretrained model. Capability threat for SURF's structural prior module: if WAMs absorb geometric priors implicitly via scale, what does explicit modular token selection add? The answer SURF needs: modularity gives interpretable budget control and OOD generalization that scale cannot guarantee. Read to extract: how is the 4D geometric prior operationalized, and which benchmarks are used for evaluation?

- [VisForce: Visual Grounding of Current and Desired Forces for Goal-Conditioned Dexterous Manipulation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1144dfe32846e350419b3c177e56d82121e6b29e) — Lee, Lim · arXiv · 2026-09-22
  Renders current and desired force cues at fingertip contact locations on wrist images for VLA conditioning; spatially grounds forces at the image regions where contact occurs. Provides an operational link between contact regions and specific image patches. Relevant to the WorldContact question (09-27): if task-critical force events are spatially localized at fingertips, does SURF's line-drawing structural prior score these fingertip-adjacent patches? VisForce's fingertip-to-image mapping is a candidate ground truth for measuring structural prior–contact region overlap on dexterous manipulation tasks.

- [OpenVAM: Open-World Visual Attention Modeling with VLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31364v1) — Hooshanfar, Kazerouni et al. · arXiv · 2026-09-25
  VLM-based human gaze prediction with semantic grounding: outputs object-level attention peaks with context explanation (what and why), not just a dense saliency map. Relevant to [[eval/gaze]] and SURF's oracle map: VLM-predicted gaze with semantic grounding is a stronger oracle than saliency-based gaze if it tracks task-relevant objects rather than visual salience. Check: does VLM-predicted gaze correlate with action-relevant patches in manipulation tasks, or does it track generic visual salience independent of manipulation context?

**horizon**

- [Programs-of-Layers in LLMs through the Lens of Cortical Areas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31360v1) — Westerhoff, Olbrich · arXiv · 2026-09-25
  Analyzes and extends PoLar (Li et al. 2026), which routes each input through a dynamic subset of LLM layers (layer-as-function-library), through the lens of thalamo-cortical routing. Dynamic layer selection outperforms fixed-depth forward passes. Horizon read for [[claims/iterative selection]] and adaptive compute: if LLMs benefit from adaptive layer depth per input, adaptive visual token selection at the input stage may compose with adaptive layer routing inside the LLM to form a dual-level adaptive compute architecture — one reducing input width, the other reducing processing depth.

- [Learning to Stop without Learning to Stop: Self-Supervised Confidence Training Improves Reasoning Efficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31619v1) — Hosseini, Tigalappanavara · arXiv · 2026-09-25
  Self-supervised fine-tuning to predict confidence at intermediate points along a reasoning trace; efficiency gains from confidence-based stopping without length penalties or explicit early-stopping rules. Horizon read for SURF's when-to-stop question in iterative selection: the confidence-based stopping mechanism is a model of when accumulated evidence is sufficient, without requiring task-specific reward supervision. For SURF, the analogous question: when does accumulated structural evidence suffice to fix the token selection, and can a self-supervised confidence signal make this decision?
